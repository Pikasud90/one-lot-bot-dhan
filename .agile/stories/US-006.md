# US-006 — Supervised service with crash recovery

## Story
As the owner, I want the desk to start at login, restart after a crash and resume exactly where it was, so that a reboot or crash never silently stops alerts.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given the install script, when run, then a launchd agent starts the app at login, restarts it within 10 seconds of an unclean exit and writes logs to a rotated file; an uninstall script removes it completely.
- AC2: Given kill -9 of the process while paper trades and manual positions are open, when it restarts, then both are reloaded intact and no alert, paper trade or Telegram message is duplicated (integration test).
- AC3: Given SIGTERM, then the process stops within 5 seconds, flushes the Telegram outbox state and releases the single-instance lock.
- AC4: Given the Mac wakes from sleep after the market opened, then the desk detects the gap, backfills missed 1-minute bars and marks alerts evaluated on stale data as delayed.
