# US-030 — Paper trades visible on the desk (thin slice of US-016)

## Story
As the owner testing the paper book, I want to see every paper trade and its outcome on the desk, so that I can judge the alerts without reading the database.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given paper trades exist, when GET /api/paper is called, then it returns status counts (opened, filled, no_fill, closed, hook_errors) and the most recent 200 trades newest first with alert id, instrument, side, strategy, grade, status, close_reason, entry, exit (rupees as strings from integer paise), net and timestamps; read-only, same auth as other GET routes.
- AC2: Given the Desk page, then a Paper panel lists open paper trades and today's closed ones with status chips (pending, open, no fill, closed), entry/exit/net in rupees, grade (live or delayed) and close reason; it refreshes with the desk snapshot; Day theme default, large type, CSS variables only.
- AC3: Given no paper trades, then the panel says so; given paper.enabled false, then it says paper trading is off.
