# US-114: Three small page defects: gap outcome names, News times in UTC, Volume axis text

## Story
As the owner, I want outcome names, news times and the Volume chart's axis to read right, so that
every word and time on the pages is plain and in India's time.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given an audited outcome GAP_THROUGH_STOP or GAP_THROUGH_TARGET, when the alert card or the
  archive row shows it, then the text reads "GAP THROUGH STOP" or "GAP THROUGH TARGET" (every
  underscore replaced), and the CSS class keeps the raw name.
- AC2: Given a news item or a sentiment score whose ts ends in +00:00, when the News tab shows its
  time, then it reads in IST (09:15 UTC shows as 14:45) and the column or line says "IST".
- AC3: Given the Volume chart, then its axis and crosshair text are at least 14px (the chart option
  fontSize), and the pages' existing test that no text is under 14px also covers chart options.
- AC4: Given every other time on the pages (alerts, paper, wide list), then it does not change.

## Non-functional requirements
- No change to any API body: the fix is in the page; the stored times stay UTC.
- No colour literal outside the theme blocks.

## Public contract
- `outcomeCell` in `src/onelot/ui/app.js` line 346: `o.outcome.replace("_", " ")` replaces the FIRST
  underscore only (lines 351, 681, 1048 and 1049 do the same for sides, which have one underscore
  and are fine). Use `/_/g`.
- `tsf` (line 18) cuts the ISO text, so a UTC stamp shows as UTC; used by `loadNews` (lines 1527 to
  1543) for `n.ts`, `x.ts` and `r.ts`. `GET /api/news` and `/api/sentiment` return `_iso(ts)` as
  stored (UTC).
- `src/onelot/ui/volume.js` line 240: `fontSize: 13` in the chart layout (charts.js line 13 has 14).
