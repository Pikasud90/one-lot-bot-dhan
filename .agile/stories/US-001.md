# US-001 — Every alert opens a paper trade

## Story
As the owner, I want every alert the desk raises to automatically open a paper trade, so that strategy evidence accumulates without me clicking anything and without any real order.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given an alert of any status and strategy (stock or index option), when PaperBook.on_alert(alert, now) runs, then exactly one paper_trades row exists for that alert id with status PENDING_FILL, recording alert id, instrument, side, strategy, timeframe, confidence score, data source and grade (live iff data.source starts with dhan, otherwise delayed); a second call with the same alert id returns the existing trade and creates no row; mutating the alert dict after the call does not change the stored row.
- AC2: Given a PENDING_FILL trade, when step(now, bars) runs, then it fills at the OPEN of the first closed bar of the desk fine resolution with open later than created_at, open plus resolution not later than now, and open not later than expires_at (plus feed_delay_minutes for delayed grade); worked example: created 10:05:03, bars opening 10:05 and 10:06, step at 10:08 fills at the 10:06 open; stock long open 1000.00 with slippage_pct 0.03 percent gives 1000.30 snapped UP to the 0.05 tick, entry_paise 100030; a short gives 99970 snapped DOWN; index options mirror lab.execution.execute_trade (round_to_tick up of fair*(1+option_spread_pct)+option_slippage_ticks*tick, IV frozen at the alert) to zero paise with a fake pricer.
- AC3: Given the fill bar opens beyond the stop, then the trade closes NO_FILL with reason GAP_THROUGH_STOP; given no eligible bar exists and the data has advanced to a bar opening at or after the deadline (expires_at, plus the feed delay for delayed grade) or the session has closed, then it closes NO_FILL; NO_FILL rows have NULL entry and exit, zero costs and zero net and are excluded from counts and win rate; an alert at 15:29 on a Friday is NO_FILL, never a Monday fill; no price is ever invented while waiting.
- AC4: Given a risk-blocked, low-confidence or already-expired alert, then a paper trade is still opened, and governor.snapshot().as_dict() is identical before and after, including after a paper loss larger than daily_loss_limit_inr closes.
- AC5: Given an index alert the unit is exactly one lot of the alerted option and given a stock alert exactly 1 share, even when risk.lots_per_trade is 5 or capital is huge.
- AC6: Given a restart, then non-closed paper trades reload and keep being managed with no duplicates, every fill or close row commits together with its paper_events row in one transaction (an injected failure leaves no half-state), and alerts present in desk_signals without a paper row are reconciled at startup.
- AC7: Given paper.enabled is false, then on_alert returns None and writes nothing, while step still manages trades that are already open.
- AC8: Given a Desk with a PaperBook attached, then on_alert is called exactly once per alert AFTER the desk_signals insert and BEFORE notifier.send, step is called every poll after _manage_positions inside a try/except that counts hook_errors, and a PaperBook exception never prevents the alert or its notification.
- AC9: Given hostile data (None or empty DataFrame as left by Desk._reset_bars, NaN open, duplicate or unsorted bars, 1m and 2m spacing mixed in one frame, tz-naive timestamps), then no exception escapes step, mixed spacing grades the trade delayed, numpy integers are stored as SQL integers (typeof entry_paise is integer), and the same alert id from two threads yields one row without a leaked IntegrityError.

## Non-functional requirements
**2026-11-01T13:57+05:30**
- Integer paise columns (BIGINT): entry/exit/gross/costs/net/risk_paise; Decimal only at the cost-model boundary; no float sums. Rounding rules: ADR-001 amendment 5.
- All timestamps tz-aware IST in logic, stored as UTC like the rest of the schema; naive input is rejected, not guessed.
- Idempotent by alert id (UNIQUE); the paper trade is persisted BEFORE any notification about the alert.
- No look-ahead: fills use only bars strictly after created_at. TTL and NO_FILL are judged on DATA time, not wall time (ADR-001 amendment 3).
- Hermetic: dependencies injected (cost_for, premium_at, BacktestCfg, specs, clock); never current_settings(); costs.yaml is parsed once, not per call; no network; no order-verb identifiers (working agreements).
- Performance: bars() called once per series per step; per-trade cursor with searchsorted; 45 trades on 30,000-row frames in under 200 ms; on_alert under 50 ms. (Proxy assertions, not wall-clock flaky ones.)
- Units trap: slippage_pct is a PERCENT, option_spread_pct is a FRACTION — tests must pin both.
- Config: add PaperCfg (enabled, feed_delay_minutes=15) and its YAML key in the model BEFORE the YAML (Strict config forbids unknown keys).
