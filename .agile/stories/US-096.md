# US-096: the app's IST time zone as a fixed offset (a 400-fold stall removed)

Found by the Simulator audit of 25 Nov 2026 (`.agile/stories/SIM-AUDIT-2026-11-25.md`, finding
F1): `src/onelot/clock.py` sets `IST = ZoneInfo("Asia/Kolkata")`. pandas has no fast path for
`zoneinfo` zones: every `normalize`, `tz_localize`, `tz_convert`, `.date`, `.time`, `.hour` on
an IST index calls Python once per element WITHOUT releasing the interpreter lock. Measured on
36,000 one-minute stamps: `normalize()` 2.35 s with ZoneInfo, 0.006 s with pytz's
"Asia/Kolkata", 0.001 s with a fixed +05:30 offset; a thread ticking every 10 ms froze for
2.1 s meanwhile. The desk's poll, the feed, the API and every backtest share that lock, so
this is the "whole app lags" the owner reported, and it slows every study and the test run.
India has had no daylight saving time since 1945, so a fixed offset is exact for all data
the app holds (2010 onward).

## What to change
1. `onelot.clock.IST` becomes `datetime.timezone(datetime.timedelta(hours=5, minutes=30),
   "IST")` (the name keeps `%Z` printing "IST"; `isoformat()` keeps "+05:30").
2. ONE definition: every `ZoneInfo("Asia/Kolkata")` and every string "Asia/Kolkata" or
   "Asia/Calcutta" handed to pandas or datetime under `src/onelot/` and `scripts/` is
   replaced by the one `IST` (import it; do not define a second). Where a library hands back
   its own zone object (Yahoo bars, Dhan helpers), convert to `IST` at the point of entry.
   Search also for `tz="Asia/...`, `tz_localize(`, `tz_convert(`, `ZoneInfo(`, `pytz`,
   `.tz ==`, `str(` of a zone, `tzname`, `utcoffset`, `%Z`, `zone` keys in configs and tests.
3. Anything that compares zones by identity or by name (`is IST`, `== ZoneInfo(...)`,
   `str(index.tz) == "Asia/Kolkata"`, a test asserting `tzinfo.key`) is changed to compare
   offsets or to use `IST` itself. Mixed zones in one frame or concat (an old cached frame
   with a ZoneInfo zone joined to a new one) must not silently turn into UTC or object dtype:
   where frames are read from a cache or pickle, convert to `IST` on load.
4. Nothing else changes: no behaviour, no stored format (the store keeps naive UTC), no JSON.
