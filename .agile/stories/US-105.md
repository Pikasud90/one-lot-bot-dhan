# US-105: a paper trade on a contract that was not tradeable is marked and left out of the score

## The finding (25 Nov 2026)
Three of the last five index option alerts named a contract nobody trades (open interest 0),
because the pick looked only at delta (story US-104 fixes the pick and is separate):

- NIFTY, id f6a7b9, 25 Nov 13:14, BUY_PE 29200 PE 29 Dec: bid and ask 4962.0, oi 0.0, delta
  0.646, `economics.synthetic` false. Its paper trade closed at -9,027.36.
- FINNIFTY, id 950611, 25 Nov 13:14, BUY_PE 32600 PE: bid and ask 5696.6, oi 0.0. Paper trade
  -9,614.88.
- MIDCPNIFTY, id 551e7c, 23 Nov 13:15, BUY_PE 15800 PE: bid 1313.45, ask 1678.5, oi 0.0. Paper
  trade +256.19.

The two sound alerts: BANKNIFTY 65700 PE (oi 1050.0) and SENSEX 83400 CE (oi 540.0).

## The owner's decision (25 Nov 2026)
"yes exclude those three trades". Such trades are marked "contract not tradeable" and left out
of the score. The owner's standing rule stays: alert prices are the record, so nothing is
deleted or rewritten.

## Rules
- V1 `onelot.void.void_reason(alert)`: an INDEX alert whose `economics.synthetic` is exactly
  False and whose `economics.option.oi` is a number equal to 0 gives "contract not tradeable:
  no open interest when the alert was raised". Everything else gives None (oi missing or None,
  oi above 0, a model estimate, a stock alert, a malformed payload; never an exception).
- V2 Table `paper_void` (trade_id primary key, alert_id, reason, marked_at). Written in the
  same transaction as the trade when it opens, and by an idempotent pass at every start over
  the stored trades. A mark is never removed by the code.
- V3 Left out of every score, never silently: the paper ledger (totals, by strategy, by slot),
  the day and last-session totals, the audit scoreboard, and the Home and Live Desk lines.
  Each says how many it left out (`left_out`: trades, net, reason). Absent when none.
  A void NO_FILL has no money, so it stays in its slot's "No fill" count and no score changes
  by it. Only a close with a known net is left out and counted in `left_out`; the day line and
  the ledger use that one rule. A slot with only void trades gets no row of its own; its
  trades are in the top-level figure. The snapshot (GET /api/desk and its stream) carries
  `scoreboard_left_out` beside `scoreboard`. A day whose only closes are void is shown as that
  day, with its left-out line, never as "nothing closed today yet".
- V4 The alert list and the paper trade list still show the alert, its prices and its exit as
  recorded, with the tag "not counted: the contract had no open interest".
- V5 Nothing else changes: prices, exits, alert text, phone messages, delivery and wide-list
  books, the Simulator, the management of open trades (a void open trade exits as usual).
- Decision: the confidence score of a new alert keeps reading the audit record with the void
  alerts included (`strategy_scoreboard(counted_only=False)`), so no alert text changes.
  Tell the owner; they may choose to drop them from it later.
- Left alone, by design: the fill-cost report (it measures quotes), the owner's own journal
  tiles (their taken trades), the process counters of the paper book, the study scripts.

## Review round (25 Nov 2026)
Three independent reviews found: the page fell back to the last session on a day of void closes
only (fixed in `paperFallback`); a void NO_FILL left the "No fill" column silently (it stays
in it now); the day line and the ledger disagreed on a void close with an unknown net (one
rule now); the snapshot carried the filtered scoreboard without the left-out figure (added);
the confidence record, the audit left-out figure on the real Desk, the "open" column, the
idempotence guard, both tags and `oi` false had no test (added). The start pass now reads only
INDEX trades, and the ledger checks its table once per engine. Left as it was: the join on
`trade_id` alone (nothing deletes trades; a reused id needs a hand edit) and the narrow-screen
look of the tag.
