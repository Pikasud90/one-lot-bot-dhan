## 1. The workspace (layout)
The tab fills the window under the app's header; the page itself does not scroll on a
desktop. Three rows in a CSS grid (`auto 1fr auto`, `min-height: 0` on the middle):
- TOP STRIP, one row at 1440 px (it may wrap to two rows under 1200 px), about 56 px a row:
  instrument, strategy, strategy timeframe, date with previous day, next day and random day,
  Past or Live; then play or pause, step, next alert, the speed chips, the clock; then the
  chart timeframe chips, fit, settings, help ("?") and full screen.
- MIDDLE: the chart takes all the width left of a dock of about 340 px. The chart box is
  `position: relative` and carries, over the chart: the legend (top left), the alert banner
  (top centre), the loading veil, the "to now" button, pinned tags for levels that are off
  the scale. The DOCK is always there (no layout jump): the ticket when an alert is live, the
  position while a trade is open, else the last result and a short hint; under it the
  session's numbers.
- BOTTOM PANEL, tabs: Trades, Log, Numbers, How it works. About 200 px high, collapsible to
  one line (key L); collapsed by default when the window is under 800 px high.
Targets, checked by a test of computed sizes in a browser: at 1440x900 the chart is at least
520 px high and chart, strip, dock and the panel's tab row are all visible without scrolling;
at 1024x768 the chart is at least 380 px high (the dock moves under the strip as one row of
two cards, the panel is collapsed); at 390x844 the chart comes first under a two-row strip,
is about half the window high, and Take, Skip and Close sit in a sticky bar at the bottom
with targets of at least 44 px.
Size the workspace from where it starts on the page (a ResizeObserver on the root; the app's
header height changes when it compacts), never from a fixed number.

## 2. The chart (TradingView Lightweight Charts v5.2.1, vendored; one chart, built once)
- One chart object and its series for the life of the page; a switch swaps data. Panes: 0
  price (candles, the strategy's lines, trade lines, zones), 1 volume (a histogram; shown
  only when the instrument has volume), 2 premium (a line of what is held, only while an
  option trade is open or was just closed; may be left out if time is short: say so).
- Price format from the instrument (`tick`, two decimals); `scaleMargins` so candles never
  touch the edges (more room at the bottom when volume shares the pane).
- THE SCALE RULE (owner's first bug, SIM-01): after every switch the price scale is reset to
  automatic and fits the visible candles. The present trade's entry, stop and target widen
  the scale only when they lie within 60% of the visible candles' height beyond their range;
  a level farther away is shown as a tag pinned to the pane's top or bottom edge ("Target
  24,110 ↑"), never by squashing the candles. Nothing of an earlier session survives a
  switch (contract rule 7): write the reset once, in one place, and test it.
- Time axis in IST through the library's own formatters (not by shifting the times). About
  120 candles shown at the start, the newest at the right with a small gap; the user's zoom
  and scroll are kept while playing; `fitContent` is never called behind the user's back; a
  "to now" button appears when the newest candle is out of view; key F resets the view.
- Legend, top left, following the crosshair and falling back to the newest candle:
  "NIFTY 50 · 3m   O 25,010.50  H 25,031.00  L 25,001.20  C 25,022.40  +0.12%  Vol 1.2 L",
  then one coloured entry per strategy line. A small countdown to the candle's close.
- The last price as a line with its tag. Day boundaries visible (the axis' own day marks are
  enough if they read clearly).
- The strategy's lines from `overlays` (contract), shown when the chart timeframe equals the
  strategy timeframe; otherwise one quiet line of text says where they show.
- A trade on the chart: entry, stop and target as price lines that are MOVED (never removed
  and recreated) with labels that say what they are and the rupees ("Stop 24,970 · −₹2,288");
  the first stop and target stay as faint dashed lines once moved; a shaded reward zone
  (entry to target) and risk zone (entry to stop) from the entry's time to the right edge
  (a series primitive); markers for the alert, the entry ("In 413.20"), each stage ("BE",
  "35%", "Trail") and the exit ("Target · +₹6,713 · +2.9R"). Colours only from theme
  variables (read with getComputedStyle; no colour literal in JS or CSS).
- Chart timeframe chips (1m to 60m) re-read the candles for the new timeframe to the cursor
  (`GET /api/sim/candles`) and go on from there; the session, the clock and the trade stay.

## 3. Play (one clock)
- One replay clock. The page asks the server for a CHUNK (`advance` with `until: "event"`)
  and reveals its minutes one by one on `requestAnimationFrame` at the chosen speed (1x is
  real time: one market minute a minute; 2x, 5x, 10x, 60x, 300x). Chunk size: the minutes
  that fit into the next 1 second of play while an alert or a trade is open and into the
  next 3 seconds otherwise, at least 1, at most 30. Ask for the next chunk when less than
  half a second of play is left; never two requests at once; owed time is never dropped.
- Inside a minute the candle may move along a DRAWN path from the bar's own open, high, low
  and close (as today, a setting "Smooth candles", on by default, off = step by minute). It
  never shows a price outside the real bar and ends on the real close. The "How it works"
  tab says so in plain words, and that stops and targets are decided by the server on the
  minute's real high and low, the stop first.
- Events are applied when the clock reaches the chunk's last minute (contract rule 8). An
  alert pauses play when "Pause on an alert" is on (default). After Take or Skip play goes on
  at the chosen speed, but while a trade is open the speed is held to 10x when "Slow down in
  a trade" is on (default); the speed chips show the speed in force. Close and End first draw
  the rest of the chunk (contract rule 9).
- Step (key Right): one minute. Shift+Right: to the close of the chart's candle. "Next alert"
  (key N): `advance` with `until: "alert"` and up to 390 minutes, drawn at once. At a day's
  end a day card shows the day's result; "Go on to the next day" continues (a setting can
  make that automatic).
- Leaving the tab, hiding the window or opening a dialog pauses play. The clock reads
  "Ready: Tue 03 Nov 09:15" before the first minute and the same minute everywhere after.

## 4. Choosing and switching (owner's second bug)
- On opening: the defaults of `options` (NIFTY, micro trend, 3m), a start several trading
  days before the last stored one. The tab is usable at once: draw from `GET
  /api/sim/candles` while `options` and `days` load; never wait for a slow call with a blank
  page.
- Any change of instrument, date or strategy: (1) cancel what is in flight (AbortController
  and a request number: a late answer never overwrites a newer choice); (2) veil the chart
  with what is loading ("Loading RELIANCE · 5m …") and clear the old picture at once; (3)
  draw the new candles from `GET /api/sim/candles`; (4) call `GET /api/sim/prepare`; (5) the
  session itself is created at the first Play, Step or Next alert, and a BUILDING session is
  polled with a visible, honest progress line ("Preparing the strategy: building signals").
- A change in the middle of a session keeps the clock (the new session starts at the old
  cursor plus one minute). With a trade open the page asks first, in plain words, saying
  what will be closed.
- Previous day, next day, random day ("a day I do not know": the date is hidden until the
  day ends, if the user ticks "hide the date"), from `GET /api/sim/days`.
- Resume: the offer to continue the last session is answered from stored facts and restores
  the log and the markers (`events` in the contract).

## 5. The dock and the panel
- TICKET (an alert): the action in large type, what would be traded, for an option the
  PREMIUM plan first (buy at ₹573.85, stop ₹x, target ₹y, risk and reward in rupees, one lot
  of 75) and the index triggers second; minutes left; the reasons folded; Take (T), Skip (S).
- POSITION: what is held, entry to now (premium and index), open result in rupees, % and R,
  a bar from stop to target with the price on it and ticks at 25%, 50% and 80% of the way,
  the stage in plain words, the present stop and target (premium and index), minutes in the
  trade, Close (X).
- RESULT: the reason in plain words, net rupees, R, charges, and what the rule did.
- NUMBERS: trades, winners, net, average win and loss, best, worst, and the equity line.
- TRADES tab: one row a trade with premium in and out as well as the index levels (SIM-09);
  a click scrolls the chart to that trade. LOG tab: every event at its own minute, appended
  (never the whole list rebuilt each minute). HOW IT WORKS tab: a legend and the honest
  limits (one-minute bars, drawn path inside a minute, stop before target, fills at the next
  minute's open with slippage and charges, real premiums where stored, else modelled).
- Panels are updated in place (text and attributes), not rebuilt with innerHTML each minute.

## 6. Keys (only while this tab is shown, never while typing in a field)
Space play or pause; Right step; Shift+Right to the candle's close; N next alert; Up and
Down the next and previous speed; T take; S skip; X close; F fit; L the bottom panel; [ and ]
the chart timeframe; ? the key sheet; Esc closes a sheet or dialog. Number keys stay with the
app's tabs. Every button's tooltip names its key.

## 7. States that must look finished
Loading, empty (no stored bars for this instrument: say what to do), building, error with a
retry (the last picture stays), offline engine (the fake's banner), the market closed in
Live mode, a session that failed to build. No dead control: one that cannot act is disabled
and says why.
