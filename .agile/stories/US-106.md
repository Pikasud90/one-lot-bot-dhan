## First step for whoever resumes: compare the evening quote with the official bars
The quote answer of 25 Nov 19:38 for all 222 universe stocks is saved in the live folder at
`data/research/quote_vs_daily_2026-11-25/quote_1938.json`. Once Dhan's daily history holds
25 Nov (from 26 Nov on), fetch that day's daily bar of each stock through the app's adapter
(one request a stock, at most one a second, after market hours) and compare open, high, low,
close and volume with the saved quote. Expected: equal for the 211 stocks that traded in the
closing session; a small difference in the close for the 11 that did not. Write the result
here and set `REVISE_NOTE` below from it. If the closes differ widely for many stocks, stop
and rethink before building. The same record shows the hour Dhan adds a day:
`wide_rounds.finished_at` of the session 2026-11-25 in the live store.

## Why (facts found on 25 Nov 2026, all measured or from Dhan's own page)
- Dhan's daily history ends at the PREVIOUS trading day. Dhan's support page says the Daily
  Historical Data API "provides data only up to the previous trading day and does not include
  the current day's data". Measured on 25 Nov: the day's bar was not there at 15:42, 18:11,
  19:22 or 20:02.
- The wide list's round (`WideDesk` in `src/onelot/delivery/wide.py`) evaluates a session only
  when some stock has that session's DAILY bar from `/charts/historical` (US-102's check).
  `session_for(now)` is "today after 15:35, else the trading day before". So today a session
  can only be read on the next day before 09:00, and if Dhan adds the day later than 09:00 the
  round never reads at all (from 15:35 it waits for the new day). The owner's paper test of
  the pullback rule on 204 stocks then records nothing.
- Dhan's quote answer DOES hold the day's bar after the close. `POST /marketfeed/quote`
  (`EP_QUOTE` in `src/onelot/broker/dhan.py`; body `{segment: [security ids]}`, up to 1,000
  instruments a request, limiter endpoint "quote") at 19:38 answered all 222 universe stocks.
  Each stock: `last_price`, `ohlc: {open, high, low, close}`, `volume`,
  `last_trade_time: "25/11/2026 15:59:13"` (India time, dd/mm/YYYY HH:MM:SS), `net_change`,
  `average_price`, `depth`, and more. `ohlc.close == last_price` for all 222; 211 had traded at
  or after 15:40, in the closing session (15:40 to 16:00), where every trade is at the
  exchange's closing price. A real sample (shape `{asked_at, session, stocks: {KEY: {last_price,
  open, high, low, close, volume, last_trade_time}}}`) is in the live folder, git-ignored:
  `data/research/quote_vs_daily_2026-11-25/quote_1938.json` (read it for the shape; a
  fixture may copy two or three rows; it is public market data).
- The intraday one-minute answer for the same day ended at 15:14 for 9 of 10 stocks: never
  build the day's close from it.
- NIFTY's own session comes from the desk's live one-minute bars (`DeliveryDesk._wide_nifty`):
  it is there the same evening already. Only the delivery-only stocks need this story.

## What to build
Q1 ADAPTER. `DhanAdapter.day_quotes(contracts) -> dict[str, DayQuote]` in
   `src/onelot/broker/dhan.py`, `DayQuote` a frozen dataclass in `src/onelot/broker/base.py`
   (security_id, open, high, low, close, volume, last_trade: aware India-time datetime or
   None). One request for each chunk of at most 1,000 instruments, through the existing
   `_post(EP_QUOTE, ..., "quote")`. `close` is `last_price` when it is a number above 0, else
   `ohlc.close`. A malformed row is skipped, never an exception; a failed request raises as
   the other reads do. The existing `quote()` is not changed.
Q2 WIRING. `Desk._quote_day_bars(stocks)` beside `Desk._fetch_daily_bars` in
   `src/onelot/desk/desk.py` (a read of quotes through the desk's own adapter, never an
   order), passed as `wide_quote=` through `DeliveryDesk` (`src/onelot/delivery/runner.py`,
   `_build_wide`) to `WideDesk(..., quote=...)`. `quote=None` (the default, the replay tools,
   every existing test): the round behaves exactly as before this story.
Q3 THE ROUND with a quote source, for its session `s`:
   a. One snapshot at the start of each try (one request for each 1,000 stocks, counted in
      `self.requests`). It is taken only from `QUOTE_FROM = 16:05` India time on the day of `s`
      (the closing session ends at 16:00), or at any time on a later calendar day (a round
      before 09:00 the next morning).
   b. A stock's quote is OF `s` when its last trade's date is `s`, open, high, low and close
      are finite and above 0, low <= min(open, close), high >= max(open, close), and volume is
      finite and not negative. Anything else: no quote for that stock on this try.
   c. The session is THERE when a stock has the session's daily bar from the history (as
      today) OR at least half of the list's stocks have a quote of `s`. When it is not there,
      US-102's behaviour stays: not a try, nothing stored, nothing evaluated, a plain note,
      the next look `ROUND_GAP` later. The note says what is true: before 16:05 "the day's
      prices are read from 16:05"; the quote request failed "Dhan did not answer (...)"; fewer
      than half "Dhan's quote does not show the session of <s> yet (N of M stocks)". With the
      quote readable the 5-stock check of US-102 is not needed; without it the check decides
      as before.
   d. When the session is there, each stock not yet fetched for `s`: FIRST the history fetch
      exactly as today (`_fetch_one`: the window, the overlap check, the save, the whole
      refetch on a re-based history). THEN, if the stock still has no bar of `s`, has a quote
      of `s`, and no trading day of the calendar lies strictly between its last stored bar and
      `s` (no hole), the quote is stored as the stock's daily bar of `s` with the source
      "quote" (same timestamp and frame shape as a fetched daily bar): a PROVISIONAL bar. The
      stock then counts as fetched for `s` (`fetched_for = s`) and is evaluated in this try.
      A stock whose history request failed is still given its quote bar when it has no hole
      (the error stays listed). A stock with neither is "waiting", as today.
   e. THE OFFICIAL BAR REPLACES IT. A later history fetch that has the day overwrites the
      provisional bar (the store upserts on the bar's time) with the source "dhan". When any
      of open, high, low, close differs from the provisional one by more than
      `REVISE_NOTE = 0.0005` (0.05%), the round's summary lists it in `revised`
      ("KEY 2026-11-25: close 2517.30 in the evening, 2516.85 official", at most 50) and logs
      it. No trade is changed: a signal, a fill or an exit decided on the evening's reading
      stays as recorded. Say so in the docstring and the guide.
   f. A PROVISIONAL DAY IS NEVER EVIDENCE OF A RE-BASED HISTORY. `_disagrees` skips days whose
      stored bar has the source "quote", wherever they sit (today only the newest shared day
      is excepted). A real re-basing (official days more than `ADJUST_LIMIT` apart) still
      triggers the whole refetch, and no open trade is re-based because of a provisional bar.
      `_replace` (the whole refetch) never deletes a provisional bar that is newer than the
      refetched history's last day.
   g. Counts for the owner: `summary["quoted"]` (stocks read from the quote in this round,
      added up over a resumed round's tries) and `summary["revised"]` (list). Both in
      `_round_view`, in `GET /api/delivery/wide`, and in the page's round line in plain words:
      "N stocks read from Dhan's evening quote; Dhan's official daily bars replace them on the
      next day", and when there are any, "official bars differed from the evening's reading:
      ...". JSON-safe, no text under 14px, no colour literal.
   h. Unchanged: the rule, the tiers, the cap, the alert texts, late signals (a session read
      after a newer one is still recorded and never alerted), NIFTY's wait, the four tries,
      `NO_ROUND`, `session_for`, the dormant logic (it reads stored bars: a provisional bar is
      a bar; a suspended stock has no quote of `s` and stays quiet as designed), the desk 18's
      code and tables, `protected` (a desk instrument is never written).
   i. Requests a try: one quote request for each 1,000 stocks, at most one history request a
      stock (one more for a whole refetch), paced as today.
Q4 WORDS. The module docstring of `wide.py` (step 1 gains the quote), the constants'
   comments, `docs/04_OPERATIONS.md` (replace the paragraph that starts "What Dhan does (found
   on 25 Nov 2026)" with how it works now; keep the fact and the source), `docs/14_LIVE_DESK.md`
   if it describes the evening round, and `.agile/stories/US-106.md` (this contract, your
   decisions, the tests, what is not done).

## Tests (new file `tests/delivery/test_wide_quote.py`; helpers in `tests/delivery/wide_helpers.py`)
Write the tests first and watch them fail. No real clock, no network, no real store.
- T1 the plain evening: the history serves up to `s - 1`, the quote has `s` for every stock,
  the clock is 16:10 on `s`: ONE try, the round finishes, every stock has a bar of `s` with
  the source "quote", a signal of `s` is on time, within the cap and alerted, `quoted` is the
  number of stocks, requests = one quote request + one history request a stock.
- T2 the next evening the history has `s`: equal values replace the bar (source "dhan"),
  nothing in `revised`; a close 0.2% off is replaced and listed in `revised`; a close 0.8%
  off is replaced and listed, is NOT a re-basing (no whole refetch, an open trade's prices
  unchanged).
- T3 a real re-basing (the official days before `s` at half the stored prices) still gives
  the whole refetch, and the trade is put on the new basis as before.
- T4 not there: 15:50 on `s` (before 16:05); the quote request raises; the quote shows the day
  before for all; a quote of `s` for under half of the stocks. Each: not a try, nothing
  stored, nothing evaluated, the right note, the next look 30 minutes later.
- T5 one stock's quote row is bad (each of: a zero, NaN, low above the close, high below the
  open, a missing field, a last trade dated the day before): that stock waits, the others are
  read, the round uses its tries as before and closes after the fourth.
- T6 the hole rule: a stock stored up to `s - 2` whose history request fails gets NO quote
  bar; one stored up to `s - 1` whose history request fails gets it.
- T7 the morning after (08:30 on the next day): the history has `s` for all: no bar from the
  quote, `quoted` 0; the history has it for none and the quote is still of `s`: provisional.
- T8 a paper trade is filled at the next session's open taken from a provisional bar, and
  another exits on a provisional close; the official bars then differ by 0.1%: both trades
  are unchanged and the round lists the bars in `revised`.
- T9 `_replace` keeps a provisional bar newer than the refetched history.
- T10 with `quote=None` the round is byte for byte the old one on the US-102 tests'
  scenarios (run `tests/delivery/test_wide_wait.py` unchanged), and
  `scripts/pullback_wide_check.py` needs no change.
- T11 a desk instrument is never written from a quote.
- T12 the adapter: `day_quotes` on a payload of the real shape (the three fields above, a
  malformed row, an empty segment), 1,001 contracts make two requests.
- T13 the view and the page (node, as `tests/delivery/test_wide_ui.py` does): the two lines.
- T14 the dormant logic with provisional bars: a stock with no quote of the session for
  `dormant_after` sessions still turns dormant by its own rule; a provisional bar starts its
  counts again.
