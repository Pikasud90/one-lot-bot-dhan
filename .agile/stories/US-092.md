# US-092: option setups built on what is known to repeat, and a long-volatility test (study)

Read `.agile/stories/RESEARCH-RULES.md` first. US-091 runs the registered strategies through a
grid; this story tests a short list of NEW setups, each with a reason from the evidence or
the literature, on the four index options (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, SENSEX; each alone and
the pool). Owner, 2026-11-24: "start on both options [the pullback universe and the
long-volatility idea], try finding the alpha now with maximum focus on something that works"
and "analyse it through the correlation lens from its relation with India VIX".

Facts to build on: the SIZE of the next move is predictable (active follows active; India VIX
knows part of it), its direction is not; an option buyer needs about 5 to 10 basis points of
index move a trade to cover costs; real premiums came out about 1 point a trade below the
model. Before writing a setup, check `src/onelot/strategies/` so you do not rebuild one that
exists (`intraday_momentum`, `orb`, `nr7_breakout`, `cpr_breakout` are in US-091's grid).

## The setups, fixed here before any result
Day types used below, all known before 09:15 from the previous session(s): ALL; ACTIVE-EXPECTED
(previous session's size state active, `src/onelot/mllab/states.py`); CALM-EXPECTED; NR7
(previous session's range the narrowest of the last 7); VIX-UP (India VIX rose more than 5%
in the previous session); VIX-DOWN (fell more than 5%); EXPIRY (the traded contract expires
today) and PRE-EXPIRY (tomorrow). Expiry dates come from the real option store or the
harness, never from a weekday assumption (weekly expiries changed several times since 2021).

S1. Last-half-hour momentum (the first half hour's return, measured from the previous close
    to 09:44, predicts the last half hour: Gao, Han, Li and Zhou 2018). At 15:00 buy a call
    when that return is positive, a put when negative; exit at the session's option exit.
    Variants: (a) always; (b) only when the 14:30 to 14:59 return has the same sign; each
    with and without "the first return is larger in size than its median of the previous 60
    sessions". Four cells per index.
S2. Opening range, then one option: range of 09:15 to 09:29 and of 09:15 to 09:44 (two
    lengths). Buy a call at the first one-minute close above the range high, a put below the
    range low, entries from the range's end to 13:00, one trade a day. Exit: the desk's rule
    (ratchet, first stop 1.5 x ATR(14) of 60m bars, target 3 risks, extend on) and the
    session exit. By day type: ALL, ACTIVE-EXPECTED, NR7, and "tight open" (the range is
    narrower than its median of the previous 20 sessions), "gap" (the open is more than 0.5
    sd of daily returns away from the previous close).
S3. Long volatility: buy the at-the-money call AND the at-the-money put of the same strike
    (two lots: this breaks the one-lot rule, so it is a question for the owner, not a
    product setting; say so in the note). Entries at 09:20 and at 09:45; exits (a) 15:20,
    (b) 12:00, (c) stop at -30% of the combined premium, take profit at +60%, else 15:20.
    By day type: ALL, ACTIVE-EXPECTED, CALM-EXPECTED, NR7, VIX-UP, VIX-DOWN, EXPIRY,
    PRE-EXPIRY. Real premiums of both legs are REQUIRED for any claim; report the model
    reading beside them. Also report the plain fact per day type: the mean % result of the
    pair held 09:20 to 15:20 (what the buyer of volatility earned or paid).
S4. Expiry-day afternoon trend: on EXPIRY days only, at 13:30, 14:00 and 14:30: when the index
    is more than 0.25 (and, second variant, 0.5) of its ATR(14, daily, previous session) away
    from today's open, buy the option in that direction; exit 15:15 or at -40% of the
    premium. Real premiums REQUIRED (the model is poor on expiry day).
S5. After a VIX shock: on VIX-UP days buy a call at 09:20, and separately on VIX-DOWN days a
    put at 09:20; exit by the desk's rule and the session exit. Two cells per index. Also the
    plain fact: the index's open-to-close return by decile of the previous day's VIX change.
S6. Volume-confirmed first half hour: at 09:45 buy in the direction of the 09:15 to 09:44
    index return when the first-half-hour volume is high (rupee turnover, volume x close, of
    the 18 desk stocks from 09:15 to 09:44 over its median of the previous 20 sessions above
    1.2) and, separately, when it is low (below 0.8). Exit by the desk's rule. Also the plain
    fact: the rest-of-day range and return by that volume ratio's quintile.
S7. Descriptive only, never selected: results of S1 to S6 by weekday and by days to expiry.

Selection is RESEARCH-RULES section 5 with one cell = index (or pool) x setup variant x day
type. The placebo here: shift each setup's signal dates by a random number of sessions within
the same calendar year (20 runs) and, for S1, S5 and S6, flip the side at random.
For every DISCOVERED cell give the real-premium reading before you say anything else about it.
