# US-031 — Lab option runs do not slip the underlying before pricing the option

## Story
As the owner comparing paper evidence with backtests, I want the Lab to execute a bought option at the option's ask only, as the paper book does, so that Lab R and paper R measure the same thing.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given an option run, when simulate and execute_trade book an entry or exit, then the underlying level used to price the option is the bar open (or the stop/target level), never open*(1+slippage_pct) — option costs come only from option_spread_pct and option_slippage_ticks; linear runs (stocks, futures) keep slippage_pct.
- AC2: Given the same alert, then the Lab entry premium equals the paper book's FILLED entry_paise to the paise (differential test against onelot.paper.fill).
- AC3: Given the change, then the affected leaderboard and sweep results are recomputed or marked stale with the version that produced them.
