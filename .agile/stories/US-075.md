# US-075: the Simulator's engine and API

As the owner I want to trade a strategy's alerts on past data, or on the live feed, with dummy
money and market-accurate fills, so that I can feel a strategy before I trust it.
The page is a separate story (US-076). The JSON between the two is fixed in
`.agile/stories/SIM-API.md`: read it first and serve exactly that.

## What to build
A new package `src/onelot/sim/` (the old `src/onelot/replay/` is an unrelated legacy tool:
leave it alone). Suggested modules: `bars.py` (where minute bars come from), `engine.py` (one
session: cursor, signals, alert, trade, ratchet), `pricing.py` (traded price, costs),
`store.py` (the `sim_*` tables), `manager.py` (sessions by id), `routes.py`
(`register(app, engine, require_token)`).

### Bars
- A small protocol, so the engine never needs a database handle of its own:
  `minute_bars(series_key, start, end) -> DataFrame` (IST index, bar START, cleaned) and
  `daily_bars(series_key) -> DataFrame | None`.
- `StoreBars`: `onelot.data.store.load_bars` + `onelot.data.hygiene.clean_intraday` /
  `consistent_daily` (what the Lab's `DataCache` does: see `src/onelot/lab/runner.py`).
- `DeskBars` for mode `live`: the running desk's tracker bars joined with the store's (see
  how `src/onelot/mllab/service.py` does it: `desk_bars_getter`, `desk_stored_reader`,
  `join_bars`). Only CLOSED one-minute bars are revealed.

### Signals: the Lab's own, with no look-ahead
- Build the strategy frame the way the Lab does (`lab/runner.py: generate_for_instrument`,
  `strategies/base.py: Strategy.generate`, the registry in `strategies/registry.py`), so an
  alert in the Simulator is the bar the Lab would trade. Multi-timeframe strategies need the
  one-minute frame in `ctx.extras["minute_bars"]` (the Lab does this).
- History before the start: enough for the strategy (the micro-trend rule needs about 75
  calendar days of one-minute bars for its 4h trend). Use 120 calendar days.
- For speed the signals of a past session may be computed once on the whole loaded frame,
  PROVIDED a test proves they equal the signals computed on the frame cut at the cursor, at
  20 random cursors, for `microtrend` and for one single-timeframe strategy. If they differ,
  compute at the cursor.
- A signal on a candle becomes an `ALERT` when the cursor passes that candle's close. Signals
  while a trade is open, or while an alert is pending, are dropped (one at a time).

### The plan of a trade (first stop, target, ratchet)
- `src/onelot/manage/ratchet.py` is the rule. Use `plan_from_risk`, `start`, `exit_on_bar`,
  `advance` exactly as its module text shows, on one-minute bars of the MANAGED price.
- First stop: risk = `stop_atr_k` x ATR(14, Wilder) of the managed price on the strategy's
  timeframe, but on 15m when the timeframe is shorter than 15m; read on the last closed bar.
  Target: `target_r` risks away. Defaults `stop_atr_k = 1.5`, `target_r = 3.0`,
  `extend = true` in one module-level `DEFAULTS` dict (the lead will set them from the study
  US-074); a session's `params` may override them.
- `cost` in the plan: the round-trip charges per unit; for an option, per unit of premium
  divided by the contract's delta.

### Traded price and costs ("market accurate")
- Stock: the one-minute bar's open with the Lab's slippage (`slippage_pct`, 0.03%); charges
  from `onelot.costs.CostModel` for the intraday equity product (`config/costs.yaml`), as
  `lab/execution.py: execute_trade` applies them. Quantity 1.
- Index option, one lot: the contract the Lab would choose (`lab/execution.py`:
  `monthly_expiry`, `strike_for_delta`, `OptionPricer`). Premium at a minute: the REAL traded
  premium from the expired-options store when the bar exists (`lab/real_options.py`:
  `RealPricer._find` finds a fixed strike's bar at a minute; open the store read-only and
  never fetch), otherwise the Black-76 model premium. Entry and exit apply the Lab's spread,
  tick rounding and charges. Record `source` (`real` or `model`) for the entry and the exit;
  if they differ, say so in the trade's detail.
- Mode `live`: today's real premium through `Desk.real_premium_at` (see `desk/desk.py`), the
  model as the fallback.
- An index option and an intraday stock trade are closed at the session's exit time
  (`SESSION_END`). Nothing is carried overnight in this version.

### State and storage
- Tables in `src/onelot/store/schema.py` (TZDateTime, JSON as the other tables):
  `sim_sessions(id, created_at, instrument_key, strategy, timeframe, mode, start_ts,
  cursor_ts, status, params JSON)`, `sim_trades(id, session_id, status, ... every field of
  the contract's trade ..., detail JSON)`, `sim_events(id, session_id, seq, ts, kind, text,
  data JSON)`. NEVER write to `paper_trades`, `paper_events`, `desk_signals` or
  `desk_positions`: a test must prove a full simulated trade leaves them empty.
- Live sessions in memory in a manager created once per running desk (see how `server.py`
  keeps `mllab`). A session not in memory is rebuilt from `sim_sessions` at its cursor.
- `src/onelot/api/server.py`: add exactly one import and one call
  `register_sim(app, engine, require_token)` on the line before the comment
  `# ── Machine Learning Lab (read-only statistical forecasts; never an alert) ──`.
  Touch nothing else in that file.

## Acceptance criteria (each needs a test in `tests/sim/`)
1. Given stored one-minute bars and a strategy, when a session starts at an instant, then
   `candles` holds only candles closed at or before the cursor, and `advance(n)` reveals
   exactly the next n one-minute bars of the trading calendar (the next session after 15:29).
2. Given a signal on the candle closing at 10:15, then no event mentions it while the cursor
   is before 10:14, and the `ALERT` appears in the advance that reveals 10:14.
3. Given an alert, when `take` is called, then the trade is `PENDING_FILL` and the next
   advance fills it at that minute's OPEN with slippage; `skip` clears the alert; an alert
   not taken expires after 10 market minutes.
4. Given an open trade, then the stop and target follow the ratchet minute by minute: the
   events BREAKEVEN, LOCKED, TRAILING, STOP_MOVED, TARGET_MOVED come in order with the levels
   `ratchet.advance` gives; a level raised by a minute is used from the next minute.
5. Given a minute that touches the stop, the trade closes at the stop (the open on a gap),
   reason by stage; a touch of both stop and target is a stop.
6. Given a strategy exit signal, with `auto_exit` true the trade closes at the next minute's
   open (`SIGNAL_EXIT`); with false only `EXIT_SIGNAL` is raised and `close` works.
7. Given the session's exit time, an open trade closes (`SESSION_END`); `day_end` is true
   after the last minute of a day and the next advance starts the next trading day.
8. Money: gross, charges, net and `r_multiple` equal a hand calculation for a stock trade and
   for an option trade (model premium), to the paisa.
9. Option premium: with a real bar in a (temporary) option store the fill uses it and says
   `real`; without one it uses the model and says `model`.
10. Persistence: after the manager is thrown away, `GET` rebuilds the session at its cursor
    with its trades; `GET /api/sim/trades` and `.csv` list closed trades of all sessions.
11. Live: with a fake desk whose tracker gains one-minute bars, `advance` returns only bars
    that have arrived, an alert fires on a closed candle, and a taken trade fills on the
    next arriving minute.
12. Errors: unknown instrument, strategy or timeframe, a strategy not suited to the
    instrument kind, a start outside the stored data -> 400 with a plain sentence; POST
    without the token -> 401; `take` with no alert -> 409.
13. The paper test is untouched (criterion in "State and storage").
14. `GET /api/sim/options` lists the desk's instruments with their stored range, marks
    MIDCPNIFTY `trade: false`, and lists every registered strategy with the kinds it suits
    (`strategies/registry.py: SUITABLE`).

## Non-functional
- An advance of one minute answers in under 50 ms on a warm session; creating a session on
  120 days of bars in under 5 s. Strict JSON (no NaN). Typed code; no new dependency.
- The system never places orders: no identifier from `tests/test_invariants.py`
  (`square_off`, `exit_position`, `place_order` and the like; use `close_trade`).
