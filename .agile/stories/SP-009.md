# SP-009 — Study: the spread table by index and time of day, and the option rows it changes

## Story
As the owner, I want a measured half spread for each index and time of day applied to the option
rows of the setup book, so that cheap indices are not charged too much and dear ones are not charged
too little.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given the recorded quotes of the fill recorder, when the table is built, then it shows per
  index and per half hour the median, 75th and 90th percentile half spread and the sample count, and
  marks any cell under 100 samples as thin.
- AC2: Given the table, when the option rows are re-costed, then each row prints its model result,
  its result on the table and the change in points of premium.
- AC3: Given the re-costed rows, when a row changes status by the rules of the research file, then
  the report names the row, the old and the new status and the number that changed it.
- AC4: Given fewer than 40 sessions of quotes, when the study is started, then it writes WATCH with
  the count of sessions and does not re-cost any row.

## Non-functional requirements
- Reproducible: one script, fixed percentile method (linear interpolation, written in the
  note), same quotes in, same table out.
- Reads `fill_quotes` only (`mode=ro`), and the result files of earlier studies; never the
  private tables; no request to Dhan, none to the live desk, no order.
- Plain words, no em or en dashes, prose lines at most 100 characters, note at most 180 lines.

## Public contract
- Script `scripts/spread_table.py` with stages `table` and `recost`.
- `table`: from `fill_quotes` rows of kind SAMPLE (`src/onelot/desk/fillcost.py`): half spread
  % = (ask − bid) / (ask + bid) x 100, for the chosen 0.65-delta monthly CE and PE of each
  index; rows with bid or ask not above 0 are dropped and counted. Output
  `data/research/spread_table_<DATE>/table.csv` with columns `index`, `half_hour`, `n`,
  `median`, `p75`, `p90`, `thin`.
- `recost`: for each option row of `docs/SETUP_BOOK.md` section 4 and the WATCH cells, the
  result with the model's 0.6% flat, and with the table's median at its entry and exit half
  hours; output `docs/research/SPREAD_TABLE_<DATE>.md`.

## Notes & assumptions

Where the need was found: `docs/SETUP_BOOK.md` section 2 ("the desk's recorded quotes show a
median half spread of about 0.25% to 0.3% on NIFTY and BANKNIFTY and about 1.5% on FINNIFTY and
SENSEX, far wider before 10:00. The model assumes 0.6% for all four") and
`docs/research/LIVE_ALERTS_2026-11-24.md` (the proposal of a per-index cost). Every option
result in the 3,948 grid cells and the register used 0.6% flat. SP-003 measures the spread of
its four cells only; this study builds the table every study shares.

What exists: the recorder (`fillcost.py`, table `fill_quotes`, kinds SAMPLE, ALERT, FOLLOW),
sampling since 7 Nov 2026 with the desk down on many days; US-097 adds the spread to each alert.

Pre-registration (fixed before any result; RESEARCH-RULES.md section 3):
- Question: with the half spread measured per index and half hour instead of 0.6% flat, which
  option rows of the book change status, and by how much?
- Data: `fill_quotes` of the live store, SAMPLE rows from 2026-11-07 to the start date, four
  indices (MIDCPNIFTY apart). Start only when the table holds at least 40 observed sessions
  (`desk_uptime` covers 90% or more); until then the answer is WATCH with the count.
- Cells: 4 indices x 13 half hours (09:15 to 15:30) = 52 table cells; cells under 100 samples
  are marked thin and use the index's all-day median. The re-costed rows: every option row of
  the register with a stated model result, about 30. Examined: those rows.
- Split: no new selection. The rows keep their own TUNE / CHOOSE / TEST; the study changes only
  the cost term of each period, the same on all three. TEST is not re-read for any choice.
- Costs: half spread per side from the table at the entry and exit half hours; the rest as the
  row's own study (model result less the model's 0.6% a side, plus the table's).
- Yardstick: a row CHANGES STATUS when, re-costed, rule 5's thresholds are crossed in either
  direction (DISCOVERED at +1.0% and t 2.5 in TUNE; AVOID at t −2.5). The table's cost is
  treated as known, not fitted: no free parameter is tuned on the rows.
- Luck check: the 52 cells are descriptive. For the rows, a bootstrap of the quote sample
  (200 resamples by session) gives an interval for each row's re-costed mean; a status change
  inside the interval of the old status is called "not shown".
- Verdict words: WATCH (fewer than 40 sessions, or changes not shown), NO EDGE (no row
  becomes DISCOVERED after re-costing), PASS and AVOID are not used: this study does not
  find setups, it prices them. It also states, per index, whether the 0.6% flat was too low
  (FINNIFTY, SENSEX) or too high (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY), with the number.
- Stops early: fewer than 40 observed sessions; or fewer than 100 samples in more than half of
  an index's half hours (then that index keeps its row on the all-day median).
- No look-ahead: costs are measured on quotes after 2026-11-07 and applied to history as a
  level, never fitted to a result; the note says that spreads of 2022 may have differed.

Not in scope: changing `config/costs.yaml` or the product's cost model; any new setup.
