# US-091: the setup grid, instrument by instrument (study)

Read `.agile/stories/RESEARCH-RULES.md` first: it holds the owner's request, the periods, the
costs, the selection rule and the writing rules. This story adds only what is special here.

Goal: for each of the 22 instruments (index options on NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, SENSEX;
the 18 desk stocks) find which registered strategy, at which timeframe and with which preset,
earned after costs, in which market condition and in which periods. The owner's order of
work: first fix the timeframe per instrument, then the setup, then the conditions.

## Stage 0: mine what was already run (no new backtest)
Inventory per instrument, as far as the data allows:
- the store's `backtest_runs` and `backtest_trades` (Lab runs and sweeps), read-only;
- `data/research/cross_retune_2026-11-07`, `cross_retune_v2_options`, `cross_retune_v2_stocks`,
  `mtf_2026-11-11`, `alpha_2026-11-12`, `microtrend_2026-11-13`, `ratchet_2026-11-23`,
  `regime_2026-11-23` (each has a note in `docs/research/`);
- `docs/research/STRATEGIES_TESTED_2026-11-12.md`.
Deliver per instrument: strategy x timeframe with trades and % a trade by calendar year (2022
to 2026), best and worst year, and by condition where `regime_2026-11-23` has it. Say which
runs are comparable (same costs, same exit) and which are not. Everything found here is
IN-SAMPLE: stage 0 only proposes, stage 1 tests. Keep this part to one page of the note plus
a CSV per instrument in your output folder.

## Stage 1: the fresh grid (fixed here, before any result)
- Strategies: every strategy of `src/onelot/strategies/registry.py: REGISTRY` that suits the
  instrument's kind (`SUITABLE`: "OPTIONS" for the four indices, "STOCK" for the stocks).
- Signals the way the Lab prepares them (`src/onelot/lab/runner.py: generate_for_instrument`).
  US-086 fixed `_session_params`, so a strategy's own entry window now holds (before the fix
  gap_fade fired once in five years: it gets its first real test here).
- Exit, one setting for everything: the desk's rule (`src/onelot/manage/ratchet.py`; first
  stop 1.5 x ATR(14, Wilder) of 60m bars of the managed price, target 3 risks, extend on),
  the strategy's own exit signal kept, the session exit as the harness has it. Reuse the exit
  engine of `scripts/ratchet_study.py` and the structure of `scripts/regime_study.py`.
- Step A, the timeframe: DEFAULT parameters at 5m, 15m, 30m and 60m for every bar-based
  strategy; a strategy with a fixed timeframe of its own (microtrend at 3m, daily-data ones)
  only at its own. Per instrument and strategy, FIX the timeframe with the best TUNE mean
  (at least 60 TUNE trades). Report the whole table, not only the winner.
- Step B, the preset: at the fixed timeframe, DEFAULT plus up to three presets per strategy
  taken from the Lab's own sweep or preset definitions (find them: `onelot sweep`, the Lab's
  preset code, table `param_sets`); a strategy without presets stays at DEFAULT. Write the
  preset list into the note before running. Per instrument and strategy, FIX the preset with
  the best TUNE mean.
- Step C, the conditions, on the fixed timeframe and preset (each known before the entry):
  C0 all trades;
  C1 trend of the instrument on daily bars to the previous session: ADX(14, Wilder) and close
     against SMA(20): UP, DOWN, CHOPPY, NEUTRAL exactly as in REGIME_STUDY_2026-11-23;
  C2 India VIX level: previous close above or below its 250-session median;
  C3 India VIX change of the previous session: up more than 5%, down more than 5%, between;
  C4 size state of the previous session: active or calm as `src/onelot/mllab/states.py` has it;
  C5 today's opening gap against the previous close, in units of the sd of the previous 20
     daily returns: below -0.5, -0.5 to +0.5, above +0.5;
  C6 time of entry: 09:15 to 10:14, 10:15 to 12:59, 13:00 to 14:59, later;
  C7 (index options) days from the entry to the traded contract's expiry: 0, 1, 2 or more;
     expiries from the harness or the real option store, never from a weekday assumption;
  C8 side: long or short signal;
  C9 (entries after 09:45 only) first-half-hour volume: rupee turnover (volume x close) of the
     18 desk stocks from 09:15 to 09:44 over its median of the previous 20 sessions: above
     1.2, below 0.8, between.
- Pools: besides each instrument alone, the pools "four indices" and "18 stocks" at the
  pool's best common timeframe (one draw per day).
- Every cell tried in steps A, B and C counts as examined (RESEARCH-RULES section 5). The
  timeframe and preset are chosen on TUNE only; CHOOSE and TEST are read once, at the end.
- Workers: at most 3 processes. Cache signals and trades per (instrument, strategy,
  timeframe, preset) on disk. If the full grid cannot finish in about five hours of wall
  time, run step A for everything first, then B and C for the best half by TUNE, and say so.
