# US-079: the stop/target/trail rule in the live desk, the paper book and the Lab

Owner, 2026-11-23: "for every trade we trade we have a very strict stoploss, target and trail
mechanism ... map it properly into our all strategies we run and test."
The rule exists as pure functions (`src/onelot/manage/ratchet.py`; read its module text) and
the Simulator already uses it (`src/onelot/sim/engine.py`: read how it builds a plan and
steps it minute by minute, and reuse rather than copy where you can by moving shared helpers
into `src/onelot/manage/`). This story puts the same rule under the LIVE desk: the alert, the
paper trade that opens on every alert, the manual position the owner opens with TAKE, and
the Backtest Lab's simulator. The settings come from `docs/research/RATCHET_STUDY_2026-11-23.md`:
first stop 1.5 x ATR(14, Wilder) of 60m bars, target 3 risks away, target extension on, the
strategy's own exit and the session exit kept.

Scope: intraday slots (today that is `microtrend` on the index options). The delivery path
(`src/onelot/delivery/`, the pullback rule) is NOT touched: the study found the rule makes it
worse and the owner has not decided.

## What exists today (read before designing)
- A verdict becomes an alert in `src/onelot/desk/desk.py: Desk._raise_signal`; the levels are
  computed in `src/onelot/desk/economics.py` (`underlying_entry/stop/target` for the index,
  `entry_price/stop_price/target_price` for the premium); `sig["manage"]` freezes the
  management settings; the paper trade opens in `src/onelot/paper/book.py: PaperBook.on_alert`.
- Each poll `Desk._step_paper` -> `PaperBook.step` -> `_step_open` -> the pure
  `src/onelot/paper/manage.py: manage_step / _manage` (`_close_on_bar`: gap first, then the
  stop, the stop beats the target in one bar; `_trailed_stop`; `_line_stop`; the strategy
  exit; the session end). Close reasons are `CloseReason` in that file; events go to
  `paper_events` (`STOP_MOVED` exists).
- The manual position after TAKE lives in `desk_positions` and is managed by
  `Desk._manage_positions`.
- The Lab: `src/onelot/lab/simulator.py: simulate` (ATR trail and line trail updated at a bar
  close and used from the next bar; `PathResolver` on one-minute bars).
- Shared parameters: `src/onelot/strategies/base.py: StrategyParams`.

## What to build
1. Parameters on `StrategyParams`, off by default so nothing changes unless a slot asks:
   `ratchet: bool = False`, `ratchet_stop_atr: float = 1.5`, `ratchet_atr_tf: str = "60m"`,
   `ratchet_target_r: float = 3.0`, `ratchet_extend: bool = True`. With `ratchet` on:
   - first risk = `ratchet_stop_atr` x ATR(14, Wilder) of the managed price on
     `ratchet_atr_tf` bars (bins anchored at 09:15), read on the last bar closed at or before
     the signal; when the strategy's timeframe is 60m or longer, on its own bars. One shared
     function in `src/onelot/manage/` used by the desk, the Lab and the Simulator.
   - first stop = fill -/+ risk; first target = fill +/- `ratchet_target_r` x risk, anchored
     the way the code anchors a target today (at the fill).
   - `cost` in the plan: the trade's round-trip costs per unit of the managed price (for an
     option: per unit of premium over the contract's delta), from the economics the alert
     already computes.
   - the strategy's own stop column is ignored for the first stop (it stays in the reasons
     text if it is shown today); `target_r`, `trail_atr_mult` and `line_trail` are ignored.
2. The alert: its underlying and premium stop and target are the plan's; `sig["manage"]`
   carries `ratchet: {stop_atr, atr_tf, target_r, extend, risk, cost}`; the alert text (page
   and Telegram) says in one line how the trade will be managed: "Stop above breakeven at 25%
   of the way, 35% locked at 50%, the target moves out from 80%."
3. The paper trade: for each new fine bar, in order, `ratchet.exit_on_bar` with the levels in
   force, then `ratchet.advance`; a level changed by a bar holds from the next bar. The state
   (stop, target, best, stage) is stored in the trade's plan JSON so a restart continues it.
   Events: `STOP_MOVED` with the stage, and a new `TARGET_MOVED`. Close reasons by stage:
   `STOP`, new `BREAKEVEN_STOP`, new `LOCK_STOP`, `TRAIL_STOP`; a gap keeps the existing gap
   reasons; `TARGET` only when the target was not moved; `SIGNAL_EXIT` and `SESSION_END` as
   today. Every place that lists or groups close reasons (ledger, views, CSV, scoreboard, the
   page's texts) knows the two new ones.
4. The manual position (after TAKE): the same state and levels; when the stop or target
   moves, the position's row shows it and one notification says "raise your stop to X" (use
   the channels that exist; no new channel).
5. The Lab: `simulate()` manages a `ratchet` strategy by the same functions on the
   one-minute bars it is given (`fine`), on its own bars when it has none.
6. The page: an open paper trade and an open position show the stage in plain words (first
   stop; stop above breakeven; 35% of the move locked; trailing behind the best price), the
   present stop and target and the first ones; a closed one shows the new reasons in plain
   words. Smallest possible change in `src/onelot/ui/app.js`; no colour literal; type no
   smaller than the surrounding text.
7. `config/desk.yaml`: `ratchet: true` on the micro-trend slot, and its evidence line gains
   one sentence from the study ("Tested 23 Nov on five years: the stop, target and trail rule
   cost nothing measurable and added nothing; the entries decide the result."). Keep the
   sentence that starts "Backtest of this exact rule: 3,824 trades" first and the last
   sentence "Not an edge: this is the paper test." (tests pin both).
8. A replay tool for the lead: `scripts/ratchet_desk_replay.py --store PATH --sessions N
   [--instrument NIFTY]` that opens the store READ-ONLY (SQLite `mode=ro`, as
   `scripts/pullback_replay.py` does), replays the micro-trend slot's paper trades through
   the real `PaperBook` (in a temporary database) over the last N stored sessions, and
   compares every trade's exit time, level and reason with a direct loop over `ratchet.py`.
   It prints the count that match and each mismatch.

## Acceptance criteria (tests; put new ones under `tests/paper/`, `tests/desk/`, `tests/lab/`)
1. With `ratchet` off, behaviour is unchanged: every existing test passes untouched, except
   where a test must learn the two new close reasons (say which and why).
2. Alert levels: given bars and a signal, the alert's stop is 1.5 x the 60m ATR from the
   fill-anchor price and the target 3 risks away; the ATR uses no bar that closes after the
   signal (cut the bars after the signal: same value).
3. Paper management equals the rule: on a hand-made day that walks through every stage, the
   paper trade's events, levels and close reason equal a direct replay with `ratchet.py`; a
   bar that touches stop and target is a stop; a gap fills at the open.
4. Several fine bars arriving in one poll are processed in order (same result as one by one).
5. Restart: a new `PaperBook` on the same database continues an open trade from its stored
   state (best price and stage are not reset).
6. The strategy exit and the session end still close a `ratchet` trade exactly as before.
7. Manual position: levels and stage follow the same bars; one notification per move.
8. Lab and paper agree: on a fixture, `simulate()` and the paper book give the same entry,
   exit and reason for a `ratchet` strategy.
9. Ledger, CSV and scoreboard include trades closed for `BREAKEVEN_STOP` and `LOCK_STOP`
   and do not crash on them; the page shows them in plain words (node tests, the way
   `tests/desk/test_paper_panel_ui.py` lifts functions from `app.js`).
10. The delivery path's tests pass untouched and `config/desk.yaml`'s pullback slot is
    byte-for-byte unchanged.
11. `scripts/ratchet_desk_replay.py` runs on a small fixture store in a test.
