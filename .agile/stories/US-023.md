# US-023 — Paper ledger in integer paise, with correct costs and option repricing

## Story
As the owner, I want every closed paper trade booked in exact integer paise with the right charges for its side and product, so that sums never drift and net P&L can be trusted.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given a closed trade, then gross_paise = sign times (exit minus entry) times qty, costs_paise = the sum over the two legs of round-half-up paise of CostModel.leg(price, qty, side).total with the real sides (a short pays STT on the ENTRY leg), net_paise = gross minus costs, risk_paise excludes costs, R = net divided by risk, wins mean net above zero; the per-trade total is within 1 paisa of round_trip_cost for longs.
- AC2: Given any sequence of closed trades, then PaperLedger.totals(grade=None).net_paise equals a Decimal-oracle sum exactly, is permutation-invariant, SQL typeof of every paise column is integer, and LedgerTotals includes trades, wins, losses, gross, costs and net.
- AC3: Given an index option trade, then each step reprices from the underlying with IV frozen at the alert and the close fill is round_to_tick down of premium times (1 minus spread) minus slippage ticks, floored at one tick; expiry day stays finite; a NaN VIX falls back; when repricing fails a PRICING_UNAVAILABLE event is recorded instead of a silent zero; a differential test against lab.execution.execute_trade agrees to zero paise with a fake pricer.
- AC4: Given economics._costs_for, then costs.yaml is parsed once per process (cached) instead of on every call, with a test asserting a single load.

## Non-functional requirements
**Manager 2026-11-01:**
- Integer paise; Decimal only at the cost-model boundary; each leg rounded once ROUND_HALF_UP; no float sums. Costs parsed once per process. A trade whose exit is unpriced (exit NULL) keeps costs/net NULL. NO_FILL rows keep zero costs/net.
- Backfill: CLOSED rows written by US-002 with costs/net NULL get them computed once at startup (idempotent), so the Paper panel shows net for today.
- Carry-overs: PaperTrade.from_row never truncates non-integer money (unknown instead); PaperBook._load_active skips and counts a corrupt row instead of failing to start.
