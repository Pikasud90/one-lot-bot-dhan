## What to build
1. The store's first and last minute without counting (F2, SIM-04): in
   `src/onelot/data/store.py` a query that reads the first and last `ts` of a (series,
   resolution) by index seek, and a process-wide cache of it that is dropped for a series
   when bars are written to it. `available_resolutions()` keeps its answer for its other
   callers but must no longer be what the Simulator, `load_bars` or `daily_bars` wait for.
   `GET /api/sim/options` meets its budget cold and warm.
2. Bars and frames (F3, SIM-02, SIM-05, F10): a bounded cache per series of cleaned
   one-minute frames (LRU across series, a cap in megabytes), daily bars computed once per
   series and data version, and a signal-frame cache keyed by (instrument, strategy,
   strategy timeframe, parameters that matter, the window, the data version). A second
   session on the same choice costs milliseconds. Bound the history a session loads to what
   its strategy needs (find each strategy's real warm-up; `HISTORY_DAYS = 120` for all is
   wasteful, but a shorter window must give the SAME signals: prove it per strategy with a
   test on fixture bars, and keep 120 where it does not). Sessions in memory: an LRU of a
   few; an older one is rebuilt from its rows when asked for.
3. Never in the app's way (SIM-03, F4, F6): the build of a session runs off the request,
   in a worker. `POST /api/sim/sessions` answers at once (BUILDING or RUNNING, contract);
   `GET /api/sim/prepare` warms the caches; one build runs and one waits. Choose thread or
   process by MEASUREMENT against the contract's stall budget (a thread that ticks every 10
   ms while builds run: no gap over 250 ms, 99th percentile under 50 ms): start with one
   worker thread; if the budget is not met, move the build into a worker process (it opens
   the store read-only itself and returns the finished frames), with a switch to run inline
   for the tests and for a platform where a process cannot be started. A lock per session,
   not one for the manager. The reload at the end of a 30-day window happens ahead of time in
   the worker, never inside an `advance`.
4. The second contract's routes and fields: `GET /api/sim/candles`, `GET /api/sim/days`,
   `GET /api/sim/prepare`, BUILDING and FAILED, `advance` with `until` and with `marks` and
   `overlays`, `events`, `overlays` and `marks` on the read, the new alert, trade and session
   fields, the event times of fills and next-open closes (SIM-12), the defaults.
5. The strategy's lines (`plots`): for micro trend (its 3m Supertrend line), the three other
   Supertrend strategies (their line), `ema_cross` and `ema_pullback` (their averages),
   `vwap_pullback` (the session VWAP), `orb` (the range's high and low), `donchian_breakout`
   (the channel), `cpr_breakout` (pivot, BC, TC). Take the values from the strategy's own
   frame where it has them, else compute them with the SAME indicator functions on the same
   bars. Only closed candles to the cursor are ever sent; prove no look-ahead by truncation
   (a value sent for a candle is the same when later bars are cut away). A strategy without
   plots sends none.
6. Fewer writes (F8): the cursor is saved on an event, on pause-like calls (take, skip,
   close, end) and at most every 30 revealed minutes, not on every advance; a restart resumes
   from the last saved cursor and the page is told the cursor it resumed at. The tables stay
   where they are.
7. `GET /api/sim/options` and the read of a session never rebuild a session to answer a
   resume offer (F5): the offer is answered from the stored row.

## Acceptance criteria (tests under `tests/sim/`)
1. Contract tests for every new route, field and status (FastAPI test client, mock store).
2. Sameness: for five strategy and instrument pairs on fixture bars, a session built cold,
   built from warm caches, built after another instrument's session, and rebuilt from its
   rows gives the same minutes, events, alerts and trades as the present engine (drive both
   with `tests/sim/tools/drive_sim.py` or its like). `until: "event"` and `until: "alert"`
   give the same events as minute-by-minute advances, in the same minutes.
3. Cache rules: a second create on the same choice does not read the bars again (a counting
   reader); writing bars to a series drops its cached first, last, frames and signals; the
   caps hold (a test fills past the cap and sees eviction).
4. The async path: create answers BUILDING, an action on it is a 409, the read turns
   RUNNING, a build that raises turns FAILED with a plain sentence, a second choice replaces
   the waiting build, ten quick creates leave one worker and no orphan.
5. No look-ahead for `candles` (nothing after `until`), `forming` (only minutes to `until`)
   and `overlays` (truncation), and the existing no-look-ahead tests pass untouched.
6. Event times: a fill at the 11:43 open carries 11:43; a stop hit in the 11:50 minute
   carries its close.
7. A measurement script for the lead, `scripts/sim_budget.py --store PATH [--runs 5]`: opens
   the store READ-ONLY, keeps the simulator's own tables in a temporary file, and prints for
   NIFTY micro trend 3m, BANKNIFTY and two stocks with two strategies each: options cold and
   warm, candles cold and warm, create answer time, time to RUNNING cold and warm, advance
   p50 and p95 for chunks of 1 and 30, and the ticker thread's worst gap and 99th percentile
   during builds, each against its budget with PASS or MISS. It runs on a small fixture store
   in a test.
8. The old engine tests pass, or change only where the contract changed (name each).
