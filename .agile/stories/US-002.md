# US-002 — Paper trades are managed to a close by the strategy exit rules

## Story
As the owner, I want each paper trade managed bar by bar and closed by the same kinds of exit the strategy uses, so that its outcome is a fair record of what the alert would have achieved.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given an OPEN trade, when bars are processed from the fill bar inclusive, then a long stops if a bar low is at or below the stop S (a touch counts, like the desk) and closes STOP at S, or at the bar OPEN when the bar opens at or below S (gap); STOP beats TARGET on the same bar; shorts mirror; for an index option alert (BUY_PE has direction short on the underlying with the stop above) the stop and target are on the underlying while the P&L is long premium.
- AC2: Given a target T, then a long target fills only if a bar high is at or above T plus one tick (it traded through), at exactly T, even on a gap-up where T lies outside the bar range; a bar that only reaches T exactly does not fill.
- AC3: Given trail_atr_mult above 0 and the close at or beyond trail_activate_r in profit, then the stop ratchets at bar close from that close and applies from the NEXT bar (never within the same bar), never loosens, uses the ATR frozen at alert time (None, 0 or NaN disables the trail, no fallback to live regime ATR), and a close by a moved stop is TRAIL_STOP iff the stop ever moved beyond the initial stop.
- AC4: Given the injected strategy_exit callback reports an exit or opposite signal seen on closed bar B, then the trade closes at the first fine-bar open after B closes with reason SIGNAL_EXIT or OPPOSITE_SIGNAL; when several reasons are due on one bar the precedence is STOP, TARGET, SESSION_END, SIGNAL_EXIT, OPPOSITE_SIGNAL, TIME_STOP.
- AC5: Given the strategy exit_time (default 15:15 IST), then every paper trade closes SESSION_END at the first bar open at or after it, or at the close of the last session bar when no bar exists after it, and this fires even when step first runs on a later day (date-aware, unlike the desk); until US-027 no trade is carried overnight; an intraday max_bars time stop closes TIME_STOP.
- AC6: Given step is called again with the same (now, bars), then it returns an empty list; stepping all bars at once yields the same events as stepping bar by bar (outage catch-up); event timestamps are bar or exit times, never now; a bar is eligible iff its open plus resolution is not later than now; booked events are final and store the bar prices they used even if the feed later revises that bar.
- AC7: Given any trade and bars truncated after time t, then every event up to t is unchanged (truncation property test, tick-grid integer price generators to avoid float ties).
- AC8: Given 45 open trades on 30,000-row frames, then step() uses a per-trade cursor with searchsorted and calls bars() once per series, and the shared helpers live in onelot/desk/rules.py as pure functions (ratchet_stop, r_multiple, bars_held, sessions_held, session_due, exit_priority) used by manage_step; Desk is not modified in this story.
