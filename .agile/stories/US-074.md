Owner, 2026-11-23: "check if we have been following this properly on our existing strategies
or not, if not simulate it properly". The running rules did not follow it (micro-trend: no
target and a far backstop; pullback: a fixed 15% stop). This story measures what the rule
does and picks the settings the product will use.

## The rule under test
`src/onelot/manage/ratchet.py` in this worktree. Read its module text first. Do NOT change
its behaviour. The owner fixed: 25% of the way to the target -> stop above breakeven; 50% ->

this study tests: the units of the first stop, `extend`, and whether the strategy's own exit
is kept.

## Part A: index options, micro-trend entries
- Entries: as `trades_of` (all seven trends newly aligned at a 3-minute close, 09:30 to
  14:45, one trade at a time per index). The exit changes when a trade ends, so re-run the
  entry loop for each exit variant: a new trade can only start after the previous one closed.
- Managed price: the INDEX on one-minute bars; side +1 for a call, -1 for a put. Entry as the
  reference (the open of the minute after the signal). Exit price: from `exit_on_bar` (the
  level, or the open on a gap); the open of the next minute for a strategy exit; the
  session's option exit (15:15) as the reference does it. Result: `h.pnl` with that exit.
- First stop: risk = k x ATR(14, Wilder) of the index on timeframe A, read on the last bar of
  A that closed at or before the entry minute. First target: 3 risks away. `cost` in the
  plan: 0.05% of the index level (a stand-in for round-trip premium cost over delta; say so).
- Grid, fixed in advance, 24 cells: A in {15m, 60m} x k in {1.0, 1.5, 2.0} x extend in
  {yes, no} x strategy exit (3 of 7 against) in {kept, dropped}. The session exit always
  applies.
- Baseline: the reference exit alone. Reproduce it exactly first: five indices 3,824 trades,
  -0.24% of premium a trade, 38.3% winners.
- Instruments: the four the owner trades (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, SENSEX) are THE result.
  MIDCPNIFTY is a training instrument: its own line, not in the totals.
- Per cell: trades, % of premium a trade (t), index bp, winners %, average winner, average
  loser, payoff ratio, TUNE / CHOOSE / TEST, and the share of exits by reason (first stop,
  breakeven stop, lock stop, trail stop, target, strategy exit, session end).
- Selection rule (fixed here, before any result): the cell with the highest TUNE mean among
  the cells whose CHOOSE mean is not below the baseline's CHOOSE mean. TEST of that one cell
  is the test. Every other TEST figure is "seen, not a test".
- Sensitivity on the chosen cell only: target_r 2 and 4; trail_gap 0.15 and 0.30.
- Real premiums (stage `real`, after 15:40 only; NIFTY and BANKNIFTY; chosen cell and
  baseline): price every trade with `CarryPricer.price_one`, report like for like (the real
  result where priced, the model less the measured gap where not). If you finish before
  15:40, leave the exact command in the note and say it is still to run.

  bar, a trade counted twice, a stop that moved in the same bar it was set.
