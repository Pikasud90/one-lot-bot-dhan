# US-017 — Mobile-responsive PWA, keyboard shortcuts and notification sound

## Story
As the owner, I want to use the desk comfortably on my phone and keyboard, so that I can respond to an alert in seconds from anywhere in the house.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given a 390 pixel wide screen, then every tab is usable without horizontal scrolling and the Taken/Skip controls are at least 44 pixels high.
- AC2: Given the web app manifest and service worker, then the app can be installed to the home screen and shows a clear offline state instead of a broken page.
- AC3: Given keyboard shortcuts (g then h/l/b/d/n/s for tabs, t to take and s to skip the top pending alert, ? for help), then they work and are listed in a help overlay; sound plays on a new alert only after the user has enabled it.
