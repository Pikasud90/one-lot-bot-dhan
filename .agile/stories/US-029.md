# US-029 — Fallback bars never pollute a Dhan series; alerts report the resolution that served them

## Story
As the owner, I want a series that briefly falls back to Yahoo to keep its Dhan 1-minute bars intact and every alert to state the bar resolution that actually served it, so that paper fills and strategy signals are computed on one consistent series.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: AC1: Given a series Dhan served before and Dhan returns no bars on a later poll, when Yahoo serves it, then Yahoo 2-minute bars are never merged into the Dhan 1-minute frame (no keep=last overwrite at even minutes); the tracker either keeps the Dhan frame and marks the series stale or switches the whole frame to Yahoo with resolution 2m, and the choice is logged.
- AC2: AC2: Given an alert raised on a Yahoo-served series, then data.resolution is the served resolution (2m), not the desk fine resolution.
- AC3: AC3: Given Dhan recovers, then the series returns to Dhan 1-minute bars without duplicate or mixed-spacing rows.
