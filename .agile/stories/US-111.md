# US-111: Decision: an index alert when no contract in the chain trades

## Story
As the owner, I want to decide what the desk does when nothing in the option chain trades, so that
no paper result rests on a strike and a premium the desk only estimated.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given the owner chooses behaviour A (today), then nothing changes and this story closes with
  the choice written in docs/04_OPERATIONS.md.
- AC2: Given the owner chooses behaviour B, when the chain has rows of the side but none tradeable,
  then the alert is still raised with the strike and premium of the model, and its card and phone
  text start with "NO TRADEABLE CONTRACT: the strike and premium are an estimate".
- AC3: Given behaviour B, then the paper trade of that alert is marked left out (the US-105 mark,
  reason "no tradeable contract in the chain") and counts in no score, with its row tag and the
  left-out figure showing it.
- AC4: Given behaviour B, then an alert from a chain that has no rows at all, or from no chain (Data
  API off), is unchanged.
- AC5: Given behaviour B, then the confidence record and every score count the same alerts as before
  except the marked ones (tests name each score).

## Non-functional requirements
- The alert, its prices and its exit stay recorded as they are; only the mark is new (the rule of
  US-105: nothing is deleted).
- Not a promise of profit: the mark is about what the desk can know, not about the result.

## Public contract
- `Desk._economics` (`src/onelot/desk/desk.py` line 1330): `untradeable` is True when the chain had
  rows of the side and none tradeable; the model is then used and `NO_TRADEABLE_NOTE`
  (`src/onelot/desk/economics.py` line 252) is put first in its notes.
- `src/onelot/void.py` `void_reason(alert)`: today void only when `economics.synthetic is False` and
  `option.oi == 0`; behaviour B adds a second reason.
- `paper_void` (`src/onelot/store/schema.py` line 624), `PaperBook` marking at open and at start
  (`src/onelot/paper/book.py` lines 931 to 966), the page tag `voidTag` in `src/onelot/ui/app.js`.

## Notes & assumptions

Found in `.agile/stories/US-104.md` (C4: with nothing tradeable the alert falls back to the model
and says so in its first note) and `docs/04_OPERATIONS.md`. The lead's question of 25 Nov: should
such an alert be raised at all?

Behaviour A, today. The alert is raised with the model's strike and premium (`economics.synthetic`
True, the note first). The paper book opens a trade priced on that estimate, and the trade counts
in the scores, because `void_reason` returns None for a synthetic alert. The result then rests on
a premium nobody could have paid.

Behaviour B, written above. The signal is still shown (the owner learns that the rule fired), but
marked, and its paper trade is left out of every score like the five of US-105.

A third behaviour C, no alert at all while nothing trades, is NOT written as a criterion: the owner
then never sees the rule fire on a thin day, and the rule's record loses the day. It is named so
the owner can choose it; if chosen, rewrite the criteria.

The lead's lean: B. This is a decision story: no code before the owner answers. Related: US-110 (a
cap per index changes how often this happens).
