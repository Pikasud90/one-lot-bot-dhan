## Story
As the owner, I want the 2027 trading holidays in the calendar file, so that the desk does not treat a 2027 holiday as a trading day

- AC1: Given config/nse_holidays.yaml, when it is read for 2027, then it holds every trading holiday of the exchange circular for 2027 (it held only 4 dates on 25 Nov 2026 against 23 for 2026)

- AC3: Given the file, when a test reads it, then each year from the current one has at least 12 dates or the test names the short year
