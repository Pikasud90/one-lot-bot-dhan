# US-097: the desk tells when it was not running, never alerts on unclean bars, shows the spread

From the study of the desk's own live alerts (`docs/research/LIVE_ALERTS_2026-11-24.md`) and
the lead's checks of 25 Nov 2026. The owner asked for that study so that the desk could be
improved from its live record. Its three proposals and four small faults found beside them:

- The desk was NOT RUNNING on 5 of the 7 sessions after the micro-trend rule went live (16 to
  20 Nov) and nothing recorded it; the owner did not know. It missed 16 of 17 trades.
- One alert (MIDCPNIFTY, 23 Nov, 13:15) cannot be reproduced on the stored bars. That morning
  the desk started late and its bars before 10:57 were two-minute bars from the delayed source.
- The recorded quotes show option spreads far wider than the cost model on FINNIFTY and SENSEX
  (median half spread about 1.5% of the premium, 7% before 10:00 on SENSEX) and the alert does
  not show the spread.

## What to build
1. UPTIME. While it runs the desk records a heartbeat, at most one small write every five
   minutes: one row per calendar day in a new table `desk_uptime` (day, first seen, last
   seen, beats, started late or not). `GET /api/desk/uptime?days=30` gives, per TRADING day
   (the app's own calendar), from when to when the desk ran inside market hours and the share
   of the session covered. Home shows ONE plain line, only when a session of the last ten was
   missed or covered under 90%: "The desk did not run on 5 of the last 10 sessions: 16, 17,
   18, 19, 20 Nov." Find the right place in the Today strip; no new card.
2. ON THE PHONE (through the Notifier, kind `desk`, which the Telegram channel must forward
   like `token` and `volume`; each once per day by its own dedup key):
   - "Desk started 09:02. Token valid until 21:31. Feed: live." at every start; when the start
     is after 09:15 on a trading day it adds "Started late: alerts before 10:57 were missed."
   - a morning check at 09:05 IST on trading days when the desk is up: the same three facts.
   Nothing can be sent while the laptop sleeps or the desk is stopped: the docs say so and
   advise the owner to treat a missing 09:05 message as "the desk is down".
3. CLEAN BARS FOR THE MICRO-TREND RULE. A slot whose strategy needs one-minute bars raises no
   alert for an instrument while the bars it reads today are not all one-minute bars of the
   live source: any bar of today from the delayed source, a two-minute bar, or a hole longer
   than five minutes before the signal. Find how the desk merges sources and marks an
   instrument delayed (`src/onelot/desk/data.py`, `Desk._maybe_switch_source`, `delayed_keys`,
   the grade a paper trade gets) and put the check where the verdict becomes an alert
   (`Desk._raise_signal`), with the reason shown on the instrument's card ("waiting for clean
   one-minute bars since 10:57") instead of an alert. The pullback rule (daily bars from the
   store) is not affected. Do not weaken or widen any other gate.
4. THE SPREAD ON THE ALERT. When an option alert is raised and a quote of its contract not
   older than 60 seconds is at hand (the fill-cost recorder takes them: `src/onelot/desk/
   fillcost.py`, table `fill_quotes`), the alert carries `spread_pct` (half the bid-ask spread
   in % of the mid) and, when it is 1% or more, the line "Wide spread now: about 1.8% a side"
   on the alert card and in the Telegram text. No quote, no line; never wait for a quote
   before raising the alert. The alert's recorded prices do not change.
5. VOLUME WATCH never makes a request wait. Today the first view of a session builds its
   baselines inside the request (6 s on the lead's check). The body gains `"building": true`
   with `now` null, empty `minutes`, `stocks`, `indices` and `alerts`, and a note "Preparing
   this session…", while a background thread builds; the page (`src/onelot/ui/volume.js`)
   asks again every 2 seconds while `building` is true, for a past date too. Add the key to
   `.agile/stories/VOL-API.md`. Also: "Likely to move" names an index only when that index's
   own pressure in the spike (influence x abnormal net flow, summed over ALL basket stocks)
   is among the two largest and above a small floor, so a spike led by ITC does not name the
   bank indices.
6. INDIA VIX in the nightly top-up: the top-up starts when ANY series it tops up is behind the
   last finished session (today only the delivery series are looked at, so a gap in INDIAVIX
   alone never starts it: `src/onelot/delivery/runner.py: maybe_top_up`).
7. TOKEN REMINDER at 08:30: when the desk first looks after 09:15 on a trading day and the
   token is still expired, the "renew" reminder is sent then, once that day
   (`src/onelot/notify/token_reminders.py`).

## Acceptance criteria (tests beside the code they test)
1. Uptime: beats are throttled (a simulated clock); a restart continues the day's row; the
   route's per-day cover on a hand-made month with a holiday, a missed day and a late start;
   the Home line appears only when it should (a node test of its text function).
2. Phone: the start message, the late-start addition, the morning check once a day, kind
   `desk` forwarded by the Telegram channel (the fake of `tests/api/fake_telegram.py`), and
   nothing sent in mock mode without credentials.
3. Clean bars: with mixed or delayed bars today the micro-trend slot raises nothing and the
   card says why; with clean bars it raises exactly what it raised before (the existing desk
   tests pass untouched); the pullback slot is unaffected; a replay with
   `scripts/ratchet_desk_replay.py` on a small fixture store gives the same result as before.
4. Spread: an alert with a fresh quote carries `spread_pct` and the line at 1% or more; a
   stale or missing quote gives no key and no line; raising the alert never waits (a test
   with a quote source that sleeps).
5. Volume: the first request for a session answers in under 100 ms with `building: true`, a
   later one has the full body; an exception in the build is caught and said in `notes`;
   the page test for the 2-second re-ask; the "likely to move" rule on hand-made flows.
6. Top-up: INDIAVIX behind alone starts it; nothing behind starts nothing.
7. Reminder: first look at 10:30 on a trading day with an expired token sends one reminder;
   a second look the same day sends none; a non-trading day sends none.
8. No test of the alert path, the paper book or the delivery path changes except where this
   story changes behaviour on purpose (name each).
