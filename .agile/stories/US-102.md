# US-102: the wide list's evening round waits for the broker's daily bars of the session

Lead, 2026-11-25, after the first rehearsal of the round against the real broker (US-099),
on a private store.

Why: at 15:42 IST on Wed 25 Nov 2026 the broker's daily history held NO bar of the day for
any of the 204 stocks (the newest bar was the session before). The round as built made 204
requests, called every stock "waiting" and counted a try. Four such tries 30 minutes apart
(about 15:36 to 17:06) closed the round "with what it has", and the session's signals were
then read the next evening as LATE: recorded only (tier "test"), never within the cap, never
alerted. So whenever the broker publishes the day's bars after about 17:10, no wide-list
trade would ever be within the cap and no wide-list alert ever sent, silently.

## What exists (read before designing)
- `src/onelot/delivery/wide.py`: `WideDesk.session_for`, `maybe_round`, `run_round`,
  `_fetch_all`, `_fetch_one`; the constants `ROUND_TRIES = 4`, `ROUND_GAP = 30 minutes`,
  `NO_ROUND = 09:00 to 15:35`.
- The precedent: the round's wait for NIFTY is "not a try of its own"
  (`test_a_wait_for_nifty_is_not_a_try` in `tests/delivery/test_wide_safety.py`).
- The helpers `tests/delivery/wide_helpers.py` (a fake `Broker` with `published` and `fail`,
  a recording notifier).

## Rules built
- W1 Not published yet is not a try. A round that fetches and ends with no stock of the list
  having the session's bar (none fetched for it earlier, none in this attempt) is not a try:
  `wide_rounds.tries` unchanged, `finished_at` empty, nothing evaluated (no new
  `last_session`, no trade inserted or changed), `RoundResult.finished` False.
- W2 A cheap check. While no stock has the session's bar, an attempt stops after `PROBE = 5`
  stocks, in the list's own order, whose answer did not carry it ("waiting", "stale" or an
  error): at most 5 requests when the stocks have stored history. As soon as one stock has
  the bar (in this attempt, or fetched for the session earlier) there is no early stop.
- W3 Again later, for as long as it makes sense. `maybe_round` starts the next attempt
  `ROUND_GAP` after the last one for as long as the session is still `session_for(now)` and
  the clock is outside 09:00 to 15:35: the same evening, through the night and before 09:00
  the next morning. When the bars are there, the session's signals are on time.
- W4 Counted tries are unchanged once the session is there: the round finishes when no stock
  is waiting, or after `ROUND_TRIES` counted tries with what it has.
- W5 Plain words while waiting. The round's note and `WideDesk.note` read, for example,
  "Dhan has not published the daily bars of 2026-11-25 yet (checked 15:42): the round looks
  again every 30 minutes", or, when every answer of the check was an error, "Dhan did not
  answer (5 requests failed, checked 15:42): the round tries again every 30 minutes". The
  summary's "waiting" and "errors" hold only the check's own stocks and errors. The page
  prints the note beside "not finished".
- W6 The round's `started_at` is the first attempt's time across the waiting attempts.
- W7 The words: the module docstring of `wide.py` and the wide-list part of
  `docs/04_OPERATIONS.md` (with the line "First seen at: TO BE MEASURED" for the lead).

Not in scope: the rule, the tiers, the cap, the alert texts, the desk 18's code, the late
rule, `FETCH_DAYS`, the adjustments logic, the NIFTY wait.

## Tests
`tests/delivery/test_wide_wait.py` (each through the product's own path, a fake broker):
- T1 not published: one attempt makes at most 5 requests, not a try, no state change, no
  trade, both notes in the exact words.
- T2 the long wait: seven attempts 30 minutes apart in the evening, one after midnight and
  one at 08:00, all not published: tries 0, at most 5 requests each, started_at the first
  attempt's. Then the broker publishes and the 08:30 attempt reads every stock: the signal is
  tier "cap", not late, alerted once; the round finished, tries 1, the notes cleared.
- T3 the gap: a second call within 30 minutes makes no request.
- T4 every answer fails: 5 requests, not a try, 5 errors listed, "Dhan did not answer".
- T5 partly there: one of the first five has the bar: every stock is asked, the others are
  waiting, the try is counted, and the round closes after `ROUND_TRIES` counted tries.
- T6 a resumed round asks only the stocks not yet fetched, with no early stop.
- T7 a first round while not published records nothing (no late trade of the session
  before); once published, the newest session only is read.
- T8 the bars never come: the next session's round reads the old session late (tier "test",
  never alerted); the old round row stays unfinished with tries 0 and blocks nothing.
- T9 `tests/delivery/test_wide_ui.py`: the page's `wideRound` of a waiting round, built by the
  real desk, shows "not finished" and the note.

## What was changed
- `src/onelot/delivery/wide.py`: the constant `PROBE`; `_fetch_all` answers whether the
  session is there and, while it is not, stops after `PROBE` answers without the bar and
  holds back their stock states (written as soon as one stock has the bar); `run_round` saves
  a check that found nothing as "not a try" (`tries - 1`, as the NIFTY wait) with the note of
  `_not_yet`; the docstrings.
- `docs/04_OPERATIONS.md`: the waiting paragraph of the wide list.

## After the two reviews
- The errors of a check that found no bar are not carried into the round that later reads
  the session: a check's summary is marked (`check`) and a resumed round merges only the
  record of counted tries
  (`test_the_errors_of_a_check_do_not_carry_into_the_round_that_reads_the_session`).
- One request a stock in a check: a stored history that disagrees with a short answer without
  the session's bar is "waiting" and is fetched again whole only once the session is there,
  so a check makes at most 5 requests in every case
  (`test_a_check_makes_five_requests_even_when_every_stored_history_disagrees`). A "stale"
  answer (the refetch failed) can then come only from a short answer that carried the bar:
  the session is there and the try counts
  (`test_a_stale_answer_carried_the_bar_so_the_session_is_there_and_the_try_counts`,
  `test_a_disagreeing_stock_whose_refetch_fails_while_the_bars_are_there_is_stale`).
- A check starts `PROBE` stocks further down the list than the check before (the count of
  checks is kept in the round's record, so a restart keeps it): five stocks with no bar of
  the session (suspended, a bad id) at the head of the list no longer hide the others for
  ever (`test_five_stocks_without_the_bar_at_the_head_of_the_list_do_not_block_it`). The
  first check of a session still starts at the head of the list.
- A check stopped at 09:00 before any request says so, not that Dhan has not published
  (`test_a_check_stopped_at_nine_before_any_request_does_not_say_dhan_has_not_published`).
- T1 and T4 assert the literal 5 requests; the docstring of `maybe_round` is wrapped at 100
  characters; "First seen at: TO BE MEASURED" is its own paragraph in the operations guide.
