# US-080: which technical setup for which market condition? A study

Owner, 2026-11-23: "for the strategies employing the technical indicators ... we have to
identify the best conditions some of the selected strategies work like for trending market,
for choppy market, for falling market and we dynamically switch between them but the
timeframe, setup and conditions need to be well researched before we employ them."

What is already known (do not redo): `docs/research/STRATEGIES_TESTED_2026-11-12.md`,
`ALPHA_SEARCH_2026-11-12.md`, `MICROTREND_STUDY_2026-11-13.md` and
`RATCHET_STUDY_2026-11-23.md` (in this worktree). No same-day rule has shown an edge after
costs without conditions; an exit rule adds none; a buyer of index options needs about 5 to
10 bp of index move a trade to cover costs, and real premiums came out about 1 point of
premium a trade below the model. This study asks one new thing: does any registered strategy
earn after costs INSIDE one market condition known before the session opens?

## Fixed in advance (write these into the note before any result)

### Instruments and trades
- Index options: NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, SENSEX. A long signal buys a call, a short
  signal buys a put, one lot, same day only. Result in % of premium by the harness's model
  (`h.pnl`), and "less 1.0 point" as the real-premium reading.
- Stocks: the 18 desk stocks, one share, same day only, long and short signals; result in %
  of price after intraday equity charges and slippage (use the product's cost model for
  intraday equity; state the round-trip cost in basis points you end up with).
- Periods TUNE to 2024-11-07, CHOOSE to 2025-11-07, TEST to 2026-11-07. Every t treats all
  trades of one entry day as one draw.

### Strategies
Every strategy in `src/onelot/strategies/registry.py: REGISTRY` that suits the instrument
kind (`SUITABLE`), with its DEFAULT parameters and DEFAULT timeframe. No tuning of any
parameter. Signals from the strategy's own `generate()` the way the Lab prepares them
(`src/onelot/lab/runner.py: generate_for_instrument`): entry columns `long_entry` /
`short_entry`, exit columns `long_exit` / `short_exit`. Entry at the open of the minute after
the signal bar closes; one trade at a time per instrument and strategy; entries inside the
strategy's own entry window.

### Exit: the desk's rule, one setting
`src/onelot/manage/ratchet.py` with the product's setting: first stop 1.5 x ATR(14, Wilder)
of 60m bars of the managed price (the index for an option, the share for a stock) read on
the last bar closed at the entry; target 3 risks away; `extend` on; the strategy's own exit
signal kept (exit at the next minute's open); the session exit as the harness has it.
Reuse the exit engine of `scripts/ratchet_study.py` in this worktree (it is proven equal to
the module's bar-by-bar loop); import it by file path.

### Market conditions (known before the open: daily bars to the previous session only)
Per instrument (the index itself for an index option, the stock itself for a stock):
- ADX(14, Wilder) on daily bars and the close against its SMA(20).
- UP-TREND: ADX >= 25 and close > SMA20. DOWN-TREND: ADX >= 25 and close < SMA20.
  CHOPPY: ADX < 20. NEUTRAL: everything else.
- A second, independent cut for robustness only: India VIX above or below its 250-session
  median (previous day's values).

### What counts
- Cell = strategy x condition x group (index options, stocks). Report every cell with at
  least 100 trades: trades, % a trade (t), winners %, payoff, TUNE / CHOOSE / TEST.
- A cell is a CANDIDATE when its TUNE mean is positive with t of 2 or more AND its CHOOSE
  mean is positive. Its test is its TEST mean (positive, and for options still positive less
  1.0 point). State how many cells were examined and how many candidates chance alone would
  give (about 2.5% of cells, before the CHOOSE filter).
- The switch: for each condition and group take the candidate with the highest TUNE mean (no
  trade in a condition with no candidate). Report the switch as one portfolio on CHOOSE and
  on TEST: trades, % a trade (t), by condition.
- Also report, per strategy and group, the result over ALL conditions together, so the gain
  from conditioning is visible.
