# US-076: the Simulator tab

As the owner I want a tab where I pick an instrument, a strategy and a timeframe, watch the
chart build candle by candle from past data (or follow the live feed), get the strategy's
alert with a notification, take the trade, see its stop and target on the chart move as the
trade develops, close it, and read the log. References the owner named: TradingView's bar
replay and MetaTrader's visual tester.

The engine is another story (US-075) and is NOT in this worktree. The JSON it will serve is
fixed in `.agile/stories/SIM-API.md`: read it first and call exactly that.

  theme blocks at the top of `styles.css`); NO colour literal (no #hex, rgb(), named colour).

- `src/onelot/ui/index.html`: only inside `<div id="sim-root">`. Do not touch `app.js`,
  `styles.css`, `charts.js` or any other part of the page.

  candle by candle; `Charts.candles` rebuilds the chart on every call).

## What the tab does

### Setup bar
Mode (Past data / Live feed), Instrument, Strategy (only those whose `kinds` include the
instrument's kind), Timeframe (the strategy's list), Start date and time (Past only; default
the last stored session at 09:15; limited to the instrument's `first` to `last`), and a
Start button. Choices are remembered (`OneLot.store`). An instrument with `trade: false`
shows "training only". Live is offered only when the instrument has `live: true`.

### Chart
Candles of the chosen timeframe built from the one-minute bars the server reveals (bins
anchored at 09:15 each day). The forming candle updates as minutes arrive. Lines: entry,
stop, target (labelled, moving when `STOP_MOVED` / `TARGET_MOVED` arrive; the first stop and
target stay as faint lines). Markers: alert (arrow in its direction), fill, exit.

### Clock and speed
- A market clock (date and time of the cursor) and Play / Pause / Step (one candle).
- Speeds 1x, 2x, 5x, 10x, 60x, 300x = market seconds per real second (1x is real time).
- The server owns the cursor: the page asks `advance` for the next minute(s) when its own
  clock reaches them. Up to 10x: one minute per call, and the page animates that minute
  inside the forming candle (open -> low -> high -> close when it closed up, open -> high ->
  low -> close when down) over the real time the minute takes, THEN shows the minute's
  events, so an alert is never announced before its candle is seen. At 60x and 300x: no
  animation; ask for as many minutes as the elapsed real time needs (several per call).
- "Pause on alert" (default on) stops the clock when an alert arrives. "Sound" (default on)
  plays a short beep made with the Web Audio API (no audio file).
- Keys while the tab is shown and no field has the focus: Space play/pause, Right arrow step.
- A tab left in the background must not race ahead: pause when `document.hidden`.
- Live mode: no speed buttons; a LIVE badge; ask `advance` every 5 seconds.

### Alert, position, close
- An `ALERT` shows a card: what to do (BUY CALL / BUY PUT / BUY / SELL), entry, stop, target
  on the managed price, risk and reward in rupees, costs, the traded instrument and its
  price with `real` or `modelled`, the reasons, a countdown to expiry, and Take / Skip. It
  also fires `OneLot.toast` and the beep.
- After Take: "waiting for the fill at the next minute's open", then the position card: side,
  quantity, entry, the present stop and target, the stage in plain words (first stop; stop
  above breakeven; 35% of the move locked; trailing behind the best price), progress to the
  target as a bar with the 25%, 50% and 80% marks, open profit in rupees, and Close.
- `EXIT_SIGNAL` shows "the strategy says close" on the card; `CLOSED` shows the result with
  its reason in plain words (stopped out at the first stop; stopped at breakeven; stopped
  with 35% locked; trailing stop; target; strategy exit; session end; closed by you).

### Log and summary
- Session summary: trades, winners, net result, charges, average winner, average loser,
  best, worst, equity against the starting capital.
- This session's closed trades (newest first): time in and out, what was traded, entry,
  exit, reason, gross, charges, net, R multiple. A second view "All simulator trades" from
  `GET /api/sim/trades`, and a "Download CSV" link to `/api/sim/trades.csv`.
- A reload of the page offers to resume the last session (`GET /api/sim/sessions/{id}`).
- A short box "How this simulator works": fills at the next minute's open with slippage and
  charges; option premiums are real traded ones where stored, otherwise modelled and marked;
  stops and targets rest in the market and close by themselves; trades are practice trades
  kept apart from the paper test; past results are not a promise.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Pure helpers are unit-tested in node the way `tests/mllab/test_mllab_ui.py` lifts
   functions out of a JS file (helpers must be single-line `const name = ...;` or functions
   whose closing brace stands alone at column 0). Put the tests in
   `tests/sim/test_sim_ui.py`. Cover at least: building candles from minute bars across a
   day boundary and for 3m, 15m and 60m; the animation path of a minute; how many minutes to
   ask for at each speed; the plain-words texts for stages and exit reasons; the alert card
   and the position card from the contract's sample objects; values are escaped (`esc`).
2. A fake of the API for tests and for looking at the tab without the engine:
   `src/onelot/ui/sim_fake.js`, which serves the contract from generated bars with an alert,
   a fill, stage events and a close. The page loads it (by adding a script tag) ONLY when the
   URL has `?simfake=1`, and then shows a banner "Demo data, not market data" in the tab.
   The node tests drive the same file.
3. `tests/acceptance/test_us030_ac2_static_ui.py` and the other static UI tests stay green;
   `sim.css` has no colour literal (add a test for that).
4. No error in the console when the tab opens before the engine exists: a failed
   `GET /api/sim/options` shows "The simulator's engine is not running in this build" in
   the tab, not a broken page.
