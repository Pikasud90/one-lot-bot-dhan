# US-103: a wide-list stock that stops trading never holds a place for good

Lead, 2026-11-25, on review finding F4 of US-099 (left open there): a stock that stops trading
(a suspension, a merger, a delisting) or stays stale gets no new daily bar, so its open paper
trade is never closed, it holds one of the ten places within the cap for good, and every
evening round uses all four tries waiting for it. A trade "held for review" after an unadjusted
corporate action likewise keeps its place with no release.

Branch `story/US-103-wide-no-data`, rebased onto `sprint-04-alpha-and-watchdog` at af0a48c
(US-102 merged).

## What is built
1. MISSING SESSIONS. For a stock that has daily bars but none of the round's session, the
   round reads from the stored bars how many MARKET SESSIONS have passed since its last bar.
   A market session is a trading day of the app's calendar on which at least half of the
   list's other stocks did get a bar. Not counted: a holiday the calendar does not list (no
   stock has a bar), a special session off the calendar (a Muhurat, a Budget weekend) and an
   evening on which the bars of a few stocks only came.
2. TWO GUARDS on top of that count:
   - the desk must find the stock without a new bar on three evening rounds in a row: two
     that count (`CONFIRM_ROUNDS`) and the one that releases. The count is kept in the
     stock's state row (`wide_state.reading.quiet`), goes up once an evening and starts
     again when a new bar is stored;
   - the stock turns dormant at the first evening round AFTER both counts were met, if it
     still has no bar. The bar of the last session counted has had a day to arrive.
3. DORMANT. `dormant_after` sessions (the pullback slot in `config/desk.yaml`, default 10,
   0 = off) and both guards: the stock is dormant, marked in its state row
   (`reading.dormant`: since which session, its last bar, both counts). With the desk
   running every evening that is the round of the eleventh session without a bar.
   - an OPEN or EXIT PENDING trade is closed at the stock's last stored close, booked as a sale
     is (both legs' charges, the depository charge, no slippage), status CLOSED, exit reason
     `NO_DATA`; an exit already signalled keeps its signal date and the note says it was never
     filled; the trade is first put on the basis of the bars it is closed on (`_rebase`);
   - a PENDING ENTRY is cancelled: status `CANCELLED`, never filled, never a trade;
   - the place within the cap is free before that round's own signals are admitted;
   - the round stops waiting for the stock: its missing bar no longer keeps the round open
     for four tries. The stock is still asked for on every try, and read again when its bars
     return ("daily bars are back" in the round's line); a released trade stays released.
4. HELD FOR REVIEW. An exit held for review (an unadjusted action) is released on the same
   count: on the `dormant_after`-th session counted from the one that first held it, once
   two evening rounds in a row before have seen it held, at the close of the newest session
   the round reads of the stock, exit reason `REVIEW`. Its result may be on another price
   basis than its entry: the note says "Check by hand". The stock is not dormant (it trades).
5. THE SCORE keeps both apart: its two rows count the rule's own exits only; each tier also
   answers `released` (trades, winners, net, how many `no_data` and `review`) and `cancelled`.
6. THE FETCH now starts at least 10 days before the stock's last stored bar (it was always
   45 days back from the session). After a silence longer than 45 days the overlap check still
   has shared days and no hole is left in the history.
7. THE PAGE (the Wide list block, Home and Live Desk): the round's line names the trades
   "released without the rule's exit", the stocks "dormant, not waited for" and those whose
   "daily bars are back"; a line lists the dormant stocks with their last bar; a line under
   the score counts what is "Not in the score"; a closed row says "no data" or "held for
   review" in words with the trade's note, and "entry cancelled: no data".

## Decisions, and why
- Sessions from the stored bars, as the brief says, plus evenings of the desk's own.
  Counting from the bars alone failed this case: the desk is off for two weeks, and on its
  first evening back one request fails. The others then have all those sessions' bars and
  the one stock looks ten sessions silent: its trade would be released at a two-week-old
  price for one failed request. Counting evenings alone (a second version) was safe there
  but slow on a desk that does not run every evening: a dead stock would have kept its place
  for ten evenings the desk ran. Both together: ten sessions in the bars, found so on two
  evenings in a row, and still so on a third.
- The evening after, not the evening itself. On the tenth evening the stock's bar may be
  merely late. A third version waited for it through that evening's four tries and released
  on the last; but a desk that is shut before its fourth try would then never release. So
  the release waits one evening instead: it falls on the first try of the next round, needs
  no particular try, and comes before that round's own signals are admitted.
- At least half of the other stocks, not "any other stock": one stray bar on a holiday must
  not make a session, and when the broker's data is broadly broken nothing may be released.
- The price is the last stored close, without slippage (it is a mark, no sale happens), but
  with a sale's charges, so "net" means the same as on every other closed trade. The exit
  session is the round's session (when the place became free); the note names the last bar.
- A cancelled entry gets its own status, as the desk 18's book has MISSED: it never was a
  trade and must not count as one.
- No new table and no new column: the counts and the mark are in `wide_state.reading`
  (JSON). A store that already has the US-099 tables needs no change.
- Why 10: in the study file (222 stocks, 16 years) the longest hole in one stock's bars is
  5 market sessions, and one stock (RELINFRA, Dec 2025 to Aug 2026) had a bar only once a
  week. At 10 no stock of the file is ever dormant. At 3 a stock would have gone dormant 26
  times (every hole of 3 sessions or more), 21 of them that one stock.
- Nothing is sent to the phone on a release (the brief asks for the round's note and the
  block). If the owner bought the shares by hand, the page is where they learn of it.

## Tests (`tests/delivery/test_wide_dormant.py`, 31; no existing test changed)
The tests run each evening through its tries, as the desk does.
- a stock stops trading with an open trade within the cap: waited for on the evenings that
  count, closed at the next evening's first try, the place goes to that evening's new signal,
  an old recorded trade never moves up, the mark, the view and the score apart; the same
  without a broker;
- a pending entry: cancelled, never filled, also when the bars return;
- a holiday the calendar does not list, a listed holiday with a special session, a Saturday
  session, and an evening on which under half of the others got a bar: not counted, and no
  stock turns dormant at the round of such a day;
- a stock that trades every third session, and one whose bar always comes a day late: never
  dormant; a bar that is merely late on the evening the counts are met: nothing released;
  a bar that comes late on the release evening itself: the release stands;
- after ten sessions off, one bad evening and a second: nothing released; a desk that runs
  one evening in three still releases a dead stock on its third evening; a desk that is shut
  after one try each evening still releases;
- a stale stock with an open trade: released at the last stored close on its entry's basis,
  read again once its refetch works; a history re-based just before the silence: the trade
  is released on the new basis;
- the round's tries: four while the stock is not dormant, one once it is, the stock still
  asked for; bars that return after 60 sessions of failed requests: fetched from before the
  last stored bar, no hole, the stock read again, its next signal a new trade on time; a
  dormant stock whose bars return a day late wakes all the same;
- an exit signalled and never filled; an exit held for review: released on the same count,
  never in a hurry after time off, on the newest session read, also for a stock read a day
  late;
- a round run again, a restart in the middle of a count and after the release; the switch
  off (`dormant_after: 0`), also after a stock was marked; one stock alone is never dormant;
  a dormant stock whose bars cannot be read one evening keeps its mark;
- the setting read strictly; the page (run in node) and the route.
Each guard in the new code was broken on purpose (27 ways): each time a test failed.

## Review (an independent read, 25 Nov) and what was done
- MAJOR, fixed: the held-for-review release never fired for a stock read a day late (it
  waited for the round's own session). It now fires on the newest session the round reads.
- MINOR, fixed: a stock could turn dormant on an evening's first try while its bar was
  merely late. It now turns dormant at the next evening's round, if it still has no bar.
- MINOR, fixed: the count was in evening rounds while the brief and the page said sessions.
  The sessions are now read from the bars; the evenings are a guard (see Decisions).
- MINOR, fixed: three new type errors; a dormant stock whose bars failed to load one evening
  lost its mark.
- NIT, fixed: the REVIEW note keeps the trade's earlier note; both releases fall on the N-th
  session; the two other tools that run the product's round pass the slot's value.
- Left for the backlog: a stock taken off `config/delivery_universe.yaml` while it has an
  active wide trade still holds its place (the round skips stocks not on the list).
- Round 2, on the final rule: APPROVE. Its one point is under "Not done, and limits" (a bar
  that comes late on the release evening itself) and has a test of its own.

## With US-102
US-102 is merged below this branch. Its check ("no stock has the session's bar yet") returns
before the stocks' bars are loaded: a check counts nothing and releases nothing, and this
story does not touch the check path. With US-102 a stock asked before any stock has answered
with the session's bar is asked in "check" mode: a history that disagrees is not fetched
again whole on that try. One test here puts its re-based stock last in the list for that
reason.

## Not done, and limits
- A silent stock still costs the round its four tries on the evenings before it is dormant.
- A stock with no stored daily bar at all is never dormant (there is no last bar to count
  from). If its answer is empty every evening it keeps the round waiting, as before.
- The day to arrive is for the bar of the last session counted. A stock that trades again
  on the very evening it is released, its bar coming after that round's first try, stays
  released; its bars are read from the next evening on ("daily bars are back").
- The sessions an exit is held are counted from the session that first held it, also when
  the hold was broken in between; the evening rounds are counted in a row.
- No message to the phone on a release.
