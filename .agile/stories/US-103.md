# US-103: a wide-list stock that stops trading never holds a place for good

Lead, 2026-11-25, on review finding F4 of US-099 (left open there): a stock that stops trading
(a suspension, a merger, a delisting) or stays stale gets no new daily bar, so its open paper
trade is never closed, it holds one of the ten places within the cap for good, and every
evening round uses all four tries waiting for it. A trade "held for review" after an unadjusted
corporate action likewise keeps its place with no release.

Branch `story/US-103-wide-no-data`, on `sprint-04-alpha-and-watchdog` (95672be when this was
written: US-102 and US-104 merged below it).

## What is built
1. MISSING SESSIONS. For a stock that has daily bars but none of the round's session, the
   round reads from the stored bars how many MARKET SESSIONS have passed since its last bar.
   A market session is a trading day of the app's calendar on which at least half of the
   list's other stocks did get a bar. Not counted: a holiday the calendar does not list (no
   stock has a bar), a special session off the calendar (a Muhurat, a Budget weekend) and an
   evening on which the bars of a few stocks only came. A stock not listed yet is not one of
   "the others".
2. WHEN. Two evening rounds (`CONFIRM_ROUNDS`) must each find the stock with `dormant_after`
   missing sessions or more, and the round of the next session releases it if it still has no
   bar. With the desk running every evening and the default of 10: the rounds of the 10th and
   the 11th session count, the round of the 12th releases.
   - An evening counts once, when its session is a market session and the missing sessions it
     finds have reached `dormant_after`. An evening that finds fewer confirms nothing.
   - The release falls on any try of that later round, whether or not the try has half of
     the bars yet, and before that try's signals are admitted.
   - Only a stock the try asked the broker for is counted or released (an answer without the
     bar and a failed request both count as asked; a round stopped at 09:00 says nothing of
     the stocks it never reached). Without a broker the bars in memory of a replay are every
     stock's answer; the stored bars alone are nobody's.
   - The counts live in the stock's state row (`wide_state.reading.quiet`) and start again
     when a new bar is stored.
3. DORMANT. The stock is marked in its state row (`reading.dormant`: since which session, its
   last bar, both counts):
   - an OPEN or EXIT PENDING trade is closed at the stock's last stored close, booked as a sale
     is (both legs' charges, the depository charge, no slippage), status CLOSED, exit reason
     `NO_DATA`; an exit already signalled keeps its signal date and the note says it was never
     filled; the trade is first put on the basis of the bars it is closed on (`_rebase`);
   - a PENDING ENTRY is cancelled: status `CANCELLED`, never filled, never a trade;
   - the place within the cap is free before that try's own signals are admitted;
   - the round stops waiting for the stock: its missing bar no longer keeps the round open
     for four tries. The stock is still asked for on every try, and read again when its bars
     return ("daily bars are back" in the round's line); a released trade stays released.
4. HELD FOR REVIEW. An exit held for review (an unadjusted action) is released on the same
   count of the sessions it is held: two rounds that each see it held for `dormant_after`
   sessions or more, then the next round, on the newest session that round reads of the stock.
   Exit reason `REVIEW`, and NO money is booked: exit price, gross, charges, depository charge,
   net and net % stay empty. The note says the result is not booked because the price basis is
   in doubt. The stock is not dormant (it trades).
5. THE SCORE keeps both apart: its two rows count the rule's own exits only. Each tier also
   answers `released` (how many, how many of each reason, how many with a booked result, and
   the winners and the net of those) and `cancelled`.
6. THE FETCH now starts at least 10 days before the stock's last stored bar (it was always
   45 days back from the session). After a silence longer than 45 days the overlap check still
   has shared days and no hole is left in the history.
7. THE PAGE (the Wide list block, Home and Live Desk): the round's line names the trades
   "released without the rule's exit", the stocks "dormant, not waited for" and those whose
   "daily bars are back"; a line lists up to ten stocks with "No daily price for 10 trading
   days or more" and their last bar, then "and N more"; a line under the score counts what
   is "Not in the score"; a closed row says "no data" or "held for review" in words with the
   trade's note, "result not booked" where a REVIEW release has no net, and "entry cancelled:
   no data". The closed trades are listed by the session they ended on, newest first.

## Decisions, and why
- Sessions from the stored bars, as the brief says, confirmed by evenings of the desk's own.
  Counting from the bars alone failed this case: the desk is off for two weeks, and on its
  first evening back one request fails. The others then have all those sessions' bars and
  the one stock looks ten sessions silent: its trade would be released at a two-week-old
  price for one failed request. Counting evenings alone was safe there but slow on a desk
  that does not run every evening. Both together: ten sessions in the bars, found so on two
  evenings, and still so on a third.
- An evening confirms only once the count is reached (lead's decision after the review).
  Otherwise one failed request on the last evening before a long break would be the first
  of the two, and the second evening back would release at a weeks-old close.
- The 12th session's round, not the 11th (lead left the choice). Three evenings that each
  see ten sessions or more is the rule the story set out; a false release of a trade that is
  alive costs more than a dead stock holding its place one more evening.
- The evening after, not the evening itself. On the evening the counts are met the stock's
  bar may be merely late; waiting for the round's fourth try would never release on a desk
  that is shut early. So the release waits for the next session's round and needs no
  particular try.
- The release does not ask whether that try has half of the bars (lead's decision). The
  counts were met on earlier evenings; if the release waited for a later try, a signal read
  on the first try would be recorded only and the freed place would stay empty.
- At least half of the other stocks, not "any other stock": one stray bar on a holiday must
  not make a session, and when the broker's data is broadly broken nothing may be counted.
- NO_DATA: the price is the last stored close, without slippage (no sale happens), but with
  a sale's charges, so "net" means the same as on every other closed trade. The exit session
  is the round's session (when the place became free); the note names the last bar.
- REVIEW books no money (lead's decision). A 1:2 split served unadjusted would otherwise be
  booked as a loss of half the trade, a figure the code knows may be wrong. Dividing by the
  detected jump is not done either: a real crash would then be hidden.
- A cancelled entry gets its own status, as the desk 18's book has MISSED: it never was a
  trade and must not count as one.
- No new table and no new column: the counts and the mark are in `wide_state.reading`
  (JSON). A store that already has the US-099 tables needs no change.
- Why 10: in the study file (222 stocks, 16 years) the longest hole in one stock's bars is
  5 market sessions, and one stock (RELINFRA, Dec 2025 to Aug 2026) had a bar only once a
  week. At 10 no stock of the file is ever dormant. A value of 1 to 4 is accepted with a
  warning in the log at the desk's start.
- `dormant_after: 0` turns off dormancy and the REVIEW release only. The wider fetch window
  stays: it is the better behaviour with or without dormancy.
- Nothing is sent to the phone on a release (the brief asks for the round's note and the
  block). If the owner bought the shares by hand, the page is where they learn of it.

## Tests (`tests/delivery/test_wide_dormant.py`, 44; no existing test changed)
The tests run each evening through its tries, as the desk does, and none reads the real
clock. With `dormant_after: 3` a stock is released on the fifth evening.
- a stock stops trading with an open trade within the cap: waited for while the counts run,
  closed at the fifth evening's first try, the place goes to that evening's new signal, an
  old recorded trade never moves up, the mark, the view and the score apart; the same
  without a broker; the release on a first try with under half of the bars in still frees
  the place for that try's signal; a release with a gain is a winner among the released;
- a pending entry: cancelled, never filled, also when the bars return, and listed among the
  trades that are over; a filled trade is never cancelled as an entry;
- a holiday the calendar does not list, a listed holiday with a special session, a Saturday
  session, and an evening on which under half of the others got a bar: not counted, and the
  round of such a day is not one of the two that confirm; the share at its edge (2 of 4
  counts; 1 of 3 and 2 of 5 do not); a stock not listed yet does not lower the share;
- a stock that trades every third session, and one whose bar always comes a day late: never
  dormant; a bar that is merely late on an evening that counts: nothing released; a bar
  that comes late on the release evening itself: the release stands;
- one failed evening before a break and two after it: nothing released; a desk that runs
  one evening in three still releases a dead stock; a desk shut after one try each evening
  still releases; a stock the try never asked for (a round stopped at 09:00) is neither
  counted nor released;
- a stale stock with an open trade: released at the last stored close on its entry's basis,
  read again once its refetch works; a history re-based just before the silence: the trade
  is released on the new basis;
- the round's tries: four while the stock is not dormant, one once it is, the stock still
  asked for; bars that return after 60 sessions of failed requests: fetched from ten days
  before the last stored bar, no hole, the stock read again, its next signal a new trade on
  time; a dormant stock whose bars return a day late wakes all the same;
- an exit signalled and never filled; an exit held for review: released as REVIEW with no
  figure written, the route's answer plain JSON, the page saying "result not booked"; the
  count of held sessions; never in a hurry after time off; on the newest session read; also
  for a stock read a day late;
- the trades that are over are listed by the session they ended on: a release after twenty
  later closes is first in the list and on the page;
- a round run again, a restart in the middle of a count and after the release; the switch
  off (`dormant_after: 0`), also after a stock was marked; one stock alone is never dormant;
  a dormant stock whose bars cannot be read one evening keeps its mark;
- the setting read strictly, the warning under 5; the page (run in node), ten names and
  "and N more", and the route.
Each guard in the new code was broken on purpose (42 ways, the nine the lead's mutation
review found surviving among them): each time a test failed.

  a stock read a day late; a stock could turn dormant on a first try while its bar was
  merely late; the count's unit; three type errors; a mark lost on a failed load.
  Round 2: APPROVE.
- The lead's three reviews (rule and money, tests by mutation, callers and page), all made:
  the release no longer depends on the try's share of bars; a REVIEW release books no money;
  an evening confirms only at the count; only a stock that was asked is counted or released;
  the closed list is by exit session; the check script keeps releases out of the rule's
  figures and names a cancelled entry; the tests the mutation review asked for; the plain
  words on the page and in the owner's note; the schema comments; the warning under 5.

## With US-102
US-102 is merged below this branch. Its check ("no stock has the session's bar yet") returns
before the stocks' bars are loaded: a check counts nothing and releases nothing, and this
story does not touch the check path (it only records which stocks a try asked for). With
US-102 a stock asked before any stock has answered with the session's bar is asked in "check"
mode: a history that disagrees is not fetched again whole on that try. One test here puts
its re-based stock last in the list for that reason.

## Not done, and limits
- A silent stock still costs the round its four tries on the evenings before it is dormant.
- A stock with no stored daily bar at all is never dormant (there is no last bar to count
  from). If its answer is empty every evening it keeps the round waiting, as before.
- No message to the phone on a release.
- A stock that trades again on the very evening it is released, its bar coming after that
  try, stays released; its bars are read from the next evening on ("daily bars are back").
- The sessions an exit is held are counted from the session that first held it, also when
  the hold was broken in between.
- A stock taken off `config/delivery_universe.yaml` while it has an active wide trade still
  holds its place (the round skips stocks not on the list): for the backlog.
- `scripts/pullback_book_product.py` needed no change: it writes each trade's status and
  exit reason, and a release frees its place on its exit session, as its reader counts.
