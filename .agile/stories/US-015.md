# US-015 — Pre-registered promotion gate: live-grade vs experimental

## Story
As the owner, I want every strategy labelled live-grade or experimental by numbers fixed in advance, so that the system cannot talk me into trusting a strategy that has not earned it.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given config/promotion.yaml with the thresholds written and committed before results are viewed (minimum 30 live-grade paper trades, profit factor at least 1.2 net of costs, positive expectancy lower bound, out-of-sample walk-forward pass), then a strategy is live-grade only when all hold, otherwise experimental.
- AC2: Given an alert, then its card and Telegram message show the strategy grade; experimental alerts are still sent when enabled but clearly marked.
- AC3: Given a live-grade strategy whose rolling paper record falls below the demotion threshold, then it is demoted automatically and the owner is told; thresholds can only be changed through a recorded change (no silent edits).
