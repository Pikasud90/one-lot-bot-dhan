# US-016 — Paper book view: positions, ledger and outcome of every alert

## Story
As the owner, I want a clear Paper view showing open paper trades, closed trades, the equity curve and the strategy table, so that I can judge the system at a glance.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given the Paper view, then it shows open paper trades with live P&L and exit advice, closed trades with reason and costs, the equity and drawdown chart, and the per-strategy table from US-011; delayed-grade trades are visually distinct.
- AC2: Given an alert card on Home, then it links to its paper trade and shows the paper outcome once closed.
- AC3: Given the page at 1280 and 390 pixel widths, then no horizontal scroll appears and every table is usable.
