# US-098: real put prices, and the long-volatility question settled (study)

Read `.agile/stories/RESEARCH-RULES.md` first (how to run, periods, costs, the luck rule, no
look-ahead, writing). Owner, 2026-11-24: "start on both options [...] try finding the alpha
now with maximum focus on something that works": one of the two was a long-volatility idea
for options, built on the one thing that can be told in advance, the SIZE of a move.

Where it stands (`docs/research/OPTION_SETUPS_2026-11-24.md`, setup S3 and its "Independent
check"): buying the at-the-money call AND put at 09:20 and selling both at 15:20 earned on no
kind of day, but the store holds real PUT prices for only 12 days, so the put was priced from
the real call by put-call parity with an assumed dividend yield, and that estimate was off by
a median of 13%. The verifier's verdict: the size of the loss is not shown; zero is possible.
This story gets the real puts and settles it.

- What: one-minute bars of the near-month AT-THE-MONEY PUT (and, if one request gives it at
  no extra cost, the strikes one step either side) of NIFTY and BANKNIFTY for every session
  from 2021-11-08 to 2026-11-07, and the at-the-money CALL for any session the store lacks.
- How: through the app's own adapter and its own expired-option reader, built the way the app
  builds them (`DhanAdapter.rolling_option`, `src/onelot/data/expired_options.py`,

  filled `data/expired_options.db` did it). Known quirks, from the first use: the endpoint
  accepts ONLY a one-day window (from, from + 1 day) and returns both days; expiry code 0 is
  refused (1 is the near one); Dhan's own `iv` field is not usable.
- Where: your OWN file `data/research/real_puts_2026-11-25/options.sqlite` in the main
  checkout's git-ignored research folder, in the SAME schema as `data/expired_options.db`
  (use the app's store class on your path), so that it can be merged later. NEVER write to
  `data/expired_options.db` or `data/onelot.db`.
- When: the owner's live desk uses the same data plan and the market is open until 15:30
  IST. Before 15:40 IST you may send at most FIVE requests in all (to prove the fetcher on
  two or three days). The bulk fetch starts after 15:40 IST, at most ONE request a second,
  resumable (a done-table of fetched days: never fetch a day twice), started with nohup and
  a done-marker file. While you wait for 15:40 build and test everything else on the 12 days
  the store has. If Dhan refuses three times in a row (token expired, rate limit,
  entitlement), stop asking, keep what you have, and say so: the token on this desk expires
  at about 21:30 IST today, so the fetch must be finished or paused before then.
- Never open `.env` or `data/api_token.txt` yourself, never print a settings object or a
  token; never call port 8787.
- Check what came back: days asked, days with bars, minutes per day, strikes against the
  index at 09:20 (is the strike really at the money?), bad prints (the study's filter), and
  put-call parity on the days and minutes where both legs are real.

## Part 2: the question, fixed before any result
Same definitions as S3 in `OPTION_SETUPS_2026-11-24.md` (entries 09:20 and 09:45; exits 15:20,
12:00, and the -30% / +60% rule; day types ALL, ACTIVE-EXPECTED, CALM-EXPECTED, NR7, VIX-UP,
VIX-DOWN, EXPIRY, PRE-EXPIRY), NIFTY and BANKNIFTY each and the pool of two, BOTH LEGS REAL
(a day without both real legs at entry and exit is left out and counted). Costs as there for
two lots. Report per cell: days, % a pair (t by day), winners, TUNE / CHOOSE / TEST; the
selection rule of RESEARCH-RULES section 5; beside each the earlier parity-put figure.
Then the plain answers:
1. Did the buyer of both options pay, on all days and on each kind of day? By how much, with
   the interval. Is any kind of day positive beyond luck?
2. How far was the parity put from the real put (median and spread), and did it bias the
   earlier answer?
3. One step further, only if 1 is "paid on all days": the same for the at-the-money call
   alone and put alone held 09:20 to 15:20 (what a one-lot buyer without a view pays).
