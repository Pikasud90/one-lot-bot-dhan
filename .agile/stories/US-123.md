# US-123 — The confidence badge says no evidence yet when its parts are all defaults

## Story
As the owner, I want the badge to say plainly when it has nothing to go on, so that 53 medium is not read as a measured chance

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given an alert whose backtest part and live record part are both the neutral default and whose strength is the fixed value of its rule, when the badge is shown on the page and on the phone, then it reads no evidence yet and shows no number
- AC2: Given an alert with a saved backtest of 40 trades or more, or 5 or more archived alerts, when the badge is shown, then it shows the number and the band as today
- AC3: Given the cheat sheet of Home, when it is read, then it explains both cases in the words on the page
