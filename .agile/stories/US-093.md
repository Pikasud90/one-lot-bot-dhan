Read `.agile/stories/RESEARCH-RULES.md` first. Owner, 2026-11-24: the pullback rule keeps its
own exits and about Rs 10,000 a trade; "start on both options, try finding the alpha now with
maximum focus on something that works". The pullback rule is the only positive result so
far: +1.21% a trade on the 18 desk stocks and, unchanged, +0.78% a trade (t 2.7) on 43 other
stocks (`docs/research/ALPHA_SEARCH_2026-11-12.md` section 5; plan in `ALPHA_PLAN_2026-11-12.md`
section A4; scripts `scripts/alpha_*.py`; the rule in the product: `src/onelot/delivery/` and
`src/onelot/strategies/`; reference fixtures `tests/fixtures/pullback/`). It trades rarely on
18 stocks. This story asks: does it hold on a wide universe, and do three other well-known
daily setups hold there, as delivery trades of about Rs 10,000, long only, after costs?

## The setups, fixed here before any result (entry at the next session's open)
P1. The pullback rule exactly as the product has it, own exits. Reported for: the 18 desk
    stocks (known), the 43 stocks of the alpha study (known), and the NEW stocks only (the
    clean test: no one has looked at them). The 10% depth variant of the alpha study as P1b.
M1. New 52-week high: the close is the highest of 252 sessions for the first time in 20
    sessions, the day's volume is at least 1.5 times its 50-session average, NIFTY's close is
    above its 200-session average. Exits tested: (a) close below the 50-session average or
    60 sessions, whichever first; (b) the desk's ratchet on daily bars (first stop 2 x
    ATR(14) daily, target 3 risks, extend on).
M2. Gap up on volume (a stand-in for good news; earnings dates are not stored): the open is
    at least 3% above the previous high, the day's volume at least 3 times its 50-session
    average, the close in the upper half of the day's range. Exits: (a) 20 sessions; (b) 40
    sessions; both also end on a close below the gap day's low.
M3. Strongest stocks: on the first session of each month, the 10% of the universe with the
    highest return from 252 to 21 sessions ago, only while NIFTY is above its 200-session
    average; hold to the next month's first session. (Survivorship flatters this one most:
    lean on the excess and the placebo.)
R1. Sharp dip in an uptrend: RSI(2) below 10 with the close above its 200-session average;
    exit at the first close above the 5-session average or after 7 sessions.
Each setup is one cell on the pool of all stocks, and is also reported by group: desk 18,
alpha 43, new names; by calendar year; by NIFTY above or below its 200-session average at
entry; by the stock's size bucket (three buckets by median rupee turnover).
Per-stock or per-sector settings are NOT selected here (too few trades a stock); if a setup
passes on the pool, list the stocks and sectors where it was strongest as a remark only.

Costs: about Rs 10,000 a trade (whole shares: the count that keeps the amount at or just
under Rs 10,000, at least one share; say how many stocks cost more than Rs 10,000 a share),
the delivery cost model of the pullback study, slippage as there. The t treats one entry
week as one draw. Selection: RESEARCH-RULES section 5 with the delivery thresholds.
Also report for each PASSED setup what the owner would live with: signals a month, how many
trades are open at once (median and worst), the longest losing run, the worst month of a
book that takes every signal at Rs 10,000.
