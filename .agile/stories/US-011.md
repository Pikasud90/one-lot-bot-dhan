# US-011 — Paper analytics: evidence per strategy, instrument and confidence

## Story
As the owner, I want live paper statistics for each strategy, instrument, timeframe and confidence bucket, so that I know which alerts to trust and the confidence score can be calibrated.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given closed paper trades, then GET /api/paper/stats returns per group: n, win rate, average R, expectancy in rupees, profit factor net of costs, max drawdown in paise and a 95 percent bootstrap interval for expectancy; groups with n below 30 are labelled insufficient evidence.
- AC2: Given grade delayed and grade live trades, then delayed trades are excluded from statistics by default and included only when requested.
- AC3: Given confidence buckets (0-40, 40-65, 65-100), then a calibration table shows realised win rate and average R per bucket, and the endpoint states plainly when the buckets are not monotonic.
- AC4: Given the equity curve request, then it is the cumulative net_paise sequence ordered by close time, equal to the ledger total at its end.
