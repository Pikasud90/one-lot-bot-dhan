# SP-008: Study: is there any buy-only use of the next move's size

## Story
As the owner, I want it settled whether the forecast size of the next move can be used by a buy-
only, intraday, one-lot option rule, or that nothing is left to use, so that the one thing that can
be told in advance is either used or put aside with a reason.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given real option bars of NIFTY and BANKNIFTY, when part A runs, then it prints for 18 pre-
  registered cells the mean ratio of the realised move to the move the option's own IV charges, with
  and without each size predictor.
- AC2: Given part A, when the gate is applied, then part B runs only for the predictors that meet
  the gate written in the story, and the report says which did not and why.
- AC3: Given part B cells, when the report is written, then each shows trades, % a trade after real
  costs, t, win share and TUNE / CHOOSE / TEST.
- AC4: Given predictor labels shuffled over days inside index and year, when part A runs 20 times,
  then the report prints how many gates luck alone passes.
- AC5: Given a part A that passes nothing, when the report is written, then it says plainly that no
  use is left and shows the decomposition of why.

## Non-functional requirements
- Reproducible: seeded placebo; spike scores computed by `src/onelot/volume/indicator.py`
  loaded by file path (never put the worktree's `src` on the path), cached on disk.
- Stores read only (`mode=ro`); no request to Dhan, none to the live desk, no order. Use
  `nice -n 10` and at most 4 workers.
- Plain words, no em or en dashes, prose lines at most 100 characters, note at most 180 lines.

## Public contract
- Script `scripts/size_use_study.py` with stages `ratio` (part A), `gate`, `pair` (part B),
  `report`. Output `docs/research/SIZE_USE_<DATE>.md` and `cells.csv`.
- Part A, per entry minute t and exit minute u: charged move = ATM implied volatility at t
  (from the real call's bar, `onelot.lab.real_options.implied_days`) x sqrt(time from t to u as
  a fraction of a trading year, in session minutes); realised move = |index(u) / index(t) - 1|.
  `R = realised / charged`. Cell mean of R with the predictor true and false, difference, day t.
- Predictors, fixed here: P1 the previous session was ACTIVE (the setup grid's definition);
  P2 the previous session's India VIX close is in the top third of its trailing 250 sessions;
  P3 a market-wide volume spike (Volume Watch score of 2.5 or more, `bvc` rule) in the 15
  minutes before t, first spike of the day only. Windows: 09:20 to 15:20 and 09:45 to 12:00
  for P1 and P2; 30 and 60 minutes after the spike for P3.

## Notes & assumptions

Where the need was found: `docs/SETUP_BOOK.md` section 2 ("the SIZE of the next move can be
told: an active session tends to follow an active one, 57% against 50%; a high India VIX means
a larger next day; after a market-wide volume spike the next 5 to 60 minutes move about 1.5
times as much"; "the direction cannot be told"), `docs/research/OPTION_SETUPS_2026-11-24.md`
(the buyer of volatility earned on no day type, and the straddle result correlates -0.03 with
the previous VIX level) and `docs/research/VOLUME_STUDY_2026-11-24.md` ("nothing here is
tradable for an option buyer").

State plainly: long volatility ALREADY FAILED. Bought at 09:20 and sold at 15:20 on real prices
the pair lost 2.61% a day (-3.59 to -1.63), on every kind of day, negative in all three
periods; the call alone 3.1% and the put alone 2.2%. A size forecast did not help it. The
likely reason is that the premium already charges the size: a high VIX raises the premium as
it raises the move. So this study does NOT repeat the straddle on day types. It asks what is
left: does any size signal predict a realised move that is larger than the one the option's
own implied volatility charges? If none does, the answer is that there is no buy-only use of
size, and the note says why in numbers.

Pre-registration (fixed before any result; RESEARCH-RULES.md; US-098 and US-092 as method):
- Question: after the premium charges for the expected size, does any size predictor known
  before entry still tell a larger-than-charged move, and can a buy-only intraday one-lot rule
  collect it after real costs?
- Data: NIFTY and BANKNIFTY only (the real puts are theirs: `scripts/merge_option_bars.py`
  merged 21,279 day blocks), sessions 2021-11-08 to 2026-11-07 from `data/expired_options.db`
  and the one-minute bars of `data/onelot.db`; the 18 stocks' volume since November 2021 for
  the spike score. FINNIFTY and SENSEX wait for SP-007. MIDCPNIFTY is apart.
- Part A cells: 18 = 3 predictors x 2 windows x (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, pool). Examined: 18. No
  trade is taken in part A; it only measures R with the predictor true against false.
- Gate from A to B, fixed now: a predictor goes to part B when, in the pool, the mean R is at
  least 0.10 higher with the predictor true (the premium is under-charging size by 10 points of
  the charged move), day t of 2.5 or more in TUNE, and the same sign with t of 1.0 or more in
  CHOOSE, and the same sign in TEST. A predictor that misses is named, with its numbers.
- Part B (only for predictors past the gate): the BUY-only use that needs no direction, the
  at-the-money call and put of one strike bought at t and sold at u, each leg one lot, both
  legs real prints (US-098's pricing). At most 6 cells (the passing predictors x windows,
  pooled). The pair is two positions: a question for the owner (the one-lot rule). Directional
  uses of size are NOT retried: S1 with the size filter is AVOID, S6 is REJECTED.
- Split: rule 2.
- Costs: real prints of both legs, the live measured half spread (0.25% NIFTY, 0.3% BANKNIFTY)
  on each of four leg-sides, charges from `config/costs.yaml`. Benchmark: zero after costs, and
  the same pair on the predictor-false days (the predictor must add at least +1.0% over it, t
  of 2 or more). Holding NIFTY is no benchmark for a same-day trade.
- Luck check: shuffle the predictor labels over the DAYS inside index and year, 20 shuffles,
  same selection; print how many gates luck alone passes beside the real number, plus the
  arithmetic (18 x 0.6% x 16% x 50% for rule 5 in part B).
- Verdict words: NO EDGE (part A passes nothing, or part B discovers nothing: "no buy-only use
  of size"), WATCH (part B positive in all three periods but short of a threshold), AVOID (a
  part B cell with TUNE t of -2.5 or less and negative in CHOOSE and TEST), PASS (rule 5
  discovered, confirmed and passed on real prints; not expected).
- Stops early: if part A passes no predictor, stop; write that no use is left and show the
  decomposition (the mean charged move, the mean realised move and R with and without each
  predictor). That is a full answer.
- No look-ahead (rule 6): P1 and P2 use the previous session only; P3 uses bars closed before
  t; truncation test on at least 50 random entries per predictor.

Not in scope: selling options, spreads or any short position (owner: options are bought,
never sold); overnight; FINNIFTY and SENSEX (SP-007); weekly contracts; a direction rule.
Open question for the owner: if part B ever passes, the pair is two lots of one: is that
inside his one-lot rule?
