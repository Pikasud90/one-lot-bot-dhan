# SP-003: Study: follow the four WATCH option cells on paper with real quotes

## Story
As the owner, I want the four WATCH option cells followed on paper for three months with real quotes
recorded each day, so that what was only a model result is judged on what the market really quoted.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given the protocol, when it is committed before the first record, then it lists every field
  recorded per session and cell.
- AC2: Given three months of records, when the report is written, then it lists every session of the
  cell, with and without a signal.
- AC3: Given the report, then it gives the signals, fills and quoted spread per cell, and no t statistic
  on fewer than 20 trades.
- AC4: Given the report, then the verdict is WATCH or NO EDGE, never PASS, with the arithmetic that
  says why.
- AC5: Given 30 recorded entry quotes of a SENSEX cell, when the cost rule is applied, then the
  report prints the cell's live-cost margin and says NO EDGE when it is zero or less.

## Non-functional requirements
- Reproducible: one script, same table in, same report out, byte for byte.
- Read only on the store (SQLite `mode=ro`); nothing here places an order. The recording raises
  no alert, no Telegram message and no paper trade (the invariants scan stays green).
- Private tables (`desk_positions`, `positions`, `trade_journal`, `account_cache`,
  `app_settings`) are never read by the report.
- Plain words, no em or en dashes, prose lines at most 100 characters, note at most 180 lines.

## Public contract
The recorder is a separate build story the lead writes; the names below are the contract the
tests of this study aim at, with real names from the code where they exist.
- Table `watch_follow` (proposed), one row per session and cell, written whether or not the
  signal came: `session` (ISO date), `cell` (W1 to W4), `instrument`, `desk_up` (1 when
  `desk_uptime` covers at least 90% of the session, else 0), `condition_value` (VIX over its
  60-session median, or the previous session's range over its 20-session median),
  `condition_true`, `signal` (0 or 1), `signal_ts`, `side`, and for a signal the contract the
  tradeable pick names (`onelot.desk.option_pick.choose`): `label`, `security_id`, `strike`,
  `expiry`, then bid, ask, ltp, oi, iv, delta at the signal minute and at +1, +2 and +5 minutes
  (the `fill_quotes` kinds ALERT and FOLLOW), the exit minute and reason of the cell's own
  rule, the exit bid, ask and ltp, `model_entry` and `model_exit` (the harness price of the
  same trade, `h.pnl`), `real_pct`, `model_pct`.
- Cells: W1 NIFTY `cpr_breakout` low, 60m, after an active session. W2 SENSEX
  `supertrend_mtf` default, 30m, VIX below its median. W3 SENSEX `supertrend_mtf` default,
  30m, after a calm session. W4 SENSEX `ema_cross` high, 30m, VIX below its median.
- Report: `scripts/watch_follow_report.py --from DATE --to DATE` writes
  `docs/research/WATCH_FOLLOW_<DATE>.md` and `data/research/watch_follow_<DATE>/cells.csv`.
- Protocol note, committed before the first record: `docs/research/WATCH_FOLLOW_PROTOCOL.md`.

## Notes & assumptions

Where the need was found: `docs/SETUP_BOOK.md` section 6 item 3 ("the four WATCH option cells,
followed on paper with real quotes for three months") and the four WATCH rows of section 4
(`docs/research/SETUP_GRID_2026-11-24.md`). Today the desk runs only `microtrend` and
`pullback`, so nothing records those four cells, and their numbers are model premiums.

What exists today: a model result per cell (W1 +3.75 / +1.20 / +2.00, W2 +3.26 / +1.05 / +1.13,
W3 +2.86 / +2.01 / +3.72, W4 +2.93 / +4.37 / +1.38), 20, 36, 38 and 19 trades a year, no real
premium reading, and live half spreads of 0.25% on NIFTY and 1.5% on SENSEX against the model's
0.6% (`docs/research/LIVE_ALERTS_2026-11-24.md`, US-097). What is missing: the signals as they
would have been raised, and the quotes at the minute of each.

Pre-registration (fixed before any record is read; RESEARCH-RULES.md sections 2, 3, 5, 6):
- Question: when the four WATCH cells are followed live for 60 observed sessions, what do the
  real quotes say about their costs, and is anything left of their model margin?
- Data: the table `watch_follow` of the live store, from the first session with `desk_up` 1
  after the recorder is merged, for 60 OBSERVED sessions (`desk_up` 1). Unobserved sessions are
  listed and do not count. Part 0 (no new data): for the past trades of the four cells in
  `data/research/setup_grid_2026-11-24/`, the share priced by `data/expired_options.db`
  (`mode=ro`), reported per cell; a share under 30% gives no verdict (rule 3).
- Cells: 4 (W1 to W4), fixed now; no cell is added during the follow. Examined: 4.
- Split: TUNE / CHOOSE / TEST are the history's (to 7 Nov 2024, to 7 Nov 2025, to 7 Nov 2026),
  copied from the book. The follow is a fourth period named FOLLOW, from the first record,
  never merged into TEST.
- Costs: entry at the ASK of the named contract at the open of the minute after the signal bar
  closes; exit at the BID; charges from `config/costs.yaml` (index options). Also reported: the
  model's price of the same trade, and the measured half spread at entry against the model's
  0.6%. The yardstick is zero after real costs, plus the cell's own history; holding NIFTY is
  no benchmark for a same-day option trade.
- Sample arithmetic, in advance: 20, 36, 38 and 19 trades a year give about 4.8, 8.6, 9.1 and
  4.6 trades in 60 sessions (0.24 of a year). Rule 5 asks 20 trades for a TEST reading, so no
  cell can PASS in this window: W2 and W3 need about 7 months, W1 and W4 about 12 months. The
  report prints no t on fewer than 20 trades, and says it follows on month by month.
- Yardstick for costs, computable from quotes alone: margin = the lowest of the cell's three
  period means; live-cost margin = margin minus 2 x (median measured half spread at entry
  minus 0.6) in points. A cell whose live-cost margin is zero or less, on at least 30 recorded
  entry quotes, is NO EDGE by arithmetic.
- Luck check: record the signal on EVERY session, also when the condition is false; the report
  compares the cell's FOLLOW trades with the same rule's trades on condition-false sessions
  (what the condition adds), and with 20 shuffles of the condition label over the observed
  days. It states how many of the 4 cells are positive and what chance gives: all 4 positive is
  6% by luck, 3 or more 31%.
- Verdict words: WATCH (the default, and the only positive word this window can give) and NO
  EDGE (the cost rule above, or a real mean below zero on at least 20 trades). Not PASS, not
  AVOID (rule 5 needs TUNE).
- Stops early: (a) a recording fault, quotes missing (no bid or ask) on over 30% of signals:
  stop, report the fault, fix, restart the 60 sessions; (b) the cost rule can end a SENSEX
  cell at 30 entry quotes, without waiting for trades.
- No look-ahead (rule 6): the condition uses the previous session's close and the 60-session
  median up to the previous session; the signal is written at its bar's close. Prove it by a
  truncation test on at least 50 recorded signals.

Not in scope: building the recorder; alerts or paper trades for these cells; any real money;
FINNIFTY and BANKNIFTY WATCH-like rows (there are none). Adani Green is excluded.
Open question for the owner: may the four cells be recorded silently (no alert, no score), as
shadow slots? The standing rule pauses every slot but the pullback and the micro-trend rule.
