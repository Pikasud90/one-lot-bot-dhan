# US-012 — Paper vs taken reconciliation and slippage report

## Story
As the owner, I want to compare what I actually got against the paper fill, so that I can see the real cost of acting by hand.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given an alert I marked Taken with my own fill, then the app shows my fill, the paper fill, the difference in rupees and in percent, and the elapsed seconds between alert and my confirmation.
- AC2: Given at least 10 taken alerts, then a summary shows average slippage versus paper by strategy and a warning when it exceeds the paper model assumption.
