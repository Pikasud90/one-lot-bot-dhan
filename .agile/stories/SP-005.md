# SP-005 — Study: how many micro-trend alerts before anything can be said

## Story
As the owner, I want the number of live micro-trend alerts needed before a verdict, and the gate
that decides it, fixed before the paper record is read, so that a few lucky weeks cannot talk me
into the rule.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given the 3,065 history trades of the rule on the four indices, when part 1 runs, then it
  prints the spread of one entry day and the entry days needed to see a true +0.5, +1, +2 and +3
  points at t 2, with the calendar months each needs at the history's alert rate.
- AC2: Given part 1, when the note is committed, then the gate (counting rule, minimum entry days,
  verdict words and their numbers) is in the repository before any live trade after the restart date
  is read.
- AC3: Given the paper record after the restart date, when the monthly reading runs, then it counts
  only live-grade, clean-bar, non-void trades and lists every excluded one with its reason.
- AC4: Given fewer entry days than the gate's minimum, when the reading runs, then it prints WATCH
  and prints no t.
- AC5: Given the live signals and a replay of the rule on the stored bars of the same sessions, when
  the reading runs, then it prints the share of live signals the replay also raised and the share it
  did not.

## Non-functional requirements
- Reproducible: part 1 is a pure computation on the history file, seeded where it samples.
- Read only (`mode=ro`) on `paper_trades`, `paper_void`, `desk_uptime`, `desk_signals`; no
  request to the live desk, no order. Never reads `trade_journal` or `desk_positions`.
- Plain words, no em or en dashes, prose lines at most 100 characters, note at most 180 lines.

## Public contract
- Script `scripts/microtrend_gate.py` with stages `needed` and `read`.
  `needed` writes `docs/research/MICROTREND_GATE_<DATE>.md` (the table below, and the gate).
  `read --month YYYY-MM` writes `docs/research/MICROTREND_LIVE_<YYYY-MM>.md`.
- `needed` table: columns `true_mean_points` (0.5, 1, 2, 3), `entry_days_needed`,
  `sessions_needed`, `calendar_months_at_history_rate`, `calendar_months_if_desk_up_80pct`.
  Formula: entry days = (2 x s / true mean) squared, s the standard deviation of the
  day-summed result, from the 3,065 history trades (`microtrend_2026-11-13`).
- `read` keys: `restart_date`, `trades_counted`, `entry_days`, `excluded` (list of trade id and
  reason: delayed grade, unclean bars, `paper_void`, MIDCPNIFTY, before the restart date),
  `pct_trade`, `t_day`, `ci95`, `thirds`, `replay_agree_share`, `placebo_percentile`,
  `verdict`.
- Config: the numbers of the gate go in `config/promotion.yaml` only through US-015, whose AC1
  asks for them to be committed before results are viewed; this study supplies them.

## Notes & assumptions

Where the need was found: `docs/SETUP_BOOK.md` section 5 (the desk's micro-trend slot: "since it
went live the desk ran on 2 of 7 sessions"), `docs/research/LIVE_ALERTS_2026-11-24.md` (live
record: 2 alerts), `.agile/stories/US-104.md` (the dead-contract fix; 3 of the 5 index alerts
named a contract nobody trades), `US-105.md` (those trades are left out of the score), `US-097`
(uptime and clean one-minute bars) and `US-015.md` (the promotion gate, not built: no
`config/promotion.yaml` exists). After the fixes the live record restarts from a date to be
written in the note (the first session the desk runs with US-097, US-104 and US-105 merged).

What exists: history of the rule on the four indices, 3,065 trades, about 613 a year: −0.07%
(t −0.1) on the model, and about −1.3% on real-like premiums, TUNE / CHOOSE / TEST −0.20 /
+0.08 / −1.38 on time. A rough reading of those t values puts the spread of one entry day at
about 14 to 15% of premium. If so, a true +1 point needs about 840 entry days at t 2, +2 points
about 210 and +3 about 94, and the rule has an entry on about 8 of 10 sessions: so a few weeks
of live alerts can say nothing. Part 1 recomputes this properly; do not rely on these figures.

Pre-registration (fixed before any live trade after the restart date is read):
- Question: how many live alerts and entry days are needed before the paper record of the
  micro-trend rule can say anything, and what exactly must it show to be promoted, kept on
  watch or dropped?
- Data: part 1: the 3,065 trades of `data/research/microtrend_2026-11-13/` (four indices;
  MIDCPNIFTY is a training instrument, apart, never counted), TUNE / CHOOSE / TEST by rule 2.
  Part 2: `paper_trades` of strategy `microtrend`, grade live, on or after the restart date,
  minus trades in `paper_void`, minus any session with unclean bars (US-097 item 3).
- Cell: ONE rule on four indices pooled (rule 5: one declared pool). The four indices alone are
  descriptive. No filter is added: the five filters of LIVE_ALERTS part D (H1 to H5) are
  REJECTED or neutral, and no new one may be tried on the live record. Examined: 1.
- Split: history TUNE / CHOOSE / TEST copied from the book; the live record is a fourth period
  LIVE, never merged. Costs: the alert's own bid and ask (entry at the ask, exit at the bid),
  charges from `config/costs.yaml`. Benchmark: zero after real costs, and a no-skill entry
  (same side and exit rule, a random minute of the same session, 200 draws); NIFTY held is no
  benchmark for a same-day option trade.
- The gate (numbers fixed here; a change is a recorded change, as US-015 AC3):
  G0 counting: only live-grade, clean-bar, non-void, four-index trades count; delayed-grade
  trades never count toward promotion (US-015 note).
  G1 no word but WATCH, and no t, before 100 entry days (about the first 6 months at 80% of
  sessions, if the desk is up).
  G2 PASS: at least 210 entry days AND at least 100 trades, mean at least +1.0% of premium (the
  rule 5 size for options) with the 95% day-bootstrap interval above zero, positive in each
  third of the record, above 95% of the no-skill draws, and live signals matched by the replay
  on at least 90% of sessions.
  G3 AVOID: at least 60 entry days, t of −2.5 or less, and the last two thirds each negative.
  G4 NO EDGE: at least 100 entry days and the upper end of the 95% interval below +1.0%.
  Otherwise WATCH.
- Luck check: the live record is one cell, but each monthly reading is a look; the note prints
  the number of looks, and only readings at 6, 12 and 24 months after the restart may print a
  verdict word other than WATCH.
- Stops early: a month with the desk up on under 90% of sessions (`desk_uptime`) is listed and
  excluded; if clean-bar waits stop over 30% of the rule's replay signals, stop and report that
  the live rule is not the tested rule. A replay agreement under 90% voids the reading.
- No look-ahead: the replay uses only bars closed before each signal (truncation test on at
  least 50 live signals).

Not in scope: changing the rule; the stop, target and trail rule (neutral per the book);
building the promotion gate (US-015). Open question for the owner: is a wait of about a year
(210 entry days, enough to see a true +2 points) acceptable before the first PASS? A true +1
point needs about four years, and nothing smaller can be shown.
