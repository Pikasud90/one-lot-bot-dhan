# US-032 — Paper panel shows the last session when nothing closed today

## Story
As the owner reviewing paper results on a weekend or before the first close of the day, I want the Paper panel to show the most recent session's closed trades, so that the evidence is visible without reading the API.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given no paper trade closed today (IST) but closed or no-fill trades exist on an earlier date, when the Live Desk opens, then the panel shows the open trades plus the closed/no-fill trades of the most recent session date that has any, under a visible heading such as 'Last session · Fri 30 Oct', and the day line totals (closed, won, lost, gross, net) are for that session.
- AC2: Given a segmented control 'Today | Last session | All', then the owner can switch views; 'Today' keeps today's behaviour (and falls back to the last session with its heading when today has none), 'Last session' always shows the latest session with closes, 'All' shows every trade the API returned (newest first, at most 200); the choice is remembered per browser (localStorage wrapped in try/catch) and the page works without it.
- AC3: Given the API, then /api/paper adds totals for the chosen day so the day line is exact (e.g. totals_for(date) or a 'last_session' block with its date and totals computed from integer paise on the server); the existing totals_today and fields are unchanged.
