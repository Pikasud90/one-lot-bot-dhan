# SP-007: Study: real puts on FINNIFTY and SENSEX, does long volatility lose there too

## Story
As the owner, I want the long-volatility test repeated on real puts and calls of FINNIFTY and
SENSEX, so that the finding that it loses on NIFTY and BANKNIFTY is checked on the other two indices
I trade.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given the stored N and BN pair results, when step 0 runs, then it prints their mean with zero
  spread, with the model spread and with the live spread, and asks the owner whether to fetch before
  any request is sent.
- AC2: Given an approved fetch, when it runs, then it sends at most 5 requests before 15:40 IST and
  1 a second after, never a day twice, and stops after 3 refusals in a row.
- AC3: Given the fetched bars, when the checks run, then the report prints days asked, days with
  bars, minutes a day, strike against spot at 09:20 and the share of bad prints, per index.
- AC4: Given the 13 pre-registered cells, when the report is written, then each shows trades, % a
  pair, t, win share, TUNE / CHOOSE / TEST and the same cell on live spreads.
- AC5: Given the counts, when the report is written, then it lists cells examined, discovered,
  confirmed, passed and AVOID beside the placebo's and the arithmetic's.

## Non-functional requirements
- Reproducible: the fetcher is resumable, writes a done-table of days, never fetches a day
  twice; analysis seeded; outputs under `data/research/real_puts_fin_sensex_<DATE>/`.
- Never writes `data/expired_options.db` or `data/onelot.db`; never opens `.env` or
  `data/api_token.txt`; never prints a token; never calls port 8787. The only requests sent
  are the data requests of the fetch step, through the app's own adapter.
- Plain words, no em or en dashes, prose lines at most 100 characters, note at most 180 lines.

## Public contract
- Step 0 (no request): `scripts/long_vol_real.py` of US-098 re-run on the stored NIFTY and
  BANKNIFTY bars with half spread 0, 0.6% and the live 0.3%; prints the three pair means. The
  script has no spread option today (`add_argument` lists none): add one, default unchanged.
- Fetch: `scripts/real_puts_fetch.py` of US-098 (stages `probe`, `plan`, `fetch`, `status`),
  given an underlyings option for FINNIFTY and SENSEX (it has none today); the app's own adapter
  (`DhanAdapter.rolling_option`,
  `src/onelot/data/expired_options.py`), same schema as `data/expired_options.db`, so
  `scripts/merge_option_bars.py` can merge it later.
- Analysis: `scripts/long_vol_real.py` run on the two new indices; report
  `docs/research/LONG_VOL_FIN_SENSEX_<DATE>.md`; cells in `cells.csv` with the columns of
  `data/research/real_puts_2026-11-25/cells.csv`.

## Notes & assumptions

Where the need was found: `docs/SETUP_BOOK.md` section 6 item 2 ("Left: FINNIFTY and SENSEX
puts, and the weekly contracts") and section 2 (long volatility loses 2.61% of its cost a pair
a day on NIFTY and BANKNIFTY, 95% interval -3.59 to -1.63, 2,444 trades, negative in all three
periods). The store has real puts only for those two indices; FINNIFTY and SENSEX are two of
the owner's four option indices and have wider live spreads (about 1.5% a side).

State plainly: long volatility ALREADY FAILED on the two indices that were tested, the call
alone lost 3.1% a day and the put alone 2.2%, and no day type was positive. The prior is that
it fails here too, with the wider spreads making it worse. This story asks only whether that
holds, so that the other two indices are not left as an open door. It is the cheapest story
here to drop.

Pre-registration (fixed before any request; RESEARCH-RULES.md; US-098 as the method):
- Question: does the long at-the-money call and put pair, bought 09:20 and sold 15:20, lose
  on FINNIFTY and SENSEX on their own real prices as it does on NIFTY and BANKNIFTY?
- Data: one-minute bars of the near-month at-the-money put and call, and the strikes up to ten
  steps away, FINNIFTY and SENSEX, every session Dhan serves from 2021-11-08 to 2026-11-07
  (SENSEX starts later: the store shows no SENSEX call bars in 2022, 94 days in 2023). About
  11,000 requests, one a second, about 3 hours after 15:40 IST. Monthly contracts only.
- Cells: 13, fixed: per index (FINNIFTY, SENSEX) and pooled, 09:20 to 15:20 ALL (3); 09:20 to
  12:00 ALL (3); 09:45 to 15:20 ALL (3); the pool at 09:20 to 15:20 for ACTIVE-EXPECTED,
  CALM-EXPECTED, VIX-UP and VIX-DOWN days (4). Examined: those with 60 trades on 30 days in
  TUNE (rule 5). The NIFTY and BANKNIFTY figures are shown beside, not re-tested.
- Split: rule 2; SENSEX's TUNE is shorter and is printed with its length.
- Costs: both legs their own real prints, as US-098; then a second reading with the measured
  half spread of each index (about 1.5% a side, per leg, four leg-sides) in place of the
  model's. Charges from `config/costs.yaml`. The pair is two positions of one lot each: a
  question for the owner (the one-lot rule); the % is of the pair's cost.
- Yardstick: "holds there" = the 95% interval of the pooled FINNIFTY and SENSEX pair mean has
  its upper end below zero (it loses, as on NIFTY and BANKNIFTY). "Does not hold" = the interval
  contains zero or lies above. A PROFIT is claimed only by rule 5 (discovered: at least +1.0%
  and t of 2.5 in TUNE; confirmed; passed on real prints). Benchmark: zero after costs (a
  same-day pair has no holding benchmark) and the N and BN mean of -2.61%.
- Luck check: placebo, 20 shuffles of the day-type labels over days inside index and year; the
  arithmetic count (examined x 0.6% x 16% x 50%); AVOID counts also compared with the placebo's,
  as US-098 did (63 against 59.9).
- Verdict words: AVOID (rule 5: TUNE t of -2.5 or less and negative in CHOOSE and TEST), NO
  EDGE (not different from zero, nothing earned), WATCH (positive in all three periods but
  short of a threshold), PASS (discovered, confirmed and passed on real prints: not expected).
- Stops early: Step 0 first. If the N and BN pair is zero or negative even at zero half spread,
  the wider spreads of the other two indices cannot lift it unless their own gross differs; the
  note then asks the owner in one line whether the fetch is still wanted, and sends no request
  until he says yes. Also stop the fetch after 3 refusals in a row (token, rate limit,
  entitlement) and keep what came; at most 5 requests before 15:40 IST, the live desk shares the
  data plan; the token on the desk expires at about 21:30 IST.
- No look-ahead (rule 6): day-type labels use only the previous session and the entry minute's
  known data; truncation test on at least 50 labels.

Related: US-113 (the same fetcher for FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY and SENSEX plus weekly flags, as data
only). Build the fetcher option once, in whichever story comes first, and reuse it in the other.

Not in scope: weekly contracts (the book's other "left" item: the desk names monthly
contracts), MIDCPNIFTY, any selling of options, any rule that is not the pair.
