# US-101: the pullback rule by market condition (study)

Read `.agile/stories/RESEARCH-RULES.md` first, then `.agile/stories/US-100.md` and its note
`docs/research/PULLBACK_BOOK_2026-11-25.md`. Owner, 2026-11-24: "single out the periods where
a strategy works a particular condition or some setup ... figure out when and under what
circumstances they performed best ... analyse it thoroughly until we see a tradable alpha".

Why: US-100 showed that a ten-trade book of the pullback rule did not beat NIFTY held, and
that the rule's money was made on evenings with many signals at once (broad dips), while
signals of quiet evenings lost. That table was descriptive, over all periods together, and
its split was not fixed in advance. This study fixes the conditions NOW and tests them by the
house rule, so that "trade the rule only in broad dips" becomes either a candidate or a
closed question, and "do not take it on quiet evenings" either an AVOID or nothing.

Where the idea comes from must be said in the note: it was first seen in US-100's table
over the whole span, so no period is fully unseen for it. TUNE holds 93% of the trades.

## The conditions (each read at the close of the signal evening; fixed)
One condition at a time (RESEARCH-RULES section 5). Each is a partition: a trade has exactly
one value of each condition.
- BREADTH: the number of the rule's signals that evening among the 222 stocks:
  `1 to 2`, `3 to 9`, `10 to 19`, `20 or more`.
- DEPTH: how far NIFTY's close stands below its highest close of the last 20 sessions:
  `under 1%`, `1 to 2%`, `2 to 4%`, `4% or more`.
- VIX (the owner's lens): India VIX's close divided by its own median close of the last 60
  sessions: `under 0.9`, `0.9 to 1.1`, `1.1 to 1.3`, `1.3 or more`. Only for evenings where
  the store has those 60 closes; the rest are counted and left out of this condition.
- ALL: no condition (the reference).
So 13 cells. The instrument is the declared pool of the 222 stocks (delivery).

## What a cell is measured on (fixed)
- RAW: the trade's own result after costs, % of the amount (`net_pct`).
- EXCESS: RAW less the return, over the trade's own holding days (entry open to exit), of
  the equal-weight basket of the 222 stocks with a bar on those days. The study's own
  `bench_pct` (NIFTY over the same days) gives a second excess, reported beside it.
- The t is the weekly-sum t of RESEARCH-RULES section 4 (one entry week is one draw).
- THE SELECTION RUNS ON THE EXCESS OVER THE BASKET (the same stocks, the same days): a cell
  that only earns what the market gave is drift, not a finding. Thresholds as in section 5
  for delivery: EXAMINED 60 TUNE trades on 30 entry days; DISCOVERED TUNE excess above zero
  with t of 2.5 or more and at least +0.50% a trade; CONFIRMED CHOOSE excess above zero with
  t of 1.0 or more on at least 20 trades; PASSED TEST excess above zero on at least 20
  trades. AVOID: TUNE excess t of −2.5 or less and negative in CHOOSE and TEST.
  The same selection on RAW is reported beside it, labelled "with drift".
- Against luck: the counts examined, discovered, confirmed, passed, beside the arithmetic of
  section 5 and a placebo of 20 shuffles of each condition's labels over the EVENINGS inside
  each calendar year (an evening keeps all its trades).
- Neighbours for any PASSED or AVOID cell: the adjacent values of its condition, in all three
  periods.

## Three books, named now (K = 10, the product's order, Rs 1 lakh, the cash rule of US-100)
- BOOK-QUIET-OUT: takes signals only on evenings with 3 or more signals.
- BOOK-BROAD: takes signals only on evenings with 10 or more signals.
- BOOK-DEEP: takes signals only on evenings with NIFTY 2% or more below its 20-session high.
Each against US-100's book (all evenings) and against B1, B2, B3 as defined there: end
value, growth a year, worst fall, places in use, trades held and refused, per period and
over the span, with the month bootstrap for "book less B" (2,000 draws, seed 20261125) AND,
because US-100's verdict hung on the seed, the share of seeds 0 to 39 whose interval
excludes zero, and the same with blocks of 3 months.
