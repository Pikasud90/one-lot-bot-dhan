# US-025 — Desk manual positions adopt the shared rules; fix short costing

## Story
As the owner, I want my manually taken positions managed by the same tested rules as the paper book, so that advice and paper results agree and short trades are charged correctly.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given the characterisation tests of the current desk advice (written first), when Desk._manage_positions is refactored to call onelot/desk/rules.py, then every characterised advice is unchanged (SQUARE_OFF label preserved where the desk used it).
- AC2: Given a manually taken SHORT stock, when it is closed, then charges use leg() with the real sides (STT on the entry leg) instead of round_trip_cost, verified against a hand-computed contract-note example.
