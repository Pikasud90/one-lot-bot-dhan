# US-121 — The desk stocks' day close is the exchange's closing price, not the 15:14 minute

## Story
As the owner, I want the day's close of each desk stock to be the exchange's closing price, so that the pullback rule, the charts and the paper results read the same close the exchange publishes

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given a finished session and Dhan's quote answer of that session for a desk stock, when the stock's daily bar of that session is built, then its close equals the quote's closing price and its open, high and low are the session's (a high or low made after 15:14 is included)
- AC2: Given the quote answer is missing, older than the session or malformed for a stock, when the daily bar is built, then the bar derived from the one-minute bars is used as today and the card says the close is the 15:14 price
- AC3: Given Dhan's official daily bar arrives on the next day, when the history is topped up, then it replaces the evening's bar and a difference above 0.05 per cent is listed in the top-up's note
- AC4: Given the five indices, when their daily bars are built, then nothing changes (their one-minute bars run to 15:29 and matched the exchange exactly on 25 Nov 2026)
- AC5: Given a session of 18 desk stocks, when the evening read runs, then it makes one quote request for all of them and no request before 16:05

## Notes & assumptions (lead, 2026-11-25)
Found by a cross-check on 25 Nov 2026 (script kept in the session's scratch folder; rerun it
with any session): Dhan's quote answer of the day against the one-minute bars the desk stored.
- Five indices: 375 bars, 09:15 to 15:29; open, high, low and close equal the exchange's
  figures (SENSEX's open differs by 0.035%).
- Eighteen stocks: 360 bars, 09:15 to 15:14 (Dhan's stock minute bars have ended at 15:14
  since 2026-08-03, see `.agile/sprints/sprint-02a.md`). Open and low equal the exchange's;
  two highs were made after 15:14 (0.03% and 0.16% off). The last stored price differs from
  the exchange's closing price by up to 0.60%: BAJFINANCE 0.60, AXISBANK 0.49, TCS 0.37,
  HDFCBANK 0.29, SUNPHARMA 0.28; only 5 of 18 are within 0.05%.
- Where it matters: `src/onelot/desk/history.py` (`rule_daily`, `consistent_daily`) builds
  today's daily bar of a desk stock from those minute bars, so the pullback rule on the 18
  desk stocks reads a "close" that is the 15:14 price until Dhan's official daily bar comes
  the next day. A signal near a threshold can differ from the one the official close gives.
- The quote answer (`POST /marketfeed/quote`, one request for many stocks) holds the day's
  open, high, low and closing price after 16:00: the same source US-106 uses for the wide
  list. Build this after US-106 and share its adapter method (`day_quotes`).
- Not in scope: the intraday rules (they act before 15:15), the indices, the option prices.
