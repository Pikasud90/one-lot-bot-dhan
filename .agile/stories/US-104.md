# US-104: an option alert never names a dead contract

Lead, 2026-11-25, after reading back the owner's live index option alerts since 23 Nov.

Why: of the 5 index option alerts raised since 23 Nov, 3 named a contract nobody trades:
- NIFTY, 25 Nov 13:14, BUY_PE: strike 29200 PE, expiry 2026-12-29, with the index at 25,653 (the
  strike 13.8% away). bid = ask = 4,962.00 (one price), open interest 0, IV 0.851, delta 0.646,
  outlay Rs 3,22,530; paper result -9,027.
- FINNIFTY, 25 Nov 13:14, BUY_PE: strike 32600 PE, index at 28,628 (13.9% away). One price
  5,696.60, open interest 0, IV 0.87, delta 0.648, outlay Rs 3,41,796; paper result -9,615.
- MIDCPNIFTY, 23 Nov 13:15, BUY_PE: strike 15800 PE, index at 14,262 (10.8% away). bid 1,313.45,
  ask 1,678.50 (a spread of 21.7%), open interest 0, IV 0.625, delta 0.643.

The two sound ones for comparison: BANKNIFTY 65700 PE (index 63,112; bid 2,716.50, ask
2,784.10; open interest 1,050; IV 0.209; delta 0.654) and SENSEX 83400 CE (index 83,858; bid
2,040.90, ask 2,096.95; open interest 540; delta 0.657).

The cause: `index_option_economics_from_chain` in `src/onelot/desk/economics.py` took every
chain row of the wanted type with a last price and picked the |delta| nearest 0.65. A dead
contract's stale last price makes the broker's IV and delta nonsense, and such a delta can land
nearest 0.65 by accident. A missing bid or ask fell back to the last price (a contract with no
quote read "spread 0.0%"), and open interest was never looked at. The fill-cost recorder
(`src/onelot/desk/fillcost.py`) watched "the contract an alert would name" by the same pick.

## Rules built
- C1 Tradeable rows only. `onelot.desk.option_pick.tradeable` decides whether a chain row can be
  traded now, and both picks use it: last price > 0, bid > 0, ask > 0, ask >= bid, open
  interest > 0, and a spread (ask - bid) / ask of at most `backtest.option_max_spread_pct` per
  cent (new, default 10, validated to lie between 1 and 50). When no row of the wanted type
  reports open interest (every oi is None) the open interest test is skipped for that pick. An
  oi of 0 is "none". None, NaN, infinities and negative numbers make a row not tradeable, never
  an exception.
- C2 The chosen row's own prices: the bid and the ask in the result are the row's own, the
  last price never stands in for a missing side.
- C3 The choice among tradeable rows is as before: the |delta| nearest the target; with no
  delta on any, the nearest in-the-money strike (else the nearest strike). A healthy chain gives
  exactly the old pick.
- C4 Nothing tradeable: `index_option_economics_from_chain` returns None (its signature is
  unchanged). The desk calls `chain_economics`, which returns a `ChainEconomics` with
  `untradeable` True when the chain had rows of the wanted type but none tradeable; the model's
  fallback then carries the first note "no tradeable contract in the live chain (no open
  interest or no two-sided quote): the premium is the model's estimate". No chain, or no rows
  of the type, gives no note, as before.
- C5 When the chosen |delta| is more than 0.10 from the target the notes gain "nearest
  tradeable contract: delta 0.52 (wanted about 0.65)" after the quote note.
- C6 One pick: the fill-cost recorder takes the tradeable rows of each side with a delta and an
  id and the same choice; with nothing tradeable it watches nothing new for that side.
- C7 The words: the docstring of `index_option_economics_from_chain`, the comment on the new
  field, `docs/04_OPERATIONS.md` (the index options section) and `docs/14_LIVE_DESK.md`.

Not in scope: the strategy, the target delta, the expiry choice, the stops and targets, the
paper book and the three recorded paper trades (the lead asks the owner about them), the
Simulator's and the Lab's model pricing, the phone message formats, the legacy engine.

## Tests
`tests/desk/test_option_pick.py`:
- T1 the NIFTY case: live puts with deltas 0.52, 0.61, 0.70 and the dead 29200 PE as recorded
  (bid and ask None, 0.0, or one price 4962.0; oi 0; delta -0.646): the pick is the 0.61 put
  with its own bid, ask and spread. A second test shows the old rule picked the dead row.
- T2 the MIDCPNIFTY case: a quoted row with oi 0 is not picked while one with open interest
  exists; the recorded 21.7% spread fails on its own.
- T3 every oi None: quotes alone decide; an oi of 0 is none; one row reporting oi means the
  side reports it.
- T4 open interest with a missing, zero or crossed side is never picked.
- T5 the spread cap: exactly 10% allowed, 10.01% and 21.7% not; the field's bounds; the chain
  pick reads the cap from the setting.
- T6 nothing tradeable: None and `untradeable`; through `Desk._economics` the model carries the
  note first; no chain gives no note; a tradeable chain is used without it.
- T7 the delta note only beyond 0.10 (0.75 is not beyond).
- T8 the fill-cost recorder watches the live row, not a dead one nearest the target, and
  nothing when nothing trades.
- T9 for 600 healthy random chains the pick equals the old rule (written inline as the oracle).
- T10 hypothesis: whatever the rows hold the functions never raise and a pick is tradeable,
  judged by the rule written out again in the test (not by the code under test).
- After the two reviews: a quoted row without a last price, and a row whose delta is NaN or
  infinite put first, are never picked; a table of bad numbers (negative open interest or IV,
  a bad strike, a NaN last price) makes a row not tradeable; the recorder reads its own spread
  cap; a desk with settings other than the defaults hands the alert and the recorder the same
  target delta and cap, so both name the same contract.

## What was changed
- `src/onelot/desk/option_pick.py` (new): `tradeable`, `oi_reported`, `tradeable_rows`,
  `choose`, `pick_contract`.
- `src/onelot/desk/economics.py`: `NO_TRADEABLE_NOTE`, `ChainEconomics`, `chain_economics`;
  `index_option_economics_from_chain` wraps it.
- `src/onelot/desk/desk.py`: `_economics` calls `chain_economics` and adds the note to the
  model's fallback, both reading the desk's own `backtest` settings; the fill-cost recorder
  gets the target delta and the spread cap from the same settings.
- `src/onelot/desk/fillcost.py`: `_refresh_options` uses the same pick; a `max_spread_pct`
  parameter.
- `src/onelot/config.py`: `BacktestCfg.option_max_spread_pct`.
- `tests/desk/test_fillcost.py`: its fake chain rows gained honest quotes and open interest.
