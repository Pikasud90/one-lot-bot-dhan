# US-078: options are traded on four indices only

Owner, 2026-11-23: "we only buy options of indices specifically, nifty, sensex, finnifty,
banknifty, no more, any further is for our training and testing." MIDCPNIFTY is the fifth
index the desk tracks. It becomes a TRAINING instrument: still tracked, still shown, still in
the Machine Learning Lab and the Simulator, but it raises no alert and opens no paper trade.

## What to build
- A flag on an instrument entry in `config/desk.yaml`: `trade: false` (default true), parsed
  in `src/onelot/desk/config.py` onto the instrument's config. Set it on MIDCPNIFTY, with a
  one-line comment that quotes the owner's rule.
- Do NOT use the slot's `enabled: false` for this: the Home scoreboard builds its "paused"
  rows from (strategy, timeframe, kind) without the instrument
  (`desk/config.py: paused_slots`), so pausing MIDCPNIFTY's slot would show the micro-trend
  row as paused for every index.
- Behaviour when `trade` is false: the instrument's slots are still evaluated (its card keeps
  its trend readings), but a verdict never becomes an alert: no row in `desk_signals`, no
  paper trade, no Telegram message, no notification. Put the guard where a verdict becomes a
  signal (`src/onelot/desk/desk.py`, around `_raise_signal`; read how it is called and choose
  the narrowest correct place). Log one line at INFO the first time it suppresses one in a
  session, not every poll.
- `GET /api/desk`: each instrument row carries `"trade": true|false`.
- The page: a training instrument's card on Home and on Live Desk shows a small label
  "Training only: no alerts" (find how cards are drawn in `src/onelot/ui/app.js`; make the
  smallest change; no colour literal; type no smaller than the card's existing small text).
- `docs/04_OPERATIONS.md` and `README.md`: where they say the desk trades five indices, say
  four traded indices and MIDCPNIFTY for training. Conventions: prose lines at most 100
  characters, plain words, no em or en dashes, ranges with "to".
- Stocks: nothing changes in this story (the size of a stock trade waits for the owner).

## Acceptance criteria (tests under `tests/desk/`)
1. Given `trade: false` on an index, when its slot gives a verdict that would alert, then
   there is no signal row, no paper trade and no notification, and the other four indices
   alert as before (same fixture, flag on and off).
2. The Home scoreboard does not show the micro-trend row as paused because of the flag.
3. `GET /api/desk` rows carry `trade`; the default is true; an unknown value in the yaml is
   refused with a clear message at load.
4. The card label appears only for a training instrument (node test of the helper, the way
   `tests/desk/test_microtrend_ui.py` lifts functions from `app.js`).
5. `config/desk.yaml` as shipped: MIDCPNIFTY has `trade: false`; NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY
   and SENSEX do not.
