# SP-004: Study: the pullback book against NIFTY held, every month

## Story
As the owner, I want the pullback paper book scored each month against NIFTY held and the same
stocks held, so that I learn from the book and not from single trades whether the rule has any edge.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given a month of the wide list's paper trades, when the review runs, then it prints tier all
  and tier cap each with trades, % a trade and a t that counts one entry week as one draw.
- AC2: Given the same month, when the review runs, then it prints the book's growth since 2026-11-25
  beside NIFTY held (B1), NIFTY at the book's exposure (B2) and the 222-stock basket at that
  exposure (B3), and the excess of each trade over the same stock held over the same days.
- AC3: Given the random-date placebo of 200 draws, when the review runs, then it prints the
  percentile of the paper mean excess among the draws, and the number of monthly looks taken so far.
- AC4: Given a look before month 12, when the verdict is printed, then it is WATCH, and no other
  word is printed before looks 12, 24, 36 and 48.
- AC5: Given a month with rounds missing on more than 20% of its sessions, when the review runs,
  then the month is marked void for scoring and the missing sessions are listed.

## Non-functional requirements
- Reproducible: the same store and month give the same table; the placebo uses a fixed seed
  written in the note.
- Read only (`mode=ro`) on `wide_trades`, `wide_rounds`, `wide_state` and `daily_bars`; writes
  only the note and a CSV under `data/research/`. No request to the live desk, no order.
- Done in under 10 minutes on the owner's machine, run with `nice -n 10`.
- Plain words, no em or en dashes, prose lines at most 100 characters, note at most 180 lines.

## Public contract
- Script `scripts/pullback_paper_review.py --month YYYY-MM` with function
  `review(store_path: str, month: str) -> dict`. Keys: `trades_all`, `trades_cap`,
  `pct_trade_all`, `pct_trade_cap`, `t_week_all`, `t_week_cap`, `book_value`, `b1`, `b2`, `b3`,
  `excess_same_stock`, `excess_t`, `placebo_percentile`, `rounds_missing` (list of sessions),
  `looks_taken`, `verdict`.
- Reads the tables of `src/onelot/store/schema.py`: `wide_trades` (columns `tier` with values
  `cap` and `test`, `late`, `signal_date`, `entry_session`, `exit_session`, `entry_paise`,
  `exit_paise`, `net_pct`), `wide_rounds` (`session_date`), `daily_bars`.
- Tier "all" is every row of `wide_trades` with `late` false; tier "cap" is `tier = 'cap'`.
- Output: `docs/research/PULLBACK_PAPER_<YYYY-MM>.md` and `data/research/pullback_paper_<YYYY-MM>/
  review.csv`. The method is the one of `scripts/pullback_book.py` (US-100) and
  `scripts/pullback_conditions.py` (US-101); reuse their functions by file path.

## Notes & assumptions

Where the need was found: `docs/SETUP_BOOK.md` section 6 item 1 (the yardstick of the paper
test of the wide list: "each month compare both tiers with NIFTY and with the 222-stock basket
at the same exposure", and score each trade against the same stocks held), and section 5 (the
wide list runs from 25 Nov 2026). The reviews of US-100 and US-101 fixed the history figures;
nothing yet reads the paper record the same way each month.

What exists: the paper test (US-099, `src/onelot/delivery/wide.py`), the history book (Rs 1 lakh,
10 places: Rs 1 lakh became Rs 1.30 lakh, against Rs 4.15 lakh in NIFTY held and Rs 2.27 lakh in
the basket), the rule's excess over the same stocks held (-0.16% a trade after costs, t -2.3).

Pre-registration (fixed before any month is read; RESEARCH-RULES.md sections 2, 3, 4, 5):
- Question: does the paper book of the pullback rule on the wide list beat NIFTY held and the
  same stocks held, on its own paper trades, after costs?
- Data: `wide_trades`, `wide_rounds`, `wide_state` of the live store from 2026-11-25 on, and
  the daily bars of NIFTY and of the 222 stocks in `daily_bars`. The history is the study's
  8,085 trades (`data/research/swing_2026-11-24/trades_P1.pkl`), TUNE / CHOOSE / TEST per
  rule 2; the paper record is a fourth period PAPER, never merged into TEST.
- What is compared (8 comparisons, counted): tiers all and cap, each against B1 NIFTY held, B2
  NIFTY at the book's exposure, B3 the 222-stock basket at that exposure, and each trade's
  excess over the same stock held over the same days (as US-101, basket without costs).
- Costs: the pullback study's model, 0.51% round trip including the depository charge of
  Rs 14.75 a sale (rule 3), at the paper trade's own fills. Benchmarks carry no costs and the
  note says so.
- Yardstick: a book BEATS a benchmark when its growth since 2026-11-25 is above the
  benchmark's on the same rupees AND a 95% bootstrap interval of the difference (draws of
  calendar weeks, 2,000 draws, seed 20261125) lies above zero. The rule's trades beat the
  same stocks held when the mean excess per trade is above zero with t of 2 or more, a week
  as one draw. About +0.6 of the rule's +0.77% is market drift, so only the excess counts.
- Sample arithmetic (setup book section 6 item 1): tier all needs about 48 months to show
  its +0.77% at t 2 (about 18 at the last year's size); the cap tier's held trades at +0.14%
  would need over 80 years. So the cap tier's own score says nothing; it is read only as the
  book's growth.
- Luck check: 200 random-date placebo books over the same stocks and counts; the report prints
  the percentile of the paper mean excess among them. It also prints the number of monthly
  looks taken, because every look is another chance for luck.
- Verdict words: WATCH, NO EDGE, PASS. AVOID is not used (the rule has no condition). Looks
  that may print a verdict other than WATCH: 12, 24, 36 and 48 months after 2026-11-25; the
  others are descriptive. PASS needs at least 100 closed tier-all trades, the book above B2
  and B3 by the yardstick, and excess t of 2 or more. NO EDGE: at look 24 or later, the
  interval of the book minus B3 lies entirely at or below zero, or the excess per trade is at
  or below zero with at least 100 closed trades.
- Stops early: if rounds ran on fewer than 80% of the sessions since 2026-11-25 (the desk was
  down, or Dhan's daily bar came late, US-106), the test is void for scoring and the report
  says so; a restart date is written in the note. Late signals (`late` true) are never scored.
- No look-ahead (rule 6): a benchmark uses closes up to the entry session's open only; prove the
  paper entry equals the study's rule on the same stored bars by the replay of
  `scripts/pullback_wide_replay.py` on at least 50 paper signals.

Not in scope: changing the rule, the cap or the order (US-100 fixed them); real money; the
desk 18's separate `delivery_trades`; a better rule. Adani Green is excluded.
