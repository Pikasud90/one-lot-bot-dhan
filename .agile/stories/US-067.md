# US-067: The pullback rule as delivery stock trades in the paper test

Status: review (built in 5 increments on 2026-11-12; awaiting the independent tester).
Priority: Must. Owner's decision of 2026-11-12. Supersedes US-027 for
stocks. Branch `story/US-067-pullback-delivery`, worktree
`/Users/sudhanshushekhar/Downloads/ONE LOT BOT - DHAN - wt-us067`.

As the owner, I want the desk to raise and paper-trade the pullback rule on my 18 stocks as
delivery trades of about Rs 10,000 that are carried for days, so that the one rule with
evidence behind it is tested forward exactly as it was backtested.

Evidence: `docs/research/ALPHA_SEARCH_2026-11-12.md` sections 5.2 and 5.3. Reference
implementation (independent, plain loops): `scripts/alpha_pullback_check.py`, function
`trades()`. Fixtures written from it: `tests/fixtures/pullback/` (19 files of daily bars
2017-01-02 to 2026-11-06, `expected_trades.csv` with 247 trades, `rule.json`).

## Owner's decisions that bind this story
- Options are intraday only and are not the focus: with no edge found, the sole focus is
  stocks where the edge is. Every other slot of the desk is paused, not deleted.
- A delivery trade is the number of shares nearest Rs 10,000 (not one share). Index options
  stay one lot.
- The system never places an order. Alerts and paper trades only.

## The rule (R)
- R1 Entry signal for stock s on the close of session t when all hold: (a) close[t] >
  SMA200[t]; (b) NIFTY's close of the same date > NIFTY's SMA200 (no NIFTY bar that day, or
  fewer than 200 NIFTY bars: no signal); (c) close[t] <= 0.92 x max(close[t-19..t]); (d) the
  same test (c) was false on session t-1, with its own window t-20..t-1; (e) no open or
  pending pullback trade in s. At least 201 bars are needed (bar index >= 200).
- R2 Entry fill: the open of session t+1 x 1.0003.
- R3 Exit signal on the close of session u (u >= the entry session), the first of: (a)
  close[u] > SMA20[u]; (b) the trade has been open 30 sessions, the entry session counting as
  1; (c) close[u] <= entry fill x 0.85. Exit fill: the open of session u+1 x 0.9997.
- R4 After an exit signal on session u, the earliest new entry signal is the close of
  session u+1 (the day of the sale). The close that exits is never an entry.
- R5 Quantity: q = max(1, floor(10000 / close[t] + 0.5)) shares, fixed at the signal.
  `unit_inr` is configuration, 10000 by default.
- R6 Charges: the delivery product's charges on both legs of the trade's turnover, plus the
  depository charge (Rs 14.75, configuration) once per sale. Net result in rupees and in %
  of the entry value.
- R7 Parity: on the fixture bars the product's rule gives exactly the 247 trades of
  `expected_trades.csv`: the same signal, entry and exit dates, entry open and exit price,
  and net % before the depository charge within 1e-6 (slippage 0.0003 a side, the default
  cost configuration's delivery charges).
- R8 Causality: cutting the bars off after any session t leaves every signal up to t
  unchanged.

## The desk and the paper book (D)
- D1 Evaluation: once per completed session and stock, after the session's last bar; and at
  start-up for the last completed session if it was not evaluated yet (catch-up).
- D2 Entry alert: one alert, "BUY q shares of S at the next open (last close Rs x)", raised
  after the close of session t. It is on Home and the Live Desk, goes to the notifier, is
  archived, and does NOT expire after ten minutes: it is pending until the next session.
- D3 Entry fill: at the first one-minute bar of the next trading session the paper trade
  opens at that bar's open x 1.0003 with quantity q. If the desk was not running at that open
  and starts during that session, the trade opens at the open of the first one-minute bar
  after the start and is marked late. If the whole session passes without the desk, the alert
  is closed as "missed: the desk was not running" and no trade opens.
- D4 Carry: the trade stays open across sessions and restarts. The session-end close of
  intraday trades never touches it. One open pullback trade a stock.
- D5 Exit: an exit signal raises one exit alert after the close of session u ("SELL at the
  next open", with the reason). The paper trade closes at the next session's first
  one-minute bar open x 0.9997. If the desk misses that open it sells at the first bar it
  sees after starting and marks the exit late.
- D6 Record: entry and exit fills and times, quantity, sessions held, charges (delivery and
  depository), net rupees and %, exit reason, late flags. Closed trades count on the Home
  scoreboard under their own slot.
- D7 Open trades show: last price, unrealised rupees and %, sessions held "k of 30", the
  SMA20 level that would exit, the stop level.
- D8 Data: the rule reads at least 221 completed daily bars for each stock and for NIFTY,
  built the way the research built them (`onelot.data.hygiene`), and adds today's bar from
  the desk's own one-minute bars once the session is complete. If the history is short or a
  completed trading session is missing, the stock's card says so, no alert is raised and a
  warning is logged. The daily history is topped up from the broker when the Data API is on.
- D9 Configuration: a slot `pullback` (timeframe `1d`, delivery, `unit_inr: 10000`, the
  parameters of R1 to R3) on the 18 stocks. A new slot key `enabled` (default true); every
  other slot is set `enabled: false` and is not evaluated. Their scoreboard rows stay,
  shown as paused.
- D10 Invariants: no order placement anywhere. The one-unit invariant is changed on purpose
  for delivery trades only, with a comment that cites the owner's decision of 2026-11-12.
- D11 Interface: each stock card shows the pullback reading (distance below the 20-session
  closing high against the 8% needed, the SMA200 test, the NIFTY test). Home shows pending
  delivery alerts and open delivery trades with D7's fields. The order of Home, the Day
  theme and the type scale do not change.
- D12 Restart safety: a restart at any moment (alert pending, trade open, exit pending)
  resumes with no duplicate alert and no duplicate trade. Identity is (stock, signal date).
- D13 Replay: a script replays stored bars session by session through the product path and
  reproduces the reference trades on the owner's store, read-only.
- D14 Evidence line: every delivery alert and the pullback block of a stock card carry one
  line of evidence from configuration, so that nobody reads the alert as a proven edge:
  "Backtest: +1.2% to +1.4% a trade after charges, about 7 winners in 10, two samples. Not
  proven: this is the paper test."
- D15 Corporate-action flag: if a stock's first bar of a session is more than 20% away from
  the previous close, any pending or open delivery trade in it gets a visible warning
  ("possible corporate action on <date>: check the figures"). Nothing else changes.
- D16 Pausing never orphans a trade: an intraday paper trade that is open when its slot is
  paused is still managed and closed by the paper book as before.

## Lead's rulings on the design (2026-11-12)
The design note `US-067-design.md` is approved with these changes:
- A session is complete for a stock when the clock is at least 10 minutes past the session's
  close AND that date's last one-minute bar is at or after 15:10 IST (configuration). F&O
  stocks stop continuous trading at 15:15 since 2026-08-03, so their last bar is 15:14: a
  test that wants the last bar within 5 minutes of 15:30 would never pass. A day that is not
  in the calendar (a special session) is complete the next day, as the design says.
- The top-up runs only outside market hours (never between 09:00 and 15:35 IST), only for
  the 19 series, only when the Data API is on, behind a configuration switch that is on by
  default, and is injected so that no test touches a broker or the network.
- Questions 1 to 8 of the design note: as recommended, with the top-up as above.

## Lead's rulings after the review and the independent test (2026-11-12)
The reviewer found no blocker and four major defects; the tester found desk-level parity of
247 of 247 (with the history minimum at the rule's own 201 bars) and one more major gap.
These rulings change or sharpen the criteria above.
- D8 minimum: the desk uses the rule's own minimum (201 bars), not 221, so the desk path and
  the reference agree on every trade. The tester's whole-window parity run must give 247 of
  247 with the shipped settings.
- D8 holes and D3/D5 stalls: a hole or an incomplete day may stop an ENTRY alert; it must
  never stop the EXIT tests of an open trade and never stop later sessions from being read.
  An exit reads only the stock's own bars; NIFTY missing on a date means "no entry signal
  that day", recorded as evaluated. A stock's day that is still incomplete when a later
  session of that stock is complete is read as it is (its last price as the close) for exit
  tests only, raises no entry, and puts a visible warning on the card and on any open trade
  ("<date> read from incomplete data"). A bar with a missing or non-positive open or close
  is ignored with a note; it must not disable the averages.
- Failure isolation: an error in one stock or one trade is logged, counted and skipped; the
  other stocks are still filled and evaluated on that poll.
- Notifications: a delivery alert must reach every channel as its own text (BUY q shares of S
  at the next open, the last close, the evidence line; SELL with the reason), including the
  Telegram channel, tested with the real channel class and a fake transport. An alert is
  marked told only when a channel accepted it.
- Cost of a poll: a poll with no new bar and no state change reads nothing from the store
  and rebuilds nothing. A test counts the calls.
- Stored against live days: where the store has a day's one-minute bars to at least 15:10,
  the stored day is used; a live day is used only for days the store lacks or has short.
- "Late" also means the fill bar is more than two minutes after the session's opening
  minute, whatever the reason (a feed gap as well as a late start).
- The top-up reports its errors, retries at most three times in a window with at least 20
  minutes between tries, and stops when the clock reaches 09:00 IST.
- Known limit, to be written in the operator notes: after a catch-up over several sessions an
  entry signal on the day after an exit signal can be dropped, because the exit is still
  pending. The reference would take it.
