# US-013 — Telegram commands and inline Taken/Skip buttons

## Story
As the owner, I want to act on alerts from my phone, so that I can confirm or skip without opening the app.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given getUpdates long polling, then messages from any chat other than the configured chat id are ignored and logged; /status, /positions, /paper and /mute N (minutes) /unmute work and answer in under 3 seconds.
- AC2: Given an alert message with Taken and Skip buttons, when Taken is pressed, then Desk.acknowledge records the trade at the alert entry price and the message is edited to show monitoring; pressing a stale button after the TTL answers expired and changes nothing.
- AC3: Given /mute, then alerts still open paper trades and appear in the app, and only the phone push is suppressed.
