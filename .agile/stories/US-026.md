# US-026 — Desk poll never blocks on notifications; SQLite contention bounded

## Story
As the owner, I want a slow notification channel or a long database write to never stall alert generation, so that alerts are never late because of a side effect.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given a notification channel that sleeps 6 seconds (the macOS toast uses subprocess with a 6 s timeout), when the desk raises an alert, then the desk poll is not blocked by it (notifications are dispatched outside the desk lock) and poll latency stays under 1 second in a test with a slow fake channel.
- AC2: Given a long Lab backfill transaction on the same SQLite file, then desk and paper writes still complete within 2 seconds (batched per step, busy_timeout reviewed) and a test with a contending writer proves it.
