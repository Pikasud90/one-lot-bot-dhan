# SP-006 — Study: do the option results change on the contract the new pick names

## Story
As the owner, I want past option signals re-priced on the contract the tradeable pick would have
named, so that I know whether the option studies measured a contract the desk would never buy.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given the five signal sets named in the story, when the study runs, then it prints for each
  how many signals can be priced on both the studies' contract and the pick's contract, and writes
  no t for a set under 25% priced.
- AC2: Given a priced signal, when both contracts are chosen, then the pick uses only data from bars
  closed before the entry minute, proved by a truncation test on at least 50 random signals.
- AC3: Given the 9 pre-registered comparisons, when the report is written, then each shows the
  paired difference in % of premium, its day-cluster t, and the three period values.
- AC4: Given the strike placebo of 20 shuffles, when the report is written, then it prints how many
  of the 9 comparisons exceed what a random neighbouring strike gives.
- AC5: Given the report, when a verdict is printed, then it is PASS, WATCH, AVOID or NO EDGE as
  defined in the story and no setup status in the book is changed by the study itself.

## Non-functional requirements
- Reproducible: seeded placebo (seed written in the note); intermediate files cached under
  `data/research/contract_pick_<DATE>/` so a stage can be re-run cheaply.
- Stores read only (`data/onelot.db` and `data/expired_options.db`, `mode=ro`); no request to
  Dhan, none to the live desk, no order. Use `nice -n 10` and at most 4 workers.
- Plain words, no em or en dashes, prose lines at most 100 characters, note at most 180 lines.

## Public contract
- Script `scripts/contract_pick_study.py` with stages `coverage`, `pick`, `compare`, `report`.
- Function `historic_pick(day, minute, side, index, blocks) -> Contract | None`: the contract
  `onelot.desk.option_pick.choose` would have named, using the history proxy of the note
  (a real bar with volume above 0 in the 5 minutes before entry, and open interest above 0
  where the bar has the field), delta from the bar's own implied volatility (see
  `onelot.lab.real_options.implied_days` and `premium_in_minute`).
- Sets: A `microtrend` 3m (3,065 trades, four indices); B to E the four WATCH cells of
  `docs/research/SETUP_GRID_2026-11-24.md` (trades from its output folder).
- Output: `docs/research/CONTRACT_PICK_<DATE>.md` and `cells.csv` with columns `set`, `index`,
  `n_both`, `share_priced`, `diff_mean`, `diff_t`, `tune`, `choose`, `test`, `placebo_p95`,
  `verdict`.

## Notes & assumptions

Where the need was found: `.agile/stories/US-104.md` (the desk's pick took any row with a last
price and the delta nearest 0.65; 3 of 5 live index alerts named a dead contract; the new rule
requires bid and ask above 0, open interest above 0 and a spread within
`backtest.option_max_spread_pct`) and `docs/research/OPTION_SETUPS_2026-11-24.md` ("Priced
share of the exact contract: S1 27%, S2 16%, S5 6%, S6 14%": the studies priced the model's own
strike, at the money or at delta 0.65, where the store held it, else the model less 1.0 point).
Nobody has asked whether the contract the desk will really name is priced like the contract the
studies assumed.

Pre-registration (fixed before any result; RESEARCH-RULES.md sections 2 to 6):
- Question: on the same past signals, does buying the contract the tradeable pick would have
  named earn a different result, in % of premium, from the contract the studies priced?
- Data: the five signal sets above, 2021-11-08 to 2026-11-07, NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY,
  SENSEX; real bars of `data/expired_options.db` (monthly contracts, strikes the store holds,
  including the 21,279 day blocks merged on 25 Nov); history for chains: `chain_snapshots` only
  where it exists (the report says from when). MIDCPNIFTY is apart and not counted.
- Contract A: what the studies assumed (the model strike at delta 0.65, real bar where priced).
  Contract B: among the strikes with a real bar that pass the history proxy of the tradeable
  rule, the |delta| nearest 0.65. A priced pair = both A and B have a real bar at the entry
  minute and the exit. Only priced pairs enter any mean; the priced share is printed.
- Cells: 9 comparisons, fixed: set A pooled and per index (5), sets B to E (4). Examined: 9.
  Rule 5's 60-trades-on-30-days test applies to each; one that fails is printed, not read.
- Split: TUNE / CHOOSE / TEST by rule 2; the paired difference B minus A is shown in each.
- Costs: both legs of the comparison are costed the same way: the half spreads measured per
  index (0.25% NIFTY, 0.3% BANKNIFTY, 1.5% FINNIFTY and SENSEX, from US-097 and the
  fill recorder) each side, plus `config/costs.yaml` charges; a second reading with the model's
  0.6% flat. The proxy cannot know a spread in the past; that is said in the note.
- Yardstick: B CHANGES the result when the paired difference is at least 0.5 point of premium
  in size, day-cluster t of 2 or more, same sign in all three periods, above the placebo's
  95th percentile. Benchmark for "better": contract A on the same signals; for a set's own
  standing, zero after costs on the real reading (rule 3). No holding-NIFTY benchmark: same day.
- Luck check: strike placebo, 20 shuffles: replace B by a random strike within 2 steps of A on
  the same day, same stats. Print the placebo's mean count of changes against the real count of
  the 9; an arithmetic count (9 x 5% x 50%) beside it.
- Verdict words, for the contract pick and never for a setup: PASS (B is better by the
  yardstick: the book's reading of that set is re-run on B in a later step), AVOID (B is worse
  by the same yardstick: the new pick costs the set), WATCH (a difference short of a threshold
  or below 25% priced), NO EDGE (no difference beyond the placebo).
- Stops early: a set under 25% priced pairs gets no t (rule 3 style) and is reported as not
  readable; if the history proxy rejects more than half of the strikes A names on NIFTY, stop
  and say the proxy is too harsh to read, before looking at any result. A looser proxy (volume in
  15 minutes) may be run only as a labelled sensitivity.
- No look-ahead (rule 6): the pick at minute t uses bars that closed before t; truncation test
  on at least 50 random signals.

Not in scope: changing any status in the book (the lead decides after reading); option exits;
real puts of FINNIFTY and SENSEX (SP-007); anything that places an order.
