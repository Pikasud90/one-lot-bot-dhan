# US-014 — Alert confluence and dedup

## Story
As the owner, I want several strategies agreeing on the same instrument and direction to produce one stronger alert instead of a burst, so that my phone and attention are not flooded.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given two or more strategies fire long on the same instrument within one poll cycle, then exactly one alert is raised listing all strategies, with confluence count and the highest-confidence parameters; each strategy still gets its own paper trade.
- AC2: Given an instrument stopped out by a strategy, then the same strategy and direction cannot alert again for a configurable cooldown.
- AC3: Given opposite-direction alerts on one instrument within the TTL, then the older pending alert is marked superseded and the owner is told.
