# US-099: the pullback rule as a paper test on a wide list of stocks

Owner, 2026-11-25, to the lead's question "Widen the pullback paper test to about 200 stocks?
I recommend yes, with a cap on open paper trades (say 10)": "yes to all three, go with your
recommendations, also fetch all the data you mentioned."

Why: the stock pullback rule is the only setup that has held on every set of stocks tried
(`docs/SETUP_BOOK.md`, `docs/research/SWING_UNIVERSE_2026-11-24.md`: +0.81% a trade on 161
stocks nobody had looked at; about +0.6 of it is market drift, +0.2 to +0.3 the rule's own
timing: WATCH, not proven). On the 18 desk stocks it trades too rarely to be judged (its last
backtest entry was in January 2026). On 222 stocks it gives about ten signals a week: a paper
test there can be judged within months. This story builds that test. It is a PAPER test: the
desk still never places an order.

## What to build
1. THE WIDE LIST as data, not code: `config/delivery_universe.yaml` (key, name, Dhan security
   id and segment, the group: desk, alpha or new), written from the study's list by a small
   script that stays in the repository. The 204 stocks that are not desk instruments are
   DELIVERY-ONLY: daily bars only, no feed subscription, no intraday bars, no card.
2. HISTORY in the store: a tool `scripts/import_daily_universe.py --from FILE --store FILE
   [--dry-run]` that copies the research file's daily bars for the delivery-only stocks into
   the app's store as daily bars through the app's own writer (commits in small batches so
   the desk's writes are never blocked; never touches a series that is a desk instrument;
   prints what it would add). The LEAD runs it on the live store; you run it only on a
   private copy.
3. THE EVENING ROUND for delivery-only stocks, after the close (never between 09:00 and
   15:35), once per finished session, in the delivery desk's background thread:
   - fetch the last 30 daily bars of each stock through the desk's own adapter, at most two
     requests a second, resumable, an error per stock noted and the rest continuing;
   - ADJUSTMENTS: when a stock's fetched closes differ from the stored ones on the overlap by
     more than 0.5% (a split, a bonus or a demerger re-based its history), fetch that stock's
     whole history again and replace it, and say so in the round's note; never evaluate a
     stock whose overlap disagrees and whose refetch failed (mark it stale);
   - then evaluate the rule on each stock with the SAME code that evaluates the desk 18: a
     signal on today's close becomes a paper trade to be filled at the next session's open
     (taken from that session's daily bar in the next round); open trades are managed on the
     daily bars of each round with the rule's own exits. Entry and exit prices, costs and
     the whole-share count at about Rs 10,000 exactly as the product does for the desk 18.
4. TWO TIERS, because the cap is about the owner's money and the evidence must be whole:
   - every signal becomes a paper trade (tier "all");
   - at most `cap_open` (config, default 10) trades are open "within the cap" at a time; a
     new signal takes a free place, the desk 18 first, then the deepest pullback first; a
     trade keeps its tier for life. Only a within-the-cap signal is ALERTED (Home, the
     computer, Telegram, as delivery alerts are today, marked "wide list"); the others are
     recorded and shown as "recorded for the test, not alerted: the cap of 10 is full".
   The desk 18 keep working exactly as today (their alert near the close, their management),
   except that their open trades count toward the cap.
5. THE PAGE: in the delivery panel that exists (find it on Home and Live Desk), a compact
   "Wide list" block, no card per stock: when the last round ran and what it found (stocks
   scanned, stale, refetched, signals), the open trades in both tiers, the closed ones, and
   the test's score in both tiers (trades, winners, % a trade after costs, net rupees),
   beside the backtest's figure. A table of the stocks closest to a signal is welcome if it
   is cheap. Plain words, large type, no colour literal, all five themes.
6. ROUTES: the delivery block of `GET /api/desk` gains what the page needs, or a new
   `GET /api/delivery/wide`; reads only.
7. CONFIG: in `config/desk.yaml` under the pullback slot: `wide: true`, `cap_open: 10`,
   `evening_round: true`, the path of the universe file; with `wide: false` the desk behaves
   exactly as before this story (a test proves it).
8. A REPLAY for the lead: `scripts/pullback_wide_replay.py --daily FILE [--from DATE]` runs
   the PRODUCT's evening round day by day over the research file (read-only, the book in a
   temporary database, no adapter) and compares its "all" tier with the study's trades of
   the same rule (`data/research/swing_2026-11-24/`): entry date, exit date, exit reason;
   it prints the match count and each kind of mismatch. Sizes: the study bought the whole
   shares at or just under Rs 10,000, the product rounds to the nearest share: compare
   dates and reasons, not rupees, and say so.

## Acceptance criteria (tests under `tests/delivery/`)
1. With `wide: false` nothing changes: every existing delivery test passes untouched and
   `scripts/pullback_replay.py` on its test store gives what it gave.
2. With `wide: true` the desk 18 still raise and manage exactly the same trades (the 247
   reference trades of the fixtures through the product path).
3. The evening round on fixture daily bars: a signal becomes a pending trade, fills at the
   next session's open from the next round, exits by each of the three exits; a round run
   twice the same evening changes nothing; a restart between rounds continues.
4. Adjustment: a stock whose history is re-based (all earlier closes halved in the fetched
   bars) is refetched and replaced and raises NO false signal; a refetch failure marks it
   stale and it is not evaluated; other stocks go on.
5. The cap: with 10 within the cap open, an 11th signal is recorded in tier "all" only and
   not alerted; when a capped trade closes the next NEW signal takes the place (an old
   uncapped trade never moves up); the order among same-day signals (desk 18 first, then
   depth) is tested.
6. Alerts: a within-the-cap signal reaches a recording Notifier once; an uncapped one never.
7. The import tool: on a private store it adds the daily bars of delivery-only stocks, never
   a desk instrument's, never twice, and `--dry-run` writes nothing.
8. The replay tool runs on a small fixture file in a test.
9. No request to the broker in any test; the round never runs between 09:00 and 15:35.
