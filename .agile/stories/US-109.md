# US-109: A stock taken off the delivery list keeps its open wide-list trade managed

## Story
As the owner, I want an open wide-list paper trade to be managed to its exit even after its stock
leaves config/delivery_universe.yaml, so that a removed stock never holds one of the ten places
for good.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given an open or exit-pending wide trade whose stock is no longer on the list, when the round
  runs, then the stock's daily bars are still fetched and the trade is managed by the rule's own
  exits.
- AC2: Given the same stock, then no NEW signal of it is read, recorded or alerted.
- AC3: Given such a trade, then the wide list's block says "off the list, managed to its exit"
  beside its row.
- AC4: Given that trade closes, then the stock is no longer fetched and its row has no flag.
- AC5: Given a removed stock that has stopped trading, then US-103's dormancy rule applies to it as
  to any other.

## Non-functional requirements
- No more than one extra request a second (the round's own limiter); the extra stocks are few (open
  trades only).
- A stock that is both off the list and a desk instrument stays with the desk (`protected` keys are
  unchanged).

## Public contract
- `WideDesk.__init__` (`src/onelot/delivery/wide.py`): `self.stocks = {s.key: s for s in stocks if
  s.key.upper() not in self.protected}` is the only set the round asks for; the open trades of
  `WideBook` (`wide_trades`) are not looked at.
- Needed: the round reads the keys of the book's active trades and adds each missing key's
  `UniverseStock` (from the book row or a stored security id) to the set asked, marked `off_list`.
- Page: `wideStatus`, the wide row in `src/onelot/ui/app.js` (line 840).

## Notes & assumptions

Found in `.agile/stories/US-103.md` on branch `story/US-103-wide-no-data`, section "Not done, and
limits": "A stock taken off `config/delivery_universe.yaml` while it has an active wide trade
still holds its place (the round skips stocks not on the list): for the backlog." Verified on
`main`: the round builds `self.stocks` from the list only (wide.py line 515), so such a trade is
never read again and keeps one of the `cap_open` places.

Depends on US-103 for the dormancy link (AC5); the rest can be built without it, but both change
`wide.py`, so build it after US-103 and US-106.

Open question for the owner (default is A): A, keep managing the trade by the rule to its own exit
(written above); or B, close it at once at the last stored close with the reason `NO_DATA`-like
"removed from the list", booked as a sale. A is chosen because removing a stock from the list says
"no new entries" and not "sell"; the owner may hold the shares.

NOT in scope: a screen to edit the list; changing the cap.
