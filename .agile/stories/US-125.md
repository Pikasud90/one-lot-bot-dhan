# US-125 — The price model names the monthly contract by the real expiry day of each period

## Story
As the owner, I want a backtest before September 2025 to price the contract that really traded, so that the days to expiry in the model are the real ones

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given a date before the exchange moved the monthly expiry (Thursdays before Sep 2025, Tuesdays after), when the model names the monthly contract, then its expiry is the real expiry date of that month
- AC2: Given a date after the change, when the model names the contract, then the result equals the result of today
- AC3: Given the Simulator reading a real price, when it names the contract, then the label and the stored bars are of the same contract
