# US-027 — Swing carry rules for stocks: shorts intraday, delivery costs, gap quarantine

## Story
As the owner, I want paper trades to carry stock longs overnight only where that is legal and realistic, so that swing results are not flattered or distorted.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given a stock SHORT alert, then its paper trade always closes SESSION_END (a retail cash-equity short cannot be carried overnight); given a stock LONG with horizon swing, then it is carried, closes TIME_STOP at the first open of session N+1 when swing_max_sessions is N (the opening session counts as 1), and is charged on equity_delivery.
- AC2: Given an overnight gap larger than paper.gap_quarantine_pct (default 12 percent, a split or bonus signature), then the trade is quarantined with an event for owner review instead of being stopped out or targeted, and excluded from statistics until resolved.
