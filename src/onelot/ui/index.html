How to read the Lab

What a backtest does

Signals are decided on closed bars and filled at the next bar's open with slippage and the full Indian charge stack (brokerage, STT, exchange, SEBI, stamp, GST). One unit per trade: 1 share, or 1 lot. Index option trades are priced with a Black-76 model off the underlying and India VIX (synthetic) until the Dhan option-chain history is wired.

The numbers that matter

  • Expectancy (R) — average result per trade in units of initial risk. Positive after costs is the first bar to clear.
  • Profit factor — gross wins ÷ gross losses. Below 1.0 loses; 1.2+ with 30+ trades starts to mean something.
  • Payoff — average win ÷ average loss. The 30 % rule: at a 30 % win rate you need a payoff of 2.33 to break even, so the panel shows the profit factor your exits would give at 30 % wins.
  • Max drawdown (₹) — worst peak-to-trough in rupees, which is what you actually feel.
  • Trades — fewer than ~30 and every other number is noise.

Walk-forward

The honest test. The data is cut into folds: optimise the parameters on an in-sample window, then run them untouched on the next out-of-sample window; slide forward; repeat. Only the stitched out-of-sample trades are scored. Windows are sized automatically from the data (at least 3 folds); it refuses to run on fewer than 12 sessions instead of reporting an empty "done". Default-params OOS shows what the untuned strategy did on the same windows — if tuning does not beat it, the tuning is curve-fitting.

Optimizer

Random search over the strategy's declared ranges, scored SQN-style, then the top candidates are re-scored with jittered neighbours and the most plateau-robust one wins (a sharp peak that dies when a parameter moves by one step is not an edge). In-sample numbers are optimistic by construction.

Sweep & leaderboard

Sweep runs every strategy on every watchlist instrument at four timeframes with default parameters and saves each run. The leaderboard ranks all saved runs by expectancy × √trades (capped at 100 trades), unranked below the minimum trade count; walk-forward rows are flagged OOS and should be trusted over in-sample rows.

Sentiment gate

Once historical scores are imported (News & Sentiment → Import), a strategy can be gated: follow blocks longs while sentiment is bearish and shorts while bullish; strict allows only trades with sentiment; contrarian the opposite. The score used for a bar is the latest one at or before that bar's close, never a later one.

Backfill the corpus

Yahoo is keyless (2m/5m/15m ≈ 60 days, 60m ≈ 2 years, daily unlimited). Once the Dhan Data API is active, pick dhan for five years of 1-minute bars — one re-download then covers every timeframe the Lab and the Desk use.
