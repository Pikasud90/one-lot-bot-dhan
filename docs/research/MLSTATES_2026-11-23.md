# Market states: do they carry memory? (US-077, 2026-11-23)

Question: if each session is given a state (direction bull, flat or bear; size calm or active),
does the state of the last sessions say anything about tomorrow's? Rules were fixed before the
run and not tuned: bull when the day's return is more than 0.5 standard deviations above zero
(sd of the 20 sessions before it), bear below minus 0.5, else flat; active when the day's range
is above the median of the 20 before it. Data: daily bars of the 23 desk instruments (5 indices,
18 stocks; the brief said 17, the desk list has 18), about 2,400 stated sessions each, 55,556 in
all. Each cell is compared with the instrument's own base rate. The t treats all instruments'
sessions of one calendar day as one draw. Script: `scripts/mlstates_study.py`.

## 6. Does a lean pay? Next-session return, close to close, basis points
A lean is a direction with conviction weak or more; the lean is made before the session.
| group | bull lean | bear lean | no lean | follow the lean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| indices | +5.9, n 979, t 1.1 | +10.1, n 603, t 1.1 | +2.8, t 1.0 | −0.2, t −0.0 |
| stocks | +16.6, n 728, t 2.1 | +7.7, n 503, t 0.7 | +5.5, t 2.1 | +6.6, t 1.2 |
| all 23 | +10.4, n 1,707, t 2.0 | +9.0, n 1,106, t 1.1 | +5.0, t 1.9 | +2.8, t 0.7 |

Moderate or strong only (all 23): bull lean +14.7 bp (n 695, t 1.8), bear lean +3.1 bp (n 708,
t 0.5), following the lean +5.7 bp (n 1,403, t 1.2). A bear lean is followed by a rise as often
as a bull lean: markets rose on average in this period (+5.8 bp a session). A stock round trip
costs about 16 bp and an index option needs 5 to 10 bp: none of this clears either.

## 7. Verdict
- Direction states have almost no memory beyond chance. The effects are real but small and one
  sided: a flat session tends to follow a flat one (+1.8 points in stocks) and a bear session
  follows a bear one in indices (+5.3 points). They are swings in the share of flat days, not a
  call on up or down, and the walk-forward does not beat naming the most common direction.
- Size states do carry memory: after an active session the next is active 57% of the time
  against 49%, after a calm one 42%. This is the known clustering of volatility. It is
  worth about 7 points over the plain rule, and it says how big, not which way.
- The owner's "third bull is less likely" is true in sign (about 2 to 5 points less after three
  or more bulls than after one) but within chance (t about −1.4). The second bull is no less
  likely than the first.
- Tradable: no. No lean beats costs, and following the lean earns about +3 bp a session before
  costs (t 0.7). The page keeps its display because the owner asked for it, and it says so.
